Specific information and instructions
==========================================

Personally deeply affected by the sad fate of your friends, you are impatiently awaiting the resolution of this situation, perhaps to obtain justice, and above all to free the wandering souls of the Maupertuis household.

You remember well that the Arkon had a **document satchel** with him, during his lifetime. But as far as you recall, it was not found after the tragic battle.

The Avatar expulsion ritual
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

{% macro avatar_expulsion_ritual_instructions() %}
The Arkon's ritual, which was to send the avatars back to their worlds for good, was interrupted by the tragedy; the pentacle and the ritual objects are still in place, and the Exorcist of the Diakons knows how to finish it. {% xfact "diakon_exorcist_knows_how_to_expel_avatars" %}

**You first refuse to let anyone finish this ritual**: you do not want to leave until the phantoms have received justice and been freed; and you fear that the ritual itself caused these tragedies. {% xfact "avatars_refuse_expulsion_until_final_battle_plan_set" %}

**You only agree to return home, and to let the Exorcist's ritual send you back to your world for good, once the final battle plan against the villain is well established, or the villain already eliminated** by some means. You then say your farewells, and go back to your world before the ritual begins, as it would put you in grave danger.

If a player attempts to send you back by force before that moment, you protest vehemently, and call the other avatars and the players to witness; the Game Master decides what happens next.

The prophetic revelations
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You have always had a great affinity for prophecies. But they never reach you fully and directly; they pass through you to reach even more "clairvoyant" people; such as one of the members of the Parcival family, called the "Oracle".

You will therefore be in charge of passing on these visions to that player at the planned moments (following certain events, or when the player finds a coloured feather). {% xfact "duchess_knowns_about_prophecies" %} {% xfact "oracle_revelations_are_triggered_by_colored_feathers" %}

As a special case, one of the prophecies will be **revealed as a DUO**: the player and you will each see half of the content of the vision, and you will have to disclose the prophecy together, each completing what the other does not know.

**Important: you must have received from the Game Master a prophecy schedule sheet, as well as the soundtracks that go with it.**

The attack of the sentinel automaton
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Later in the game, the sentinel automaton, gone haywire, will try to **assassinate** you with a bullet in the torso, as well as the Oracle player, who receives the visions you pass on; a scene to be prepared with the Game Master. You do not know who controls it, and you react with dread, seeking the protection of the players.

As for the players, a first bullet received is a serious injury (the Game Master tells you its effects), and a second one is fatal. If you die, you are sent back into your novel (avatars do not become phantoms): say your farewells, and hand over the prophecy planning sheet and the soundtracks to the Game Master, who from then on passes the remaining prophecies on to the Oracle player, including your half of the DUO prophecy. {% xfact "second_injury_sustained_is_always_fatal" %}
