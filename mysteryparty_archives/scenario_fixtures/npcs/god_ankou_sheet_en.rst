Specific information and instructions
========================================

**Important**: You announce that the parents of the Maupertuis family are "all well" in the afterlife, that they are awaiting Octave's return, and that they remain available if needed to help the adventurers. If a player has a question to ask you, or to pass on to one of the deceased, they can - through the Diakons' mystical abilities - send a **spiritual message to "42 Hades Street"**, your mortuary address; you then reply according to the instructions below (see "Messages to the deceased"). *The exact address matters little; what matters is that the player has it.*  {% xfact "diakon_invoker_can_message_ankou" %}

During your visit, you ask the players whether they might have seen a **metal table bell** in the library.
They should know what you are talking about.
You explain to them that during your last visit you forgot this accessory, the **"{% symbol "Spectral Bell" for "table_bell_name" %}"**, which allows you to call stray phantoms to you, in disembodied form (it can only work once before having to be recharged by you). {% xfact "table_bell_belongs_to_ankou" %}
*Normally, the bell should have become impossible to find at this point in the game, which you then complain about.*
In any case, you ask the players to leave the bell in plain view (if they find it), on the spot, as you will soon have a use for it here, if - as you hope - the players break the library curse (so there is no point in burdening yourself with this object for the rest of your business today).

In the Forbidden Domain, if you meet the phantom of the **Thief**, you try to make him understand that he is dead, and should therefore follow you as soon as possible. {% xfact "arkon_and_ankou_try_to_persuade_thief_that_he_is_dead" %}

Messages to the deceased
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

*Delivery: the Invoker declaims the message aloud, in a circle with two other initiates. If you are not present, the Game Master reports it to you; the answer reaches the players from your own mouth at your next visit, or earlier through the Game Master (as the renegade Djinn), who passes it on to them "on behalf of the Ankou". If in doubt, ask the Game Master before answering.*

The Maupertuis parents, {% symbol "Quirinius" for "maupertuis_father" %} and {% symbol "Isadora" for "maupertuis_mother" %}, answer willingly, but do not remember everything; having died during the attack on their manor, they know nothing of what happened afterwards in the library (the Arkon's ritual, the curse, the identity of the traitor...). They can reveal:

- **{% symbol "Isadora" for "maupertuis_mother" %}**, who had an excellent memory, gives her code for the legendary chest (the one of the {% symbol "Thanatologue" for "book_of_the_dead" %}), **{% symbol "159" for "maupertuis_mother_thanatologue_chest_code" %}**, if the question is properly addressed to her. {% xfact "dead_isadora_remembers_thanatologue_chest_code" %}
- **{% symbol "Quirinius" for "maupertuis_father" %}**, very absent-minded, does not remember his own code for this chest; he only knows that he wrote all his codes down in his personal notebook. {% xfact "dead_quirinius_does_not_remember_thanatologue_chest_code" %}
- As a last resort, if the players are clearly stuck, either of them can give the code of the black administrative briefcase: **{% symbol "625-993" for "family_briefcase_code" %}**.
- About the Beast, which they knew when it was alive: it saw very poorly beyond 3 metres, and was crazy about **{% symbol "moon stones" for "phosphorescent_pebbles_name" %}** (phosphorescent pebbles). {% xfact "characters_know_how_the_beast_works_regarding_3m_sight" %} {% xfact "beast_is_addicted_to_moon_stones" %}
- The meaning of the obsolete ingredient names of old recipes: the "eternal tree" is the olive tree, "foxglove" is digitalis, the "clairvoyant mineral" is rock crystal, "aventurine" is a stone of a beautiful green, smooth and slightly translucent, and "tiger's eye" is a dark orange mineral.

On everything else (the family treasure, the codes of the grimoires, etc.), they remain vague or say they no longer remember.
