Behind the scenes
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

From your very first meeting with the survivors of the Maupertuis household, your opinion was clear: Mother Nature had called you into this world, which had generated yours, so that you might carry out the same prophetic task there, of destroying technology and returning to Natural Harmony. The discovery of the great **{% symbol "Planetary Cataclysm" for "catastrophic_period" %}** that had occurred a few centuries earlier reinforced your convictions: the planet had already brought human technologies to the ground once; it was up to you to finish the job.

But for that, you first had to understand how this universe worked, its science, its magic, and in particular those "bewitchments" which so often resisted your own druidic spells.

So you played along, and investigated in secret to unlock access to the library's Forbidden Domain, despite the prior warnings of Octave and the Archivist. Luckily for you, Octave had not found the third volume of your novel, and therefore knew nothing of your rather harsh conception of morality. But the Arkon {% symbol "Valerias" for "arkon_name" %} came and tore your plans to pieces.

While the Arkon was performing his ritual, and closing the interdimensional portals one by one, you came back into the world of the living through the third volume of your novel. And you attacked the humans shortly before it was your own portal's turn. Octave and the Archivist were vaporised by your fire elemental before they had even understood what was happening to them. The Arkon, for his part, proved to be a terrible adversary, chaining attacks and defences on the physical, magical and sacred planes - the latter being of course without effect on you. In the midst of this outpouring of power, you managed to shoot a curare arrow right into his forehead, which secured your victory.

Alas for you, with his dying breath, {% symbol "Valerias" for "arkon_name" %} cursed the library so that no spirit could leave it as long as you yourself were alive. His skeleton moreover proving indestructible, you hid his skull at the foot of the {% symbol "library plants" for "arkon_skull_location" %}, to prevent it from compromising you. When the other avatars still at liberty came, several days later, to enquire about the situation, they could not understand what had happened here; and you put on a show of surprise and dread. {% hint 'arkon_skull_hidden_in_jungle_trees' is needed %}

Since then, your investigations have been stalling; the search of the authorised floor has yielded nothing, the forbidden floor remains inaccessible, and you are impatiently waiting for human sorcerers to reopen the library one day, so as to resume your plans for world conquest.

The anti-Arkon conspiracy
===========================

As soon as players start trusting the Arkon, you tell yourself that the tide could turn against you. So you decide to frame him, by leaving a fake compromising letter lying around in his old document satchel (which you had pilfered right after the battle, *satchel provided by the organisers*), somewhere in the finally opened Forbidden Domain. As the signature of this fake letter, you imitated what could pass for a credible coat of arms for a fanatical sect among humans. {% fact "arkon_bag_disappeared_just_after_his_death" %} {% fact "druid_makes_conspiracy_against_arkon" %}

Of course, nobody must see you plant this satchel; not only will it suggest that the Arkon entered the Forbidden Domain during his lifetime, but also that he deliberately killed the Maupertuis refugees.
