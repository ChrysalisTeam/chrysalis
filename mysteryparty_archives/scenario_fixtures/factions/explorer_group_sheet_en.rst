Faction - The Heliossarian explorers
==================================================

{# Explorer - You are an archaeologist, expert in ancient runes, or in object analysis, or in anthropology #}

You are three explorer-archaeologists from the land of Heliossar.
One of you specialises in ancient runes and other secret codes; another in the analysis of hidden inscriptions, artefacts and bones; the last in anthropological knowledge (human societies) and heraldry (the art of coats of arms).

For decades, for centuries, your country has been repelling the attempts at conquest of its neighbour, the Akarith Theocracy. But the latest news is alarming: the Akariths are said to have found a copy of the **Yodic Book of the Dead, the legendary {% symbol "Thanatologue" for "book_of_the_dead" %}**; and through this grimoire, a supposedly *infallible* means of invading Heliossar.

Since then, all the armed forces of your homeland have been on a war footing. Forces **far better armed** than the Akarith legions, but alas ten times fewer in number, and with only a few *invokers* to pit against their countless *sorcerers* cultivating obscure powers. {% fact "akarith_army_is_much_more_numerous_and_mystic_than_heliossar_army" %}

For your part, as specialists in ancient artefacts, you have been commissioned to find, at all costs, another copy of this {% symbol "Thanatologue" for "book_of_the_dead" %}; in order to learn more about the Akariths' strategy of conquest, and above all to find a way to counter it.

Your research has put you on the trail of the Maupertuis, an old lineage of healer-mages from the region of {% symbol "Thelassar" for "events_region_name" %}, who are said to have owned a copy of the {% symbol "Thanatologue" for "book_of_the_dead" %}.
Their manor had been razed a very long time ago. Fortunately, by tracing back the family tree, you found monoliths in a neighbouring country, recounting the first generations of the Maupertuis family.
They mentioned, in enigmatic form, the creation of a library buried very deep underground, in order to preserve the most powerful magic grimoires of that time. A few photos of these inscriptions unfortunately leaked to the press, while you were labouring to decipher them.
