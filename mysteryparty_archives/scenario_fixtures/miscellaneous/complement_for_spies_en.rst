Your battle plan
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

You have only one thing in mind: find the "wooden mill" mentioned in the extract from the diary of Mérédice de Maupertuis.
Given its importance, it is probably in the Forbidden Domain of these Archives, which will not make your task any easier.

You will then have to find the magic code of this source of riches, through the "brooches" that the various members of the Maupertuis family shared out among themselves. This search promises to be arduous, because these brooches could be anywhere, in chests, in hiding places, under rubble... hoping they did not burn with the Maupertuis manor. Perhaps one of them is even still locked inside a dynastic bewitchment, if Octave did not end up obtaining it by passing his dynastic initiation.
