**Out-of-game: if the gold sticker star is still visible on the box that contained this paper, you have been cursed. Call the Game Master immediately.**

Memo card of the multiple NPC actor
=====================================

*For the actor who plays the Ankou, the Magus and the Thief in turn; it gives answers, so it must stay in the hands of this actor.*

**THE ANKOU**

- **Scold** the players for coming to shut themselves up in this cursed place.
- Ask whether they have seen a metal table bell in the library.
- Announce that a "spiritual message" can be sent to the Maupertuis parents, at 42 Hades Street.

**THE {% symbol "Magus Mos Peratys" for "maupertuis_dynasty_founder" %}**

- Attack the intruders of the tomb only if they are not Parcivals (with a blue jabot), or if they mistreat the mummy by searching it.

**THE THIEF**

- Formula of the world of the Djinns: "O prodigies of wisdom and curiosity, come and answer my thirst for learning"
- Code of Isadora de Maupertuis: {% symbol "159" for "maupertuis_mother_thanatologue_chest_code" %}
- Meredice's study: rubble in the corner of the Forbidden Domain, beyond the alchemy laboratory
- Formula of the astrolabe: "Astrolabe, carry me over there"

**RICHES**

- 1 silver- or bronze-coloured coin is worth 1 kash
- 1 gold coin is worth 10 kashes in the big cities
- 1 small round diamond is worth 5 kashes in Keroskia
- 1 medium rectangular diamond is worth 10 kashes in Nimouk
- 1 large round or rectangular diamond is worth up to 20 kashes in the Axolian lands
- The colour (smoky or not) of diamonds does not matter

The estimated value of precious jewels is written on a white label. When you negotiate, you systematically undervalue by 25% the riches that are brought to you.
