Your battle plan
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Your most crucial objective is simple: find the {% symbol "Thanatologue" for "book_of_the_dead" %} that the Maupertuis owned.

And find, potentially right here in these secret-laden premises, a way to counter their attack.
