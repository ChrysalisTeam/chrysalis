**Messages of the Anthropologist's Mystic Reminiscences:**

Read through the coloured glasses, the polychromatic (and anaglyph) codes give the following words. *These pictures are the same as in the French version of the game, so they spell French words, given here with their English meaning: when the Anthropologist deciphers one, tell them its meaning.*

- "Tracker Grimoire" message: GRIMOIRE TRACEUR = TRACKER GRIMOIRE (associated caption, in Fraktur gothic font: "serrure vortex abyssal" = "abyssal vortex lock")
- "Forbidden Domain" message: DOMAINE INTERDIT = FORBIDDEN DOMAIN
- "Singing Grimoire" message: GRIMOIRE CHANTANT = SINGING GRIMOIRE
- "Dynastic Initiation" message: INITIATION DYNASTIQUE = DYNASTIC INITIATION
- "Spiritual Orb" message: ORBE SPIRITUEL = SPIRITUAL ORB
- "Death to the impure" message, before the Magus's tomb: MORT AUX IMPURS = DEATH TO THE IMPURE
- "Thanatologue" message: THANATOLOGUE

**Other pictures with a hidden text:**

- Hologram pyramid (hieroglyphs): LEG ORACLE = "the Oracle's bequest" (French "legs" means "bequest")
- Small copper well (hieroglyphs): PUITS INUTILE = USELESS WELL
- Thief's memo (ogham): ASTROLABE TRANSPORTE MOI LA BAS = "Astrolabe, carry me over there" (accept the English formula)
- Anamorphic picture of the code {% symbol "723" for "maupertuis_father_thanatologue_chest_code" %}: caption "Pour le jour où je l'oublierai..." = "For the day I forget it..."
