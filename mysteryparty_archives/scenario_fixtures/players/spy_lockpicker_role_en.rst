Lockpicking
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Most of the library's locks are protected by bewitchments, and thus elude your purely mechanical art.

But if you find a **transparent padlock**, you can try to open it with your **lockpicking kit** (*provided by the organisers*). {% hint "lock_picking_kit_for_lockpicker" is needed %}

*Out-of-game note: With the lockpicking kit you will receive, the goal is to apply rotational pressure to the inner cylinder, with an L-shaped rod, then to push one by one the pins inside the lock, until they are all in their "sliding" position. Video example: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RqWgl0nK46E*


Magnetic Unlocker
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You have a **magnetic key** (*provided by the organisers*), which can open locks hidden inside drawers, small boxes, and other storage units. This magnetic key has sometimes also proved useful for deactivating strange anti-theft devices placed on books or other objects. {% hint "magnetic_unlocker_for_lockpicker" is needed %}

Finding the exact locations of these mechanisms is not always easy, but your colleague the Sounder could help you a little with this. {% fact "spy_sounder_can_help_spy_lockpicker_regarding_magnetic_unlocker" %}

*Out-of-game note*: the presence of magnetic systems may be denoted by small **white binder hole reinforcement rings**. The "magnet" logo you may see elsewhere does not concern you. Some systems require pushing the latch slightly towards the "closed" position, so that they have enough leeway to unlock and allow the box to be opened.


Potions found
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You found on the ground, before entering the teleportation portal, a satchel with a few **potions** (*provided by the organisers*), in fairly good condition.

You have kept these potions with you, even though no one in your team has much knowledge in this field.
