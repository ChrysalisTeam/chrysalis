Metal Detector
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Your metal detector (*provided by the organisers*) alerts you with vibrations and sounds when you bring it close to jewels, coins, and various materials sensitive to magnetism (iron, aluminium, gold, silver, copper, etc.). To use it, simply switch it on *away from any metal*, then pass its tip or its edge near the search area (furniture, chests, rockfalls, etc.).

If you switch it on near metal objects, its sensitivity will be reduced, which can be useful for detecting metal aggregates in the midst of other objects that would set off the detector (such as metal shelves).

Strangely, you have already seen this detector go off in front of *paintings*; a scholar told you that these were probably paintings containing precious-metal pigments, and serving as a medium for powerful "prophetic spells". Evidently, the ones you had come across had already been dispelled by a specialist, or by the passage of time; for when the scholar covered them with **"Lotion of Revelation"**, they barely reacted.

Magnetism Detector
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You have a small sheet that reacts to magnetic fields. Originally intended to understand the internal structure of certain unusual locks, it has proved even more useful for deciphering strange inscriptions of the Maupertuis lineage. Evidently, their runic magic has left a magnetic trace in **certain papers, paintings, or books**. Good news for you.

*Out-of-game note*: By placing or sliding the magnetic viewing film (provided by the organisers) over certain surfaces, you will be able to see magnetic field lines, revealing either inscriptions, or more subtly the presence of magnetic opening systems. If the inscriptions are a three-letter code, then you will need the help of a scientist-Unveiler to understand their meaning. Labels with a "magnet" symbol can help you find the relevant objects.
