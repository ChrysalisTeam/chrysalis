DRUID'S SPECTRE
######################

**Read the other NPC actors' sheets first, so as to have a good picture of what has recently happened around the library-museum called the "Cromlech".**

Your Story
=====================

You are {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %}. You were one of the druids who, 2 millennia ago, set in motion the gestation of a **divine warrior** capable of saving the Gaulish peoples from the Roman invasion.

Then you voluntarily became a ghost, in order to watch over this gestation. From generation to generation, you advise the dynasty charged with protecting the ancient artefacts that hold the divine entity.

But this gestation has been chaotic and irregular over the centuries; and today the artefacts are being endangered by the curiosity of that accursed **Andrea di Revel**.

Your actions during the investigation
=====================================

You helped the Curator act as best he could after his blunder with Andrea *(see his character sheet)*.

You went away to recharge in your spiritual "limbo", but on your return Andrea's body had vanished... as had the gladius. {% xfact "le glaive antique a disparu le jour j" %}

Since then, you have been searching for them everywhere, fearing they may have passed into the **in-between realm** where the entity is developing; but that realm is vast, and Andrea knows how to throw you off the scent.

In the real world, private detectives have arrived on the premises; but to your great astonishment, you will discover that they too can see you, and hear you (but in your native tongue; for examples of how it sounds, see the video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wc22W3bos64).

You can only make yourself understood (in the players' own language) by the **psykers**, those detectives who have additional mental powers; to do so you must step a little aside with them, to establish the mental link and be able to speak to them.

Your goal is to turn the players against Andrea and his allies, by lying skilfully:

- Initially, you can make them believe that these artefacts are very precious, that they are the last remnants of your civilisation, and that you want them to stay with the worthy Guesclin family. You claim that Andrea is after these artefacts for villainous motives.
- Later, you can admit that there is indeed an entity gestating, but that it is a benevolent deity; the soul of the Celtic people, which will allow Brittany to regain its former glory. In that case, you say you fear that Andrea wants to appropriate the powers of this deity.

Your interventions over the interviews
=========================================

The detectives question the witnesses in four simultaneous interviews (one group per witness, one group at the Treasure):

- **1st interview**: you walk behind the Guide, listen to what he says, perform an incantation while laying hands above his head, then leave without paying attention to the players' reactions. You come to whisper in the Curator's ear, in your unintelligible tongue; you take fright and leave if the players point out that they can see you. You may pass briefly through the other rooms, without stopping, cursing in your tongue; being "seen" by the players stresses you out.
- **2nd interview**: you go to see the player groups that are with the Guide and with the Curator; each time, you beckon the group's psyker over to you; you introduce yourself, then tell them (in the players' language) your first lie (see above): these artefacts are sacred relics of the Gaulish people, and Andrea must have stolen the gladius out of greed.
- **3rd interview**: no intervention planned; keep a low profile.
- **4th interview**: you go to see the player groups that are with the Reporter and at the Treasure; each time, you beckon the group's psyker over to you, and now tell them (in the players' language) that there is indeed an entity gestating in the artefacts, but that it is a benevolent deity, to be protected so that the druids' legacy is not lost forever.


Your role in the ending
================================

After the 4th and final interrogation rotation, you gather the psykers in a corner, and announce to them out-of-game that they are now under your control; they must therefore behave like zombies, and follow your orders during the final scene. You can draw lightning-bolt tattoos on their faces and arms to make this curse visible. {% xfact "le druide envoûte les psykers à la fin du jeu" as author %}

When the players are called back to the Treasure for the final scene, you discreetly send the zombie psykers back to their groups. The final soundtrack is started; about 30 seconds after it starts, the Reporter, followed by the Guide, calls on the players to join him on the left of the cromlech. About a dozen seconds later, when the cromlech's Tesla coil takes up the "Dies Irae" again, it is your turn: you harangue all the players (in the Celtic tongue and then in broken English, with sweeping gestures) to join you on the **right** of the cromlech, and to make the **"Druids' Sign"** with you, facing the artefacts: forming a menhir shape in front of themselves, with their arms and hands. The Curator follows you, and the zombie psykers obey your orders.

**Slogan to repeat with your camp until the end of the music: "By the Might of {% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}".**

This will be a way for them to give their energy to the divine entity, so that it can slay the Hero who has come to fight it with the ancient gladius.
