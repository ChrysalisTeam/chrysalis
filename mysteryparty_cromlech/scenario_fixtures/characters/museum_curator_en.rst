MUSEUM CURATOR
########################

Your Past
==============

You are **Mr/Mrs {% symbol "Camille du Guesclin" for "curator_name" %}**, a direct descendant of the noble Breton knight of the same name. You had embarked on a fine **career as a lawyer**, when the death of your **father {% symbol "Philippe du Guesclin" for "curator_father_name" %}** - who had gone mad with age - turned everything upside down, **{% symbol "15 months" for "curator_father_death_delay" %}** ago.

His superb manor, too costly to maintain, had to be sold; so you took an inventory of the heap of bizarre objects it contained.

That is when, in the depths of its cellars, you came across artefacts from every era, including some exceptional pieces dating back to Celtic antiquity. For a while you believed this would earn you a tidy fortune from museums or wealthy collectors. But your sleep soon filled with nightmares more vivid than reality. Then HE came knocking at your door.

Him, the strange character who knew everything about your dreams and your plans, your days and your nights. At first, he passed himself off as a distant uncle. Then he revealed his true nature: **{% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %}**, a druid turned messenger of the Celtic gods. A spectre that only you, as far as you know, can see and hear.

And {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %} delivered terrible news to you: your dynasty was charged with protecting and maintaining ancient relics, but for centuries it had failed in its task. Your own father had always refused to fulfil this duty... until he sank into madness.

There was still time to avert a cataclysm, by putting these antiquities back at the heart of society, so that they could feed on the vital essence and psychic energy of humans. To hear him tell it, the whole universal order was at stake, through the immemorial magic of the druids. {% xfact "les artefacts doivent rester au contact des humains" %}

Trapped by this unexpected responsibility, you suspended your career to follow the strange instructions of {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %}: the Gaulish and Roman artefacts had to remain together at all times, out in the open, in a place frequented by many people... touched by them if possible... without, however, attracting the attention of archaeologists.

A complicated equation, which you only solved by setting up a small library-museum unlike any other: the **{% symbol "Cromlech" for "museum_name" %}**. A place of study well-known enough to draw a crowd and be economically viable, yet discreet enough not to attract attention. You passed the ancient artefacts off as "faithful replicas", so as to justify their academic interest while allowing passers-by to handle them.

The success of this project is largely due to the guide-librarian you hired **{% symbol "1 year" for "guide_arrival_at_cromlech_delay" %} ago, {% symbol "Eden Schmitt" for "guide_name" %}**. He has built up a loyal clientele with his joviality, his helpfulness, and his colourful tales about the museum's artefacts.

He persists in calling you "Curator" in front of people, but you make it quite clear, if questioned on the matter, that you are merely the "Manager", with no History-related qualification whatsoever – no need to get into legal trouble with that highly elitist profession.
That does not stop you from condescendingly dismissing the History students who presume to "teach you your job" - even though, deep down, they are right.

Business was beginning to settle down magnificently. Each month you performed **ritual ablutions** on the artefacts, and recited a few druidic incantations, in order to "dress the wounds" (as {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %} put it) of the past; in return, this spectre hardly ever appeared to you any more. You were taking steps to resume your legal career where you had left off, when a new thorn lodged itself in your side, **{% symbol "three months" for "hero_arrival_delay" %}** ago. {% xfact "le conservateur souhaiterait reprendre sa carrière d'avocat" as author %}

**{% symbol "Andrea di Revel" for "hero_name" %}, son of the Italian ambassador to France**. A History student who would not be fobbed off by your nonsense, and started frequenting the library regularly; hovering around the artefacts, especially the **Celtic Grimoire**, which you forbade anyone to read. The spectre urged you to be wary of him; and grew even more alarmed when this student "vanished from his field of perception", a few weeks ago; but what could be done without drawing further attention? So you kept a low profile.
{% xfact "le héros est devenu indétectable par le spectre il ya quelques semaines" %}

As you spied on Andrea and his historical-esoteric research, old fears gripped you again. There was talk of an ancestral **vengeance of the vanquished Armorican peoples** against the Roman invader; of **{% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}**, the bloodthirsty Celtic god; of a titanic warrior gestating in a mystic egg; and of horrible consequences if the accompanying rites were not observed...

What abominable prophecy, then, was in the process of coming true? Your great hope, in truth, was that this process, initiated 2 millennia ago, would only come to an end after your own generation. To each their own problems, after all.

Note: some key clues relating to this curse are in old nested chests in the Treasure Room; over time, these objects have also become bound to the magic ritual, so you could not remove them; but you have hidden their codes in your office. {% xfact "le conservateur connait les codes des coffrets de la salle au trésor" as author %}


The Day of the Tragedy
======================

Today, you came in early in the morning, at around **{% symbol "7:00" for "curator_arrival_at_cromlech_on_d_day" %}**, to have time to perform the sacred rituals well before your employee arrived. You made sure to **lock** the door behind you after entering.

But just as you were beginning the ceremony, clad in the traditional **linen toga**, Andrea burst into the room, in a fury. He accused you of endangering the lives of millions of innocents with "rituals of demonic essence".

His intrusion, his nerve, his verbal aggression made you lose your composure. You started fighting. The intruder seized the ancient gladius to push you back, and **wounded you on the left hand**. As if possessed, you then rushed into your adjoining office to grab your father's old **revolver**, and shot Andrea right in the belly. {% xfact "le conservateur a été blessé à la main gauche par le héros" %} {% xfact "le conservateur a tiré au pistolet sur le héros" as author %}

When you came to your senses, the young man lay in front of the artefacts, in a pool of blood. {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %} appeared to you, and dictated the course to follow:

- Bandage your bloodied left wrist, and hide it under a black glove; {% xitem "bandage sanglant au poignet pour le conservateur" is needed %}
- Clean the gladius and put it back in its place;
- Place the revolver in Andrea's left hand (for Andrea was left-handed!);
- Write a fake farewell letter in his name (with the left hand again - ouch);
- Place this letter in plain view near his body;
- Go and spend the rest of the morning at a public event, in full view of everyone.

[BR]

You followed these instructions to the letter, and went off to mingle with the notables at a literary prize-giving ceremony, the **Maurice Genevoix prize**, on the other side of town; you arrived discreetly at around **{% symbol "10:00" for "curator_arrival_at_ceremony_on_d_day" %}, more than an hour after it had begun**, but you concealed this detail during the cocktail reception that followed. Despite your brain being in utter turmoil, you more or less managed to keep up appearances **with the Burgomaster, the grand master of the conservatoire, the playwright Jeanne Lubeck**... in theory.

When you returned to the library, **shortly before noon**, no gendarme was in sight. The Guide and the regulars were going about their business. No body in the artefacts room.
Had it not been for the dark bloodstain on the floor, hidden under a chair that had been moved, you would have sworn you had dreamt it all.

Had {% symbol "Olloudios" for "spectre_name" %} changed his plan?
Did he, in fact, have the power to alter reality?
Was it he who had made the gladius vanish from its scabbard - as you noted with surprise? {% xfact "le glaive antique a disparu le jour j" %}

But just as hope was returning to you, gendarmes arrived, with a flock of private investigators.
Clearly, someone had raised the alarm without your knowledge.
Really, this day just would not stop getting off to a bad start...

**The police must absolutely not suspect that you were on the premises early this morning, let alone performing a druidic ritual... they would surely pin Andrea's disappearance on you!**

What you know about the Guide
===================================

**{% symbol "Eden Schmitt" for "guide_name" %}** is a young person who left school after the baccalauréat, and tried many odd jobs without ever sticking with any of them. He cobbled together a fake CV to land an interview with you; you saw through him immediately, as the course he described never existed at the University cited. But he had a fair amount of general knowledge, imagination, and a great sense of service; that was what you needed for the job. {% xfact "Le guide avait menti dans son CV pour être embauché" %}

His museum tours were initially embroidered on the fly, as he clearly had little knowledge of the subject; but he quickly improved, teaching himself ancient History.
He is charming with the public, cooks well (his pastries are a great boost to your morale), and shows just enough rigour in shelving the books.

You wonder whether he has not been taken in by Andrea, with whom he often chatted during his visits. Lately, he has been wearing an ancient-looking **signet ring** on his finger; a "gift from an admirer", was his only explanation. {% xfact "le guide a désormais une chevalière à la main" %} {% xitem "chevalière pour le guide" is needed %}

In light of recent events, you tell yourself that you may have to make him a scapegoat, if things get even more complicated. He was, after all, the only one besides you to have the keys to the library... in fact, might he really have something to do with this intrusion by the **young Italian**?
