Discover the Cromlech of Lyon
=====================================

The library-museum known as the "Cromlech" is a little-known place, yet one of interest in more ways than one.

On the death of the venerable {% symbol "Philippe du Guesclin" for "curator_father_name" %}, his only son, {% symbol "Camille du Guesclin" for "curator_name" %}, hastened to clear out the cellar of the family château.

There he gathered enough old books and antiquities from various eras to create a sort of large "cabinet of curiosities", to which he devoted himself body and soul - forsaking, in order to do so, a promising career as a lawyer.

At first a small museum, soon converted into a library and place of study to stave off bankruptcy, this unusual venue has become a haven of peace for anyone wishing to work in an atmosphere steeped in references to the past.

Public reviews
====================

**Rating: 5/5**
    An attentive guide-librarian (with delicious cookies to boot), a studious atmosphere, and inspiring artefacts. This place is a treat for the palate of lovers of History and Literature.

**Rating: 3/5**
    After only a week working there with classmates, all three of us had a string of strange nightmares, both mythological and surprisingly realistic. The place gives off bad vibes, or just lacks light... a pity, as it has charm.

**Rating: 1/5**
    Between two visits two months apart, our guide had completely changed his story about the origins and uses of certain antiquities displayed here. And I learned that they were mere replicas, when they looked utterly genuine. Such incompetence, if not charlatanism; thoroughly unprofessional, stay away!
