If anyone reads these lines, it means the grimoire has managed to cross the veil between our two worlds.

I am alive. Wounded, but alive: a potion saved me, and I went through the door of the cromlech, with the gladius of the legions.

I have found the egg. What is growing inside it is not the soul of the Gaulish people: it is the warrior of {% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}, who feeds only on blood and wrath, and whose awakening already makes the volcanoes of Italy rumble. The spectre who haunts the Cromlech will lie to you: he has been lying for two thousand years.

The Beast will soon hatch, and alone, I shall not be able to slay it. When the cromlech starts to sing, gather around Ryan, make the Centurions' Sign, and let your will give me the strength to strike.

*A. di R.*
