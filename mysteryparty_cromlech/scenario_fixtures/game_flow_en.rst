Game Flow - GM SHEET
#######################################

*Timetable (about 2 hours, details in the GM manual): out-of-game briefing 10 min; welcome 5 min; 4 interviews of 12 min, separated by 3 investigation updates of 5 min; 10 min interlude after the 2nd update; final huddles 10 min; final scene 5 min; debriefing 10 min. The NPC actors arrive 45 min before the players.*


NPC ACTORS' Briefing
----------------------------

**IMPORTANT:**

- Explain the appearance of ANDREA, the Hero (see the GM manual), so that they all describe him in the same way
- Explain the rotation order of the player groups
- Remind each of them of their attitude over the interviews, and of their role in the final scene: side, sign, slogan and cue (see their sheets)
- Explain the end-of-interview GONG {% xitem "gong" is needed %}
- Explain where to take them between each session


Out-of-game PLAYERS' Briefing
-------------------------------------

**Setting up the player groups, by affinity.**

**Reminder and assignment of roles: clerk/psyker/intimidator. Insist on the exact power of the intimidator!**

The Game Master states the key points:

- In-game, we will be on **a day in December 1900, at 12:30, in a small building in Vieux Lyon.**
- Show the forced OUT-OF-GAME signal with the **raised fist**
- Tell them not to touch the **GM/NPC SHEETS** out-of-game!!
- **Safety rules**: no physical violence, we stop a scene if it becomes unbearable, and we take care of the props (hands off the CROMLECH).

Welcome by the Police Inspector
---------------------------------------

- "The office of our President **Emile Loubet** has sent word of your coming."
- "I am honoured to welcome your agency of **paranormal detectives**, whose merits need no further proof."
- "This case is a murky one; for now we have heard of the **disappearance of a student, Andrea, and of a museum piece, an ancient gladius**, but we know no more."
- "We will therefore let you **interrogate** the witnesses in this case in turn, and inspect the museum's treasure."
- "Note that we already have one strange phenomenon: the **lighting has been malfunctioning** since this morning around the treasure."
- "I remain at your disposal, between each interrogation, should you need to have **facts checked** by my police forces."
- "We will also provide you with **police information**, as we go along, on our own investigations."
- "Here is a **newspaper article** about that strange institution, the Cromlech of Lyon."

**ROTATION ORDER (cyclic): Guide, then Reporter, then Curator, then Museum Treasure, then Guide again... Each group starts at a different station, and visits each station once.**

At the Treasure, do not reset anything between two groups: whatever a group has opened (chest, cryptex) stays open for the following ones, and if a group fails to open it, the next one picks up where it left off. The groups that have already visited the Treasure learn what came next from the clerks, during the final huddles.


Initial Investigation Report
-----------------------------

- Identity sheets of the witnesses in the case.
- First findings made on the premises: available access routes, bloodstain, disappearance of the ancient gladius. {% xitem "tache de sang séché au sol, sous un fauteuil déplacé" is needed %} {% xitem "fourreau vide du glaive antique" is needed %}


First Simultaneous Interview
----------------------------------

Guide: relatively relaxed and jovial, as he imagines it is merely the theft of a replica gladius; talks about his passions and his recent discoveries regarding Gallo-Roman antiquity (but without details). He momentarily loses his train of thought if the Druid "casts a spell" on him.

Reporter: recounts his meeting with Andrea, and the troubling clues of the morning (his friend's absence at the appointment, ancient gladius missing, reddish mark on the floor...).

Curator: claims to have spent the morning at a public ceremony and to understand nothing of all this fuss over a simple act of vandalism. Is visibly irritated, embarrassed, if the Druid's ghost distracts him by speaking to him.

Treasure: discovery of the museum's artefacts, of the paper with the symbols of Gaulish mythology (laid near the model), and of the {% symbol "healing potion" for "healing_potion" %} on the floor (used by Andrea).

Druid's intervention:

- He walks behind the Guide, listens to what he says, performs an incantation while laying hands above his head, then leaves without paying attention to the players' reactions.
- He comes to whisper in the Curator's ear, in an unintelligible tongue; he takes fright and leaves if the players point out that they can see him.
- He may pass briefly through the other rooms, without stopping, cursing in his unintelligible tongue. Being "seen" by the players stresses him out.


First Investigation Update
----------------------------------

- Information about a dubious key duplication carried out in town, a key identical to the Curator's and the Guide's.
- Information about the illicit analysis of an ancient gladius by a person resembling the Reporter (without proof).
- Information about the chaotic course of the public ceremony where the Curator claims to have spent the morning.
- Clue to the code of the chest in the Treasure Room.
- Analysis of the blood (blood type, age...) from the Treasure Room, showing that it belongs to Andrea.


Second Simultaneous Interview
---------------------------------------

Guide: shocked by the news of the spilled blood, somewhat distraught; candidly admits, without shame, that he duplicated the key to help Andrea succeed in his studies.

Reporter: put on the spot over the illicit analysis of the gladius, but not thrown off balance; admits he helped Andrea carry out this operation, but insists that this is not the priority right now.

Curator: put on the spot by the revelations about his timetable, thrown off balance; if cornered he can fall back on the theory that "Andrea committed suicide", which he supposedly witnessed by chance.

Treasure: probable opening of the chest's padlock, and examination of its contents (curse tablet and cryptex).

Police Inspector's interventions (one third of the way through the interview with the Curator, then two thirds of the way through it with the Guide, since they are in two different rooms):

- Points out the wound on the Curator's left hand. {% xitem "bandage sanglant à la main gauche pour le conservateur" is needed %}
- Accuses the Guide of having forged diplomas.

Druid's intervention: He goes to see the player groups currently with the Guide and the Curator; each time, he beckons the group's Psyker over to him; he introduces himself, then tells them (in the players' language) a tall tale, to the effect that these artefacts are sacred relics of the Gaulish people, and that Andrea must have stolen the gladius out of greed.


Second Investigation Update
----------------------------------

- Analysis report obtained by Andrea and the Reporter, certifying that the gladius was a genuine antiquity.
- Discovery of the Curator's liturgical paraphernalia, and of a clue to the cryptex code.
- Discovery of the (fake) farewell letter mistakenly put in the Lost and Found by the Guide. {% xitem "enveloppe non timbrée avec message codé, contenant la fausse lettre d'adieux" is needed %}


INTERLUDE: Professor Gustave Bloch's History Lesson
----------------------------------------------------------

**TAKE PHOTOS**

The players are gathered in the largest room.

The professor (played by the archaeology expert of the Treasure, or failing that by the GM, see the GM manual) arrives, eccentric and agitated, and recaps the history of the conquest of Gaul and the battle of Uxellodunum, then sceptically touches on a few legends surrounding it. At the end of his lesson, he gives each group a copy of the paper with the symbols of Gaulish mythology, as well as of the document "The Siege of Uxellodunum", which sums up his lesson.

Outline of the lesson (about 10 minutes):

- "52 BC: at Alesia, Vercingetorix surrenders to Julius Caesar. Gaul is conquered... well, almost!"
- "One year later, the last rebels, led by Lucterius and Drappes, entrench themselves in the oppidum of Uxellodunum, in the heart of Quercy."
- "Caesar comes in person. Rather than storm the place, he has tunnels dug and dries up the spring: parched, the besieged surrender. Caesar has their hands cut off, as an example."
- "Legend has it that druids from Armorica had joined the rebels; when the stronghold fell, they supposedly hid in a cave to invoke {% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}, with Roman weapons as receptacles... Balderdash, of course!"
- "{% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}, 'father of the nation', a god of war appeased only by the blood of battles; nothing to do with Belenos, Epona or Ogmios, far more peaceful deities. Here, these are their symbols."
- "A descendant of Bertrand du Guesclin even boasted, in his memoirs, of having had custody of this 'Druids' Last Wish'... But legends, my friends, are only legends!"


Third Simultaneous Interview
----------------------------------

Guide: talks about the strange measures imposed on him by the Curator regarding the handling of the artefacts.

Reporter: does not believe for a moment that Andrea's farewell letter is genuine.

Curator: if cornered, he confesses everything, but protests that the stakes are far greater than "him versus Andrea", and that certain truths must remain hidden.

Treasure: probable opening of the cryptex, and study of its contents.

Police Inspector's interventions (one third of the way through the interview with the Curator, then two thirds of the way through it with the Guide, since they are in two different rooms):

- Comes to challenge the Curator on the fact that the letter was written with a trembling hand, hence perhaps a "wounded" one.
- Points out the mysterious signet ring worn by the Guide (a gift from Andrea).


Third and Final Investigation Update
------------------------------------------------

- Discovery of a revolver (with one bullet missing), bearing fingerprints, in the Treasure Room. {% xitem "revolver où il manque une balle" is needed %}
- Vibrations set off on the museum's fake grimoire.
- Feedback from the Cromlech regulars about their nightmares.
- Andrea's call to his allies, via a message scrawled on the envelope found on the floor.

**LOGISTICS: slip the extra parchment (document "andrea_message_in_grimoire") under the cover of the Grimoire, and start the vortex video in the door of the miniature cromlech** {% xitem "message d'Andrea apparu dans le grimoire" is needed %} {% xitem "smartphone avec la vidéo de vortex" is needed %}


Fourth Simultaneous Interview
--------------------------------------

All the witnesses start to **ramble** halfway through the interview! (The awakening of the Beast troubles their minds: they lose their train of thought, mix nightmarish visions into their answers, then gradually pull themselves together.)

Guide: tries to contribute his historical and mythological knowledge to help the players.

Reporter: raises the alarm: "Andrea is in danger in another world, we must help him".

Curator: raises the alarm: "the artefacts must be left alone, and Andrea must be stopped from hijacking them for his own benefit!".

Treasure: discovery of Andrea's message that has appeared under the cover of the grimoire, and study of the artefacts and messages discovered.

Druid's intervention: He goes to see the player groups currently with the Reporter and the Treasure; each time, he beckons the group's Psyker over to him, and now says (in the players' language) that there is indeed an entity gestating in the artefacts, but that it is a benevolent deity, to be protected so that the druids' legacy is not lost forever.

Final Huddles
-------------------------------------

Everyone gathers **OUTSIDE the Treasure Room**.

- All the "clerks" are gathered by the Police Inspector to share the results of their investigations.
- All the "psykers" are gathered and briefed by the Druid: they are now like zombies, subjected to his druidic will, and will be his accomplices in the final scene (with **quick make-up** simulating demonic possession). From now on they must walk slowly, occasionally stammer unintelligible things, and follow the Druid's orders. {% xfact "le druide envoûte les psykers à la fin du jeu" %}
- The rest of the players stay and talk among themselves.

**LOGISTICS: Remove the smartphone from the door of the miniature cromlech (safety) and fit the needle of the Tesla coil.** {% xitem "bobine Tesla et son aiguille" is needed %}


Final Scene
---------------------------------

- The players are called back to the **Treasure**.
- Meanwhile the Druid nudges the **zombie psykers** to return, rather discreetly, to their groups.
- The final soundtrack is started, the cromlech begins to **crackle** from time to time. {% xitem "bande son finale et enceinte" is needed %}
- 30 s after the start, the cromlech's Tesla coil also starts playing the "Dies Irae"; the **Reporter**, followed by the **Guide**, then calls on the players to join him on the **left** of the cromlech, and to make the "Centurions' Sign" with him to help Andrea (arms crossed over the chest). **Slogan to repeat: "By the Glory of Jupiter".**
- 12 s later, when the Tesla coil takes up the "Dies Irae" again, it is the turn of the **Druid** (now in broken English), followed by the **Curator**, to urge the players to join him on the **right** of the cromlech, and to make the "Druids' Sign" with him (forming a menhir shape with the arms and hands) to save the Druids' Last Wish. The zombie psykers follow his orders. **Slogan to repeat: "By the Might of Toutatis".**
- The cromlech calms down but the music goes on; both camps must keep up their slogans.
- The Inspector urges the undecided players to choose their side, because **"remaining impassive in such situations of danger is a crime"**.
- At the end of the music, the GM counts the players in each camp (see below), then discreetly signals the outcome to Andrea's actor, hidden near the Treasure Room (thumb up: victory; thumb down: defeat).
- Andrea appears; he explains the **ending (his victory or his defeat)** and what it means for the World (see below). {% xitem "costume d'Andrea pour son apparition finale" is needed %}
- Announce the END OF GAME!

**Counting the camps:** only the players count, not the NPC actors. The Hero's camp gathers the players standing on the left of the cromlech, making the Centurions' Sign; the Druid's camp, those standing on the right, making the Druids' Sign, zombie psykers included. The undecided who stayed aside do not count. If the Hero's camp is strictly the larger one, Andrea comes back victorious; otherwise (a majority on the right, or a tie), he has failed.

**If Andrea is victorious** (he enters, staggering but beaming, the Roman gladius in hand; the Druid lets out a long scream and flees, the psykers come out of their trance): {% xitem "glaive romain pour l'apparition finale d'Andrea" is needed %}

    "My friends... it is over. Thanks to you, thanks to the Centurions' Sign, I held fast. What was growing behind that door was not the soul of the Gauls: it was the warrior of {% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}, a bloodthirsty beast, fed for two thousand years by those accursed rituals and by your dreams, and whose wrath was already making Italy tremble. I slew it with this gladius, the very one a legionary carried at Uxellodunum. The portal is closed: Rome will tremble no more. *(Turning to the Curator.)* As for you, du Guesclin... I believe the Inspector has a few questions for you."

**If Andrea is defeated** (he staggers in, empty-handed, with a haggard look):

    "I... I could not. It was too strong: every one of your signs, there, on the right, fed it. The Beast is born. It was no benevolent deity: it is the warrior of {% symbol "Toutatis" for "bloodthirsty_god_name" %}, and it wants nothing but blood. It tore the gladius from me, and it is already marching on Rome, to avenge Uxellodunum, as the druids wished. Can you hear it? The earth is shaking over there, the volcanoes of Italy are awakening... *(He collapses.)* Warn my mother... have Rome evacuated..."

    The Druid, triumphant, in broken English: "The Druids' Last Wish... is granted!"
