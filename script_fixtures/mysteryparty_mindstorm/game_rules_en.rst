Learning and following the scenario
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

- **Read your long character sheets carefully**, along with any other useful document; if printed, these papers will be considered out-of-game on site; feel free to highlight the passages that seem important to you
- We have put together **summary sheets** of what you know about the various people (and how you know it), of what you have done in the past, of your missions, your abilities, and your current financial and material possessions; **print these sheets**, and feel free to enrich them with any information you deem worthy of interest
- Bring something to take notes with during the game (a pen, or even a notebook): your memory will not be enough
- For simplicity's sake, the character sheets are written in the masculine by default, but most roles are ungendered; talk to the Game Master if you have preferences in this respect


Telling IC and OOC objects apart
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

- Unless very explicitly stated otherwise, **the food and drinks of the buffet are out-of-game**, so you risk nothing by cheerfully enjoying them
- The GM may have decided to mark the "non-trivial" objects and documents that are part of the game, by whatever means they choose; check with them at the start of the game
- Some elements (GM documents, GM cash reserves, character sheets) are automatically out-of-game and confidential; **in particular, do not show your character sheet to the other players, even allies**
- **Cameras are out-of-game**, they cannot be used to obtain incriminating evidence against you; the same goes for the **surveillance cameras**, which, in the scenario, are assumed to be deactivated throughout the manor during the evening
- when **pieces of furniture are covered with a cloth, or sealed with a piece of tape**, this means they are explicitly OOC and thus contain nothing of interest for the game
- if a participant walks across the room with a **raised fist**, they are out-of-game: ignore them
- If in doubt about what is in-game or not, ask the GM or the NPCs


Communications and Abilities
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

- By default, **any given contact is assumed to be public** ("in the directory"), and can be reached by any available means (computer message, handwritten letter, telephone, other...); luckily, you also have a mini world directory in your pocket
- It is assumed that you are **automatically authenticated**, in a highly secure way, during your exchanges with an external third party, whatever the chosen communication channel (thanks to an implicit handwritten, vocal or digital signature...)
- It is possible to order **banking operations** (money transfers, object transfers...) by any means of communication, without going through the Anthropia web portal
- Between the fact that your superiors have **no reserves left available** in Sabarim, and the new financial regulations, do not expect to obtain new bank credit (neither in kashes nor in Sabarith Pounds) for 2 weeks
- As for written documents circulating inside the manor, they are "certified true" if and only if they bear a "tamper-proof seal", which will be simulated by **a signature within a circle**; do this to certify a document you have written; if a document does not bear this seal, "nobody knows", and of course, imitating someone else's seal is forbidden
- The Game Master must be **kept informed** of the special abilities you put into action, and must attend them when they are interactive abilities between protagonists; since some faculties are new and a little daring, the Game Master reserves the right to shoot down any initiative that might ruin the evening
- Feel free to take the Game Master aside during the game, if a rule is unclear, or to ask whether X or Y is possible in this universe
- **The game props are sometimes fragile, do not force them, especially if they are fitted with a locking system. They then require either special abilities to open them, or finding, depending on the case:**

  - **Their metal key (if it is a keyed lock or padlock)**
  - **Their secret code (if it is a set of numbered dials)**
  - **Their magnetic card (if it is an opening marked with a magnetic-card logo)**


Gameplay Advice
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Rely on your **supporters on site**:

- If you have **allies in the game**, take stock together at the start of the session, to exchange information on what you know about the previous days and on your respective abilities, and to see how you can help each other in your missions; in the absence of conclusive evidence, it is better to play as a tight-knit team than to sink into "everyone here is potentially my worst enemy" paranoia.
- you probably have **supporters outside** the evening (superiors, mentors, family and friends...), with whom you will occasionally have the opportunity to take stock and think about solutions.
- If you lack information, abilities or objects to progress in your missions, think about discreetly having a word with the **Oberon mediators**, that is what they are there for; likewise, feel free to entrust them with the simple interactions to be carried out on the **Anthropia web portal** on your behalf, so as to avoid cutting yourself off from the game at interesting moments. Assume that their loyalty, and their discretion, are absolute.
- The **Game Master** (who plays one of the Oberons) is at your disposal for any "out-of-game" question.


Manage your **opponents**:

- If you have **compromising information** about someone, it may be worth letting them know, so that they do not try to harm you, or to obtain something in exchange for your silence; generally speaking, bargain with any object or information you possess
- **Inspector Shark**, who is prowling around, is ageing, but as formidable as ever. If he lays his hands on compromising evidence concerning you, you can be sure he will crush you like a cockroach, and drag down with you the people or entities involved with you; except perhaps if you offer him bigger game to sink his teeth into.
