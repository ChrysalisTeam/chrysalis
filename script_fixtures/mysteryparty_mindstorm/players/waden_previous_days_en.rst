


The secret of the atoms
====================================


{% include "missions/secrets_of_atom/generic_mission_briefing_en.rst" %}


This Sun of Atoms, you know it from up close.
And not only because of radioactive dating.
You were indeed commissioned, {% symbol "8 months" for "waden_voluto_cooperation_start_ago" %} ago, to assist Professor Voluto in his research in atomic physics.

The venerable scientist did not have an easy character, especially after being sidelined in this sulphurous field of study. This collaboration was nonetheless fruitful; very fruitful; too fruitful. By delving deeper into the fundamental equations, you stumbled upon a formidable, deadly, apocalyptic energy: the **atomic supernova**.

That moment was bitterly ironic: you who had only been able to make your ascent thanks to the grimoires inherited from your forebears, you who had always secretly hoped for Science to be opened up to the greatest number, there you were holding in your hands a discovery far too dangerous to be disseminated, or even studied.

Prof. Voluto, however, had his own opinion on the matter. He officially closed the file on this work, and set about working on "improving the precision of radiating body scanners".
But unofficially, after a few months, he rented large basement premises in an unremarkable hamlet, and discreetly moved a good part of his equipment there, along with all of your working documents on the subject. Then he tasked you with preparing the first "atomic overcompression" experiment, to confront theory with facts. {% fact "waden_secretly_worked_with_voluto_on_radioactivity" as author %}

**The address of Prof. Voluto's secret laboratory: {% symbol "145 Puy de la Fol Road, Hamlet of Le Gisant, South Alifir" for "voluto_secret_lab_address" %}.**

All this was on the edge of legality, but between the excitement of discovery and the fear of your tutor, it was hard to back out. You therefore spent a good part of your evenings in this makeshift laboratory, unbeknownst to the academy. You spent a long time refining a material called **{% symbol "Uranium-234" for "most_dangerous_radioactive_material" %}**, the only known element that would lend itself well to this overcompression technique.

Everything changed the day you understood, thanks to a paper that fell out of his binder, that Prof. Voluto was in the process of contacting several Midolian countries, to sell them his discoveries at a premium.

You confronted your mentor in his office at the Academy, {% symbol "2 months" for "waden_voluto_cooperation_end_ago" %} ago; a heated exchange, which went as far as a scuffle. His back struck the wall violently. You left, terrified as much by the ruin awaiting your career as by the savagery you had felt in your blood.

The downfall never came. The professor went home without saying anything to anyone. And the next day, he was found dead, "{% symbol "at home, of a cerebral haemorrhage following a fall" for "physicist_death_circumstances" %}" according to the newspapers. You will probably never know your exact share of responsibility in his premature death, and that is just as well. But that does not solve everything.

Prof. Voluto's secret laboratory was very well protected, and he was the only one who knew its access code.
All the documents, as well as the atomic compressor prototype, are still there.
You do not have the makings of a burglar. But the sad scientist has been dead for too long. The owner of the premises will eventually start asking questions. The Midolian countries that were approached may themselves be on the trail. Do they already know to what extent {% symbol "Uranium-234" for "most_dangerous_radioactive_material" %} could become the new strategic Grail?

You must find a way to destroy this knowledge. Perhaps hire mercenaries? Dangle before them **one of the precious pieces of equipment (Thermocyclic Fusion Core, Hydraulic Press, or Gigapulse Laser)** that are there? And make sure they destroy the rest?




Loyd Georges' find
==============================

You had not really followed the recent expedition of this "Sir Loyd Georges" to Akaris. But to your great surprise, shortly after his return, {% symbol "6 weeks" for "lg_expedition_end_ago" %} ago, he contacted you through Prof. Loakim. He needed you for an urgent mission, potentially dangerous, but one that could revolutionise today's Science. {% fact "waden_owns_lg_orb" as author %}

Despite your apprehensions, you agreed to secretly study the artefact he passed on to you: an enormous luminous geode, by his account one of the three "Divine Tears" on which the mythology of the dangerous Akarith State is focused.

For safety, you mimicked Prof. Voluto's strategy: you rented, under a false name, a small house, whose cellar you turned into a field laboratory. And you spent several weekends studying this gem from every angle: atomic, optical, electrical, magnetic, wave properties... Nothing so far indicates that this object has the magical properties the Akarith prophecies speak of, but one thing is certain, it is not a material known on Pangea.

**Your own secret laboratory is located {% symbol "in the cellar of cottage no. 556, Latourbière Street, South Faeran. It stands opposite a GigAchat Shopping Centre, and 50 m from a cluster of PangeaTelecom booths" for "waden_secret_lab_address" %}.**

Emilos Loakim also sent you various artefacts from the Temple of Nazur; but for now, you already have plenty on your plate, so they are more of a burden than anything else. {% fact "waden_owns_lots_of_lg_artefacts" as author %}

When Loyd Georges' household found itself the target of countless attacks, you took fright. Whatever the "power" of this orb, it will bring misfortune upon whoever touches it, through the sheer power of Akarith religious belief; better that no one ever knows that you have committed this kind of sacrilege. Perhaps it is time to return this orb to Loyd Georges?



The Balberith blackmail
===========================

{% fact "waden_is_being_blackmailed_regarding_voluto" as author %}

{% symbol "15 days" for "waden_blackmailing_start_ago" %} ago, as you were off to recharge your batteries in a park not far from the Alifir Academy, a smiling young lady approached you, and dragged you to a nearby café, on the pretext of "an offer you won't have the heart to refuse". {% fact "waden_has_met_unknown_girl" as author %}

**"Call me {% symbol "Elezie" for "waden_blackmailer_lady_name" %}"**, that is all she told you about herself, in her strong accent typical of the land of {% symbol "Balberith" for "waden_blackmailer_lady_country" %}. Then she asked you to provide her with the results of all your work with Professor Voluto. You asked her in whose name. "We have recovered the only copy of the Alifir Academy's video recordings, in which you can clearly be seen assaulting the late Professor Voluto; that could reopen an investigation, involve lots of people in the affair... let's keep this between us. We can bring you a great deal. But let's start with simple things." {% fact "voluto_death_day_academy_recordings_disappeared" %}

She then gave you **three pieces of black rock**, labelled A, B, and C, in a small green satin box. "I want a complete physico-chemical analysis of these samples, radioactive elements included; you have the skills and the equipment; it is strictly confidential; I will contact you again in a few weeks to take stock; 100 kashes for you now, the rest on delivery".

You did not have much choice... The Academy's CCTV cameras are probably of too poor a quality to be conclusive, especially compared with the latest image-generation technologies, but the last thing you currently wish to draw upon yourself is public attention. The samples proved very interesting to observe, and you quickly finished your report on them. {% hint "radioactive_samples_analysis_report_part_1_for_waden" is needed %}  Out of curiosity, you also submitted these stones to a biophysical analysis device at the Academy, in order to determine their possible origin; you should receive the results any day now. {% hint "radioactive_samples_analysis_report_part_2_for_waden" is needed %}

But you are not quite sure where all this will lead you. Are these analyses just a test to check your good cooperation? Or is {% symbol "Balberith" for "waden_blackmailer_lady_country" %} really as backward, in terms of physico-chemical studies, as it seems to be?

Damn Voluto, all this because of him...



These last few days...
==========================

To your great surprise, you recently received an insistent invitation, just like your sweetheart, to Loyd Georges' grand evening of private sales. You would have preferred to stay discreet, but Cynthia was delighted by this opportunity, and there was no question of leaving her alone. You therefore hope that all will go well, and that no one will pay attention to the discreet lab scientist that you are.

You spend the day before the private sales evening in your secret laboratory, studying the orb. Then on the big day, in the early afternoon, you travel up to Salima by airship; pondering all the challenges surrounding you, without finding any obvious solution... {% fact "waden_alibi_is_revealing" as author %}
You meet up with Cynthia in town, to go up together to Loyd Georges' manor.
