=================================

The Dorian espionage service, to which you belong, sent you to investigate Loyd Georges several weeks ago.

Your code name is **Topaz**; it is notably the pseudonym you use to log in to your secure account on the world network, or to communicate with your espionage service.

For this mission, you have borrowed the identity of **{{character_properties.topaz.full_official_title}}**. The individual never having existed, you had carte blanche to build the character. You thus claim to work for {% symbol "Count Adamor" for "lg_remote_friend_lord_name" %}, a fairly distant acquaintance of Loyd Georges, but one who inspires his trust. For this you take advantage of the fact that the real count has gone on a cruise, for several months. {% fact 'topaz_is_undercover' as author %} {% fact 'topaz_official_is_non_existent' as author %} {% fact 'topaz_pretends_working_for_count_adamor' as author %}

You act as a duo with **Peridot**, a Dorian agent you know well, who for his part uses the identity of **{{character_properties.peridot.full_official_title}}**. You both thus took part in Loyd Georges' first auction, with a degree of credibility. {% fact 'peridot_is_undercover' %}

<{ title_specific_information }/>
=========================================


The thorn in your side, in this whole affair, is that even if you brilliantly succeed in your mission, your superiors will probably be far more generous with shiny medals than with hard cash. Keeping a sense of priorities, you have therefore rewarded yourself in advance: you resold on the black market, at twice their purchase price, various gems acquired at the Dorian taxpayer's expense, as part of Loyd Georges' first auction; but you did this solo and in complete discretion, of course. {% fact 'topaz_sells_gems_at_black_market' as author %}

You were able to blow the cover of an agent sent to take part in the auctions and investigate Loyd Georges: the so-called "{{character_properties.amethyst.official_name}}", pseudonym Amethyst, is in reality a Lordanian agent. The local contact of the Dorian intelligence agency, stationed in Cazoar, did in fact come across the real {{character_properties.amethyst.official_name}} in the admission records of a "venereal diseases" medical ward. Surveillance of this "Amethyst" has unfortunately not yielded any further information so far. {% fact 'amethyst_official_has_venereal_disease' %}

You were able to learn, from your superiors, that the Akarith riot that recently shook the Dorian capital, and cooled relations between Doria and Akaris, was in fact organised by a Masslavian agent, using the account "Malachite". {% fact 'malachite_triggers_akarith_riot_in_dorian_dukedom' %}
