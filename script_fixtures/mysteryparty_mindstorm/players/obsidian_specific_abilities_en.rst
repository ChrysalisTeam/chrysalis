<{ title_special_abilities }/>
======================================


Free Movement
    Your status allows you to come and go as you please in the manor and the grounds, as well as to leave them, unless Inspector Shark has entirely forbidden access to a given area. *Check with the Game Master if you wish to take a particular action outside the game area, but beware, this will obviously take up "game time".*

<{ wireline_fax }/>

<{ manor_police_searches }/>

Information Interception
    As deputy police officer, all the mail, requests and pieces of evidence intended for Inspector Shark (whom people, moreover, are often afraid of) pass through you. And he, likewise, calls upon you to pass on information to the other protagonists of the affair, or to give orders to the **dozen police officers** present on the premises. This gives you a strong power to manipulate information (addition, alteration, withholding...), which of course does not come without great responsibilities.

<{ new_identity_and_life}/>

Armed Squad
    We keep at your disposal a small squad of secret agents, backed up by a few elite elements from the army. It is currently positioned in the ministerial buildings of Salima. Make judicious use of it, taking into account its travel times (1 hour to reach the other end of the country).
