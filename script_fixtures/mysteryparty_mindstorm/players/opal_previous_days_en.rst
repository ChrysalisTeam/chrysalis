
The day before yesterday
==============================

Several weeks ago, you had taken samples of all your medications to the Alifir biochemistry laboratory; from what you understood, some tests required calling on other scientific disciplines. This evening, they finally called you back, worried at having found in your antihistamines highly "myotoxic" molecules, in other words muscle-destroying ones. Had you not reduced your daily doses, owing to the far less allergenic climate of the land of Sabarim, you would most likely no longer be of this world by now.

You are torn between the joy of finally understanding, and a certain fatalism: according to the experts, "the damage is done", and **you have barely a few years left to live**.

You immediately raise the alarm with your devoted henchman in Lordania, so that he has the pharmacy that regularly ships your medicines to you from there searched; and, seized by a sudden intuition, you try to get back in touch with the many characters, since lost sight of, with whom you went on one expedition or another in your youth.

A pattern quickly emerges: of the dozen or so of them who took an interest in Akarith antiquities, five have already died prematurely of illness or accident; and two others were outright murdered, then marked with a star-shaped tattoo vaguely evoking the famous crossentar (an Akarith religious symbol). From there to conceiving of an international brotherhood of fanatics, settling scores with those who dare desecrate Yodic artefacts, is but a step... they probably do not know that Lydia also trafficked in Yodic antiquities, in the past. But her presence at your side inevitably puts her in danger, as it does your son. {% fact 'opal_and_others_were_targeted_by_sect' as author %}

Lydia and Khaal discreetly left the day before, for a thalassotherapy cure in the misty mountains (near the eastern border of Sabarim), with a cousin of your wife's. You decide not to worry them with these stories. It is agreed that Lydia will return directly to Alifir the day after Loyd Georges' evening, to take up her duties, if - as expected - she is elected.

But Loyd Georges must be informed of this peril; a peril that existed well before the "Double Agent Emerald" affair; and well before the recent intrusion - reported by the press - of Akarith zealots into various countries of the continent. You decide to speak to him about it the next morning.

After an involuntary lie-in (the hazards of the sleeping pills you have been taking for months to calm your anxieties), you come downstairs at around {% symbol "10:45" for "morning_opal_wake_up_time" %}. You ask Rydji, the butler, for a meeting with Loyd Georges. But as bad luck would have it, the gentleman is about to begin a marathon of interviews, with each of the auction participants; this evidently at the instigation of Inspector Shark, now a regular at the manor. You will therefore have to wait until lunch. You spend your morning studying a few poisons and their antidotes on the web, from the library terminal, and trying to track down your last adventuring comrades.

At around {% symbol "12:30" for "noon_opal_lydia_call_time" %}, you receive a call from Lydia, and withdraw to your bedroom to answer it. Your wife informs you that she will after all be attending Loyd Georges' evening, to "sell one of her old plots of land" there. All this catches you off guard, but you promise to ask Loyd Georges' permission, as is proper.

At {% symbol "13:15" for "noon_meal_time_lg_opal" %}, you come down for the aperitif, and find Loyd Georges and Inspector Shark conversing in grave tones, seated on the sofas. On your arrival they change the subject. You then chat, more or less light-heartedly, about the grand evening to be held the following night; not without enjoying the sweet bluish liqueur, and the tempting little nuts, that Loyd Georges shares with you; all delicacies unknown to your palate. You slip in your dear wife's request along the way; Loyd Georges waves it through with a "yes yes, no problem, as you wish".

Then lunch is served: a delicious cod gratin with chanterelles. You hesitate to speak of your discoveries in front of Inspector Shark, because from your observations, this bloodhound has lost much of his nose with age; and he is more likely to put legalistic spokes in your wheels than to help you investigate. But since Loyd Georges' afternoon will again be taken up with interviews, you finally make up your mind, and lay out the mysterious danger threatening both your households.

Loyd Georges seems very downcast when he hears the names of a few mutual acquaintances, caught up by this "curse of the Yodic desecrators". Inspector Shark, for his part, takes your correlations seriously (for once), and scribbles notes while questioning you about the expeditions in question, their various protagonists, and the current state of your investigation.

After lunch, tired of going round in circles in this splendid but silent manor, you call a cab, which arrives at {% symbol "14:45" for "afternoon_opal_departure_time" %}. Unrecognisable with a long false moustache and fake spectacles, you quietly stroll around Salima city centre, and buy a few trinkets (a silver watch, a little book on Sabarith traditions...). You then pick a small comedy café-theatre, "Aux Lucioles Salimiennes", to take your mind off things.

When you return by taxi, at around {% symbol "21:30" for "evening_opal_return_time" %}, the butler tells you that Loyd Georges has already gone to bed; and that the inspector left at the end of the afternoon, to report to the higher-ups. So you too waste no time in taking a sedative sleeping tablet and falling asleep, in the large bedroom that Loyd Georges has set aside for your family. {% fact "opal_alibi_is_average" as author %}


The day everything goes off the rails
=============================================

The next morning, getting up at around **10:00**, you come face to face with Salimian police officers in the small hall. They tell you that Loyd Georges was taken ill the previous evening, very late, and that he is currently in intensive care at the Salima Central Hospice. You have not yet fully taken in the news when Inspector Shark arrives behind you, and declares the premises a "crime scene".

There you are, confined to the manor under house arrest, and forbidden to communicate with the outside world, without further explanation. The poor gentleman's study and bedroom are placed under seal.

When Inspector Shark tosses you a meagre filled sandwich at around **13:00**, you ask him for more information. He mentions a {% symbol "scene of a brawl in Loyd Georges' bedroom" for "shark_vague_explanations_to_opal" %}, but keeps the rest under the seal of "investigative secrecy". He does not seem to have you in his sights, but does not seem to trust you either.

Then your wife turns up at the manor. You had forgotten about her, with this whole business. You "brief" her, in the library, on the latest developments: the attack on Loyd Georges, your poisoning probably by Akariths, Loyd Georges' Orb that must be lying around somewhere...
