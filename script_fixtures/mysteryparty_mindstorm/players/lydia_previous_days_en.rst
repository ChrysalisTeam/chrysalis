
The last few weeks
==============================

Your husband was burning to take part in the mysterious auctions of a certain Sir Loyd Georges, whom he had rubbed shoulders with in the past. At the end of the electoral campaign, two weeks ago, you even went to settle in with the family at the gentleman's manor, near the city of Salima.  {% fact "opal_lydia_temporarily_moved_to_lg_manor_2_weeks_ago" as author %}

You were in great need of reinvigoration; so you declined Loyd Georges' special evening, to go on a {% symbol "thalassotherapy stay in the misty mountains (near the eastern border of Sabarim)" for "khaal_isildis_location" %}, with Khaal and your good cousin **{% symbol "Isildis" for "lydia_cousin_name" %}**. It was understood that you would go straight back to Alifir the day after the said evening (which is also polling day), to take up your duties there, if - as expected - you were elected. {% fact "lydia_was_out_of_manor_with_khaal_recently" as author %}

While resting in a bubbling bath, you had a flash of inspiration: among the eclectic possessions your parents bequeathed to you, there is a large plot of "tar sands", in the Kingdom of Suabel, in Austrion. Until now it was worth nothing, but technologies have evolved so much recently...

So you called upon your political network to see if this land interested anyone. Surprise, several contacts proved extremely interested, and begged you to meet one of their representatives; representatives who happen, by the greatest of coincidences, to be taking part in Loyd Georges' auctions as well.

So you telephoned your husband, around noon on the eve of polling day, so that he would ask Loyd Georges for permission to add a somewhat special sale to the menu: **the sale of this tar sands deposit**. Entrusting Khaal to the good care of your cousin, you prepared your return to Loyd Georges' manor for the next day, polling day, in the early afternoon. {% fact "lydia_alibi_is_revealing" as author %}


Polling day
========================

Taking the airship to Salima, you hear on the radio that you are still given as the favourite in the race for the Consulate of Alifir. Perfect. Did your opponent actually have nothing with which to attack your family? Or is he still biding his time?

On your arrival at the manor, you are flabbergasted: Loyd Georges was attacked in his bedroom, the previous evening; your husband also informs you that his physical decline is due to poisoning, and that one of the famous mythical "orbs" of the Akarith religion is said to be involved in the recent events.
