<{ title_your_intervention }/>
=================================

You are an elite agent of the Sabarith secret services. Your code name is **Obsidian**; in particular, it is the code name you use to log in to your account on the world network, or to communicate with your intelligence service.

Your country has sent you as reinforcement to Inspector Shark; despite his brilliant record of service, the latter seems to be struggling in his investigation into the crimes targeting Loyd Georges; and that is not surprising given the very particular scope of the affair. You were therefore introduced to him, a few days ago, under your official Sabarith identity of **{{character_properties.obsidian.official_name}}**.

Shark was initially very cold towards you. That is quite understandable: after a whole career spent single-handedly taking down serial killers and drug traffickers, he must have experienced this addition as a personal slight.
He therefore tasked you with investigating the backgrounds of the various auction participants, while forbidding you to "get under his feet" as regards the offences themselves.
With the support of your secret services, you could quickly bring him new elements, which would no doubt soften him up. But not everything is always fit to be passed on.
