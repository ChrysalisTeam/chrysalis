Your investigation had been stalling for a few days, and you had got to the point of picking quarrels with your colleague over nothing - just to liven up this flat that was too small for two - when Loyd Georges offered you a one-to-one interview, announcing that he had "important information" to share with you.

**You therefore go to this meeting at {% symbol "16:00" for "lg_meeting_time_for_spinel" %}, full of hope.**

<{ arrival_at_manor_with_butler }/>

Loyd Georges first makes you wait in the dining room. Inspector Shark walks past, ignoring you, and goes to talk with the old head cook, in the kitchen. He leaves for the Great Hall some ten minutes later. {% fact "spinel_encounters_shark_in_dining_room" as author %}

You wait until 16:20 before being received in the study. You then understand that Loyd Georges has nothing to tell you, but that he has concocted for you a veritable doctoral examination in jewellery.

Thrown off balance, you react badly, and get muddled in the few bits of knowledge you had hastily stocked up on. Loyd Georges breathes heavily - evidently he is not used to playing tough - and ends up accusing you of having lied to him from the start.

{% fact 'loyd_georges_quarrelled_with_someone' as author %}
You then protest your good faith, you play the outraged party, voices rise, you gesticulate wildly... You then realise that there is no point in getting any angrier, and you leave the study, slamming the door violently. The butler comes out of the library and escorts you back to the entrance gate, without a word. The interview lasted less than 15 min.

Late in the evening, you go down to the basement of a shabby den in the slums, the {% symbol "Drooling Parrot" for "malachite_spinel_alibi_tavern_name" %}, for a meeting with its manager, a liquor trafficker and head of a small local underworld. You were counting on his information and his henchmen to assist you, but the fellow states that "the situation was getting too dangerous at the moment, with the lunatics involved in this affair", and ends the meeting, wishing you "to come out of it alive". {% fact "spinel_alibi_is_illegal" as author %}
