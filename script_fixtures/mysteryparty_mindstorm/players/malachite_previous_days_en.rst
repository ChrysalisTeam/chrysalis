Your investigation had been stalling for a few days, and you were starting to go round in circles, in the flat shared with your colleague. You were about to go for a drink at the local pub to see if inspiration would strike, when Loyd Georges offered you a one-to-one interview, announcing that he had "important information" to share with you. What an unhoped-for twist!

**So you go to the home of the gentleman of Salima at {% symbol "12:00" for "lg_meeting_time_for_malachite" %}.** Not without bringing some delicious (and expensive) little tropical nuts, bought on the way, as an aperitif gift. {% fact 'malachite_offers_nuts_to_lg' as author %}

<{ arrival_at_manor_with_butler }/>

The interview (which starts late) is rather cold and laborious, because - as you quickly realise - Loyd Georges is actually seeking to test your supposed knowledge of Yodic history{% fact 'malachite_official_is_expert_in_yodic_history' %}; so you go through an awkward moment, while he remains very evasive about your own questions. The old man is clearly aware of being surrounded by spies!

Fortunately, you manage to name Georg Vidlaock and Vassil Becck to him, two historians of religion working at the University of Cazoar. Uncomfortable in his role as interrogator, he seems happy to seize upon this semblance of a guarantee, and launches into a conversation about these fellows, work acquaintances he knows far better than you do. To steer the conversation away, you talk to him about his beautiful pets, the admirable ornaments on his front door, the good taste of his various interior settings...

The interview ends at around {% symbol "12:30" for "lg_meeting_end_for_malachite" %}, without much gain for you, but without loss either. At least you were able to savour a little (a lot) of the delicious aperitif nuts you brought...

Against all expectations, it is the servant Alphonse who sees you out; and he does not have his colleague's rigour. At the manor entrance, you point out to him that he already has plenty to do, and that you can find your own way. Alphonse agrees with pleasure, and slams the door in your face. The gendarme is still in his sentry box, so you begin to circle the manor on all fours, under cover of a small hedge with red berries. You have duly spotted the surveillance cameras in the Great Hall and in Loyd Georges' study; but where are the others located?

A library window is open. You risk a peek. In the corner of the wall, a camera stares at you; but its indicator light is off. Lady Luck is with you. You leap inside. Loyd Georges is talking, in his study, with a person who must be Inspector Shark. You attach and activate an **audio recorder (shaped like a pen)**, on a high shelf near the small hall. Hard to do better under the circumstances.

A male voice makes your heart jump, in the bedroom adjoining the library. "Yes darling, of course I'll ask Loyd Georges for his agreement, but honestly, strictly between us, it's a shame you're cutting short your stay at the spa just to sell that old plot of land... well, as you wish, see you tomorrow then."

Too many people around. You migrate towards the vestibule, then the dining room. The camera there is disabled too. You place your **second and last audio recorder (shaped like a dictaphone)** behind a sofa. Movement can be heard in the kitchen, but also in the vestibule; you take refuge in the spacious pantry, behind the wine barrels. It is {% symbol "12:45" for "malachite_amethyst_encounter_time" %} by your watch.

Barely a minute later, an individual furtively enters the pantry, and leaps to your side. You recognise the bidder {{character_properties.amethyst.official_name}}.

You decide to leave the way you came. You crawl on all fours along the small hedge, until you are back at the front of the manor. No alarm triggered, perfect. You straighten up, and walk calmly towards the gate. Your involuntary sidekick takes his place at your side, and exchanges small talk in an artificially reedy voice.
