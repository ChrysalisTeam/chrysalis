

The last few weeks
==============================

Busy as you were preparing a report on the great annual summit of the Nalavutian tribal chiefs, you only followed Loyd Georges' latest expedition from a great distance. But you sensed, in your exchanges with your father, that beyond the media hype, something grandiose was really happening. Indeed, despite his age, Emilos Loakim has urgently put together a team to carry out excavations in Keroskia.

A fortnight ago, a good friend working at the Alifir Academy, **{% symbol "Sylwie Lescusa" for "cynthia_friend_name" %}**, called you, very ill at ease. She had seen your fiancé, from behind, chatting at length with a beautiful smiling stranger, in a nearby café. At one point, the lady even gave Waden a small green satin box. You reassured yourself as best you could. Perhaps someone from his family? Or they were doing business? Perhaps it was even a present for you? {% fact "waden_has_met_unknown_girl" %}

In the middle of last week, as you were dropping by Waden's flat during the day, to pick up the airship tickets he had bought for you, you came across his bank statements. And the strange mention of an **"Alifir–Faeran Weekend Rail Pass"**. Yet, as far as you know, Waden has had neither family nor friends there, for years.   {% fact "waden_has_tickets_for_faeran" %}

The next day, you dropped by to say hello to Loyd Georges, in Salima. At his manor, you became quite friendly with a certain "Lydia", whose family he is currently putting up. One thing leading to another, you talked about her child, about her husband's health problems, about the widening Sabarith societal inequalities that are creating growing tension in the country... but it was only once you were back home that you realised that she was actually in the running for a position as **consul of the country**, and that you had already dealt with her husband {{character_properties.opal.official_name}}... there you go, by dint of worrying about other countries' politics, one forgets one's own.  {% fact "cynthia_and_lydia_met_in_lg_manor" as author %}
As for Loyd Georges, he unfortunately had little time to devote to you, but you were still able to exchange some news, and you promised to send him a sketch of your new home. Your old friend seemed quite worn out by all the malice that has targeted him, which worried you.  {% fact "cynthia_wants_to_help_lg_and_friends" as author %}

Unexpectedly, you received an invitation to his "private auction evening"; this fitted in well with your schedule, so you planned to attend, with your fiancé who was also invited.

The day before this special evening, you work until late in the newspaper's offices, to plan the next trips to be made: the Yumeimatchi music festivals, the Balberith athletic tournament... {% fact "cynthia_alibi_is_strong" as author %}
You spend the following day shopping in the city of Salima, then meet Waden there, to go up together to Loyd Georges' manor.
