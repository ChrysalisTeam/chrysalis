
Your Missions
=================

In decreasing order of priority:

{% if not standalone %}
- recover the scattered sacred orb(s) (MAIN GOAL)
{% else %}
- recover the sacred orb captured by Loyd Georges
{% endif %}

- bring down the enemies of the Yodic faith, by handing them over to local justice, or by having them assassinated if they are far away (via the Akarith sect of the "Purifiers" that your superiors control)

- not be unmasked, at least not before achieving these goals

Reminder: when he has sufficient charges against a player, the inspector can have them indicted, and thus gain access to their Anthropia account, via a judicial seizure. By cross-referencing the clues thus gleaned, it should be possible to find the orb. *See the legal details in the Sabarith Law document.*

{% if not standalone %}
If he has access to the accounts of two players from the same faction, the inspector can try to recover the orb that the faction might possess, via an Akarith riot.
{% endif %}
