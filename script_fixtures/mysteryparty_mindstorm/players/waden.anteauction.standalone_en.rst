===================================

You are **{{character_properties.waden.official_name}}**, a young rising star of Experimental Physics in Sabarim.

You spent your childhood in **Faeran**, before joining the boarding school of the prestigious Alifir Academy. Thanks to old documents handed down in your family from generation to generation, you were one of the first to rediscover the laws of electromagnetism, which earned you an easy entry into the world of research.

Since then, you have been striving to make genuine discoveries, in a world in the throes of scientific upheaval; and your early work, on dating by radioactive decay, is to this day still acclaimed by Professor **Emilos Loakim**, director of the archaeology department of the Alifir Academy.

Your discreet and sedentary nature did not offer you many encounters, but a single one was enough for you. **Cynthia**, Prof. Loakim's daughter, an international reporter, immediately caught your eye. For a year now, you have therefore been getting to know each other, whenever her schedule allows.

Life is not all rosy, however, because of the heavy secrets you must protect....
