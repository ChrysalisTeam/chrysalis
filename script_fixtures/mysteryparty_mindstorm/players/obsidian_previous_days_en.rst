
The last few days
==============================

You were away in Alifir, for your investigations, when Inspector Shark pushed Loyd Georges into meeting the bidders, one by one.

You learned the next morning, from your superiors, that the gentleman had been hospitalised late that evening, "following an assault". The media were not informed.

The airship suffered a technical failure on your way back, so that you only arrived at the manor shortly before Loyd Georges' "Special Evening", which is clearly still going ahead.

The inspector told you to "get the servants to tell you about the events", as he was himself far too busy. Charming. So here you are, caught up in a strange imbroglio, without even having a head start on the guests who will be arriving in a few minutes.
