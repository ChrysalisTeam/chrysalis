==============================

Your investigation had been stalling for a few days, and you were about to go for a drink at the local pub to see if inspiration would strike, when Loyd Georges offered you a one-to-one interview, announcing that he had "important information" to share with you.

**Having underestimated Salimian transport, you arrive in front of the manor at around 16:25, on the appointed day, half an hour earlier than planned.**

<{ arrival_at_manor_with_butler }/>

The butler has you wait in the library, and goes to tap away at a control panel in the small hall. Wandering about innocently, you recognise the interface he is hastily browsing. It is the alarm system monitor, which controls both the manor's openings and the electrification of the high outer perimeter wall. In the library there is another screen, an almost brand-new Anthropia terminal.

Suddenly you hear a heated altercation, in Loyd Georges' study. An individual comes out of it, and violently slams the door, without you being able to see who it is. The butler rushes after him, flinging a peremptory "Don't move" at you. Out of the corner of your eye, you are sizing up the surveillance camera pointed at you when Loyd Georges comes out of his study, and invites you in. It is {% symbol "17:00" for "lg_meeting_time_for_garnet" %}. The gentleman seems shaken, and very tired, but everything is in order in the room. {% fact 'loyd_georges_quarrelled_with_someone' %}

You are hoping for new elements about your host's finds, but he actually has nothing to tell you. On the contrary, you are subjected to a stream of very technical questions on archaeology and your supposed collection of Akarith antiquities. You try to put on a brave face, but without much persuasive power. To your own questions, Loyd Georges only answers evasively.

To soften up your host, you get him to talk about his past "exploits", and the ingratitude of the academic world towards him. He then launches into an endless monologue about his youthful adventures, which unfortunately does not help you much.

Shortly before {% symbol "18:00" for "afternoon_lg_medecine_time" %} strikes on the clock, Loyd Georges' old servant, Alphonse, comes in and sets down in front of him a glass filled with an opaque grey substance. Your host lets slip that, having some heart problems, he has to take digoxin at a fixed time. {% fact 'loyd_georges_takes_medicine' %}
Before the servant leaves, Loyd Georges reminds him not to forget to pick up a new order from Master Florent, the apothecary in the nearby village. Thereupon, seeming exhausted, the gentleman promptly dismisses you, and shuts himself in his bedroom.

The butler leads you back towards the entrance. In the Great Hall, he takes to task the famous Alphonse, who is about to leave the manor as well; clearly, the old servant was not supposed to be away this evening.

You then seize the opportunity, and pretend to have dropped your pocket watch on the way, so as to be able to go back, as far as the small hall. Luckily, the session is still active on the monitor. In a few seconds, you configure an automatic shutdown of the alarm system between **{% symbol "22:00" for "evening_alarm_deactivation_time" %} and midnight**; no time to extend this shutdown until morning, as the butler is already arriving in the room. You brandish your pocket watch with a satisfied air; he firmly escorts you back to the gate of the grounds.  {% fact 'garnet_breaks_alarm_for_evening' %}

Just time to go and have dinner in town, and there you are, back at the manor, by the back lanes.
{% symbol "22:00" for "evening_alarm_deactivation_time" %} strikes. As planned, the indicator lights on the wall switch to "standby". Everything already seems to be asleep in the manor; that is not unusual, these Sabarith lords of the manor are early-to-bed, early-to-rise types.
So you wait no longer: you get into the grounds on the east side, near Loyd Georges' study.

A mastiff roams at random between the bushes; it is of a different calibre from the old dog Bedou, but given the size of the grounds, it is not very hard to get around it.

You approach the library window, and immediately set up a light beam pointing at its infrared surveillance camera, to render it unusable. {% fact 'garnet_breaks_into_manor_in_the_evening' %}
You then cut a large hole in the library window, with a diamond knife.
In the small hall, the alarm monitor is locked; never mind, you have plenty of time ahead of you, anyway.

The door to Loyd Georges' study slides open silently, and you blind the camera there in the same way; no light filters out from under the door of the gentleman's bedroom, but soft music comes out of it. You recognise one of those very fashionable "hypnotones", a sort of lullaby for adults. Whether Loyd Georges is already asleep or not, this background music is a godsend.

Stealthily, you move forward to the safe hidden behind a wall hanging; but in terms of security, it is too big a challenge. So you start with the drawers of the large ebony writing desk.

You leaf through various invoices, maintenance contracts, insurance contracts, and a contract for a safe-deposit box at the Bank of Salima (you note down the number of this last account: **{% symbol "AIXJ726H" for "lg_bank_account_number" %}**).
A small postcard, unstamped, catches your attention: "Put this somewhere safe at your place, it's dynamite, I'll study it when I get back. *E. L.*"

Suddenly, through the music, you hear moans of pain. The bedroom light comes on, and a ringing echoes throughout the manor. The servants' call bell. Your intuition tells you to clear off, and you obey it, not without retrieving the light beams on the way back. {% fact 'garnet_hears_moanings_and_bell' %}

As you run through the grounds, a shadow cuts across your path. The mastiff. You attempt a kick, it dodges and bites your shoe. You shake your leg violently, and run off again towards the perimeter wall. Once on top, you spot the dog pacing back and forth below, with muffled yelps. It is holding half of your sole in its jaws. The manor windows light up one by one. The dog runs to a low opening in the side of the manor, then comes back to you, its mouth wide open and panting. Never mind, you flee into the night. It must be {% symbol "22:40" for "evening_garnet_escape_time" %}, something like that. {% fact 'garnet_loses_shoe_heel' %}

You discreetly go home to bed in your little room at the {% symbol "Les Myrtilles guesthouse" for "garnet_rental_location" %}. {% fact "garnet_alibi_is_void" as author %}
