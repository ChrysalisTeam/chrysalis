MISSION - The defence of Sabarim
=============================================

*Message from the head of the Sabarith secret services*


Agent Obsidian, our country is in great danger.

Perhaps not as much as the nations neighbouring Akaris... but we too run the risk of seeing our homeland plunged into chaos.

The first danger is external: Sabarim lies on the edge of what could become a new Akarith empire. The Akariths therefore have every interest in destabilising us, so that we cannot serve as a fallback base for rebels, or as a logistical platform for an international coalition that might form against them. That is no doubt why there are more and more Akariths infiltrated in our country: Loyd Georges, and his cursed archaeological expedition, are not their only concern.

The second danger is internal: the people are exasperated by the corruption and privileges of the ruling class, by its privileges, by the academies' monopoly on knowledge and culture, by the poor treatment meted out to the lower classes of the population such as the "Illiterate". In this sense, the current consular elections constitute a last safety valve: if the triumvirate of consuls is entirely renewed, and firmly commits to improving the social situation, the institutions can hold firm. Otherwise, between legitimate social exasperation and the manipulations of the infiltrated Akariths, we will certainly be dealing with massive popular revolts.

We are legally supposed to be fully under the orders of the consuls. And until now, we have kept to that role, even when we were forced into operations with obscure objectives. But it is time for us to go back to basics, that is to say the security and independence of the Sabarith country.

In the context of these Loyd Georges auctions, you will therefore have many objectives:

- Help Lydia Magnus to accede to the position of consul, while making sure that she makes strong commitments (before an Escrow Agent) in favour of the reforms desired by the people.

- Investigate the Akariths and their allies, who have infiltrated Sabarim. We must find their centres of operation, and destroy them.

- Make sure that the people present, especially if they have contacts with the Press, do not prematurely light the powder keg with reckless statements. One day we will have to "clean out the stables", but let us wait until the organs of power are legitimised once again, before dragging Sabarim's corrupt before the courts, or denouncing foreign intrusions.

- Investigate this famous "orb" that Loyd Georges is said to have found, but with a single aim: to make sure that Sabarim stays out of any story about it. This kind of tawdry sacred symbol, capable of electrifying fanatical crowds, is the last thing we need on our soil.

- Protect Loyd Georges, who is currently one of the most loved and admired personalities in Sabarim. If his injuries were to prove fatal, it would be one more disgrace cast upon our State's ability to ensure the defence of its citizens.

- Officially assist Inspector Shark in his investigations, but while making sure that his discoveries do not run counter to the objectives above. Shark is getting old, his exploits are gradually being forgotten, which must irritate his ego, and he seems less and less attached to respecting legal procedures. All this can lead to additional problems, so you will at times have to (discreetly and cautiously) put a spoke in his wheels.

As regards foreign policy towards Akarith imperialism: strict neutrality has, historically, always been Sabarim's Credo. But we may be led to take a public stance, or even to give logistical help to one faction or another, depending on the plans of conquest being hatched by those crackpot Akarith High Judicators, and also on the image we must preserve in the eyes of the other Pangean countries.
