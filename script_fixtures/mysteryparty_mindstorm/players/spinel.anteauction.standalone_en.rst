<{ title_your_intervention }/>
===============================

The espionage service of Masslavia, to which you belong, sent you to investigate Loyd Georges several weeks ago.

Your code name is **Spinel**; it is notably the pseudonym you use to log in to your account on the world network, or to communicate with your espionage service.

For this mission, you are borrowing the identity of **{{character_properties.spinel.full_official_title}}**. The real person having gone trekking in the Sabarim hinterland for six months, you are in principle safe from any nasty surprise. {% fact 'spinel_is_undercover' as author %}{% fact 'spinel_official_does_trekking' %}

With your long-standing acquaintance, agent **Malachite**, who is your partner on this case, you have settled into a shabby flat in Salima, above the shop of "Master Bibine, apothecary". You have thus taken part in Loyd Georges' auctions, with a degree of credibility, and spied on the various participants. Malachite, for his part, is posing as **{{character_properties.malachite.full_official_title}}**. {% fact 'malachite_is_undercover' %}


<{ title_investigation_so_far }/>
======================================

Together with agent Malachite, you were led to organise a heist in a private warehouse in Alifir, your teammate having got wind that some of Loyd Georges' artefacts were there. You hired the services of mercenaries to seize them, but the container was empty. Either Loyd Georges had been tipped off, or spies from another faction had beaten you to it. {% fact 'malachite&spinel_rob_alifir_warehouse' as author %}

You then went back to the good old methods, and personally tapped the phones of Loyd Georges and his close acquaintances. {% fact 'spinel_wiretaps_lg_and_friends' as author %}
In the course of one of his telephone conversations with his friend Professor Loakim, of the Alifir Academy, you gathered that Loyd Georges was storing some of his discoveries in the basement of his manor. A devotee of direct methods, you then broke into Loyd Georges' home, taking advantage of his absence at a conference. But the manor's security systems were well honed, and you owed your salvation only to a swift escape. {% fact 'spinel_robs_lg_manor_fail' as author %}

Your services intercepted some accidentally poorly encrypted communications, which revealed (unsurprisingly) that the Akariths were indeed behind the threatening letters received by Loyd Georges, that they were pressuring the Dorian secret services to help them recover the orb, and that they were preparing a "divine show of force" in Sabarim.  {% fact 'akariths_pressure_dorians_secret_services' %} {% fact 'akariths_have_conspiracy_against_sabarim' %}

The conversation of a participant named "{{character_properties.opal.official_name}}" with his partner "Lydia" allowed you to discover that he had cheated at chess while playing online against Loyd Georges (thanks to the "ChessCheat54" software installed on his computer). He had thus won a few bottles of old wine. {% fact 'opal_cheats_at_chess_against_lg' %}
