The story of Inspector Shark
=====================================

Throughout his career, Inspector Shark has been a loyal and effective bloodhound of Sabarith justice. But as retirement approached, the midlife crisis hit him head-on. During the early stages of his investigation alongside Loyd Georges, he was unsettled by the Akariths' mystical propaganda, following the overtures of **the Akarith Archdeacon Baazel**. In particular, **he was seduced by the prospect of "transfiliation"**, namely the possibility of becoming, through his actions, a Chosen Son of the Yods (Yodism being originally a "hereditary" religion).

Shark therefore takes advantage of his position to set out in quest of the Divine Orbs (starting with the one Loyd Georges recovered). He keeps in frequent contact with his new Akarith masters, via a **P.O. box** located not far from Loyd Georges' manor, in the centre of the **{% symbol "Hamlet of Wiskons" for "hamlet_near_mlg_manor" %}**; in an emergency, he calls his mentors in Akaris from one of the **telephone booths** located in the same place (but he never uses the manor's telephone for this, it is too dangerous).

But his investigation proves too laborious, in the face of the high-tech means of action of the various foreign agents operating in the midst of these auctions. After trying to extract all possible information from Loyd Georges, and sensing the latter's reluctance (then growing suspicion), the inspector decides to get rid of him. But not without trapping, in the process, the auction participants around this murder, so as to be in a position of strength to worm information out of them.

Being like an "examining magistrate", the inspector can indeed **requisition** individuals for questioning. When he has sufficient incriminating evidence against a suspect, he can request their **indictment**, and take advantage of the additional powers (search, confiscation, coercion...) that this grants. But the inspector also intends, unofficially, to negotiate the dropping of certain charges, the "disappearance" of certain pieces of evidence, in exchange for an accused's cooperation. *See the "Elements of Sabarith Law" sheet for the legal side of this system.*

The inspector therefore convinces Loyd Georges to meet the various players, so that they find themselves in a "chronologically compromising" situation with regard to his future victim.

At the same time, Shark poisons Loyd Georges, making sure that he ingests, in one form or another, cardiac toxins causing severe bradycardia (slowing of the heart rate):

- before the evening meal on the "day of the interviews" (the day before the reception), he plans to replace the bag of bay leaves, in the kitchen, with one of oleander (toxic because of the oleandrin it contains), in the dish in sauce he himself suggested to the cook; this poison is fast-acting, and therefore takes effect shortly after Loyd Georges ingests it at dinner. This oleander was ordered from "Master Bibine, apothecary in Salima", and delivered to the P.O. box in the neighbouring hamlet. The ageing cook does not notice the trick.
- in case the oleander was not cooked for dinner, he could have tried to force an overdose of LG's usual anti-tachycardia medication (digoxin); but that was not necessary

Note that the anti-emetic "Resorpa" that LG takes to offset the side effects of digoxin (see the "MEMO" for Robb Barrow in the LibreOffice documents) prevents the poison from being regurgitated.

Loyd Georges suffers a cardiac collapse late in the evening, and is secretly hospitalised.

Until the following evening, this incident is kept secret by the inspector, so that most of the bidders come to the evening without suspecting anything.

He had also sent **fake invitations** so that Cynthia Loakim and Waden Shaw would attend too, although they were not initially expected. {% fact 'shark_has_sent_fake_invitations_to_cynthia_and_waden' as author %}

Shark will thus manipulate the suspects locked in the manor, frighten them so that they give each other away, and spy on them to take advantage of any discoveries they might each make on their own.

The inspector is ready to **break Sabarith law** if need be, but he must be careful, as certain people (including the Tabellion of Salima) could put serious spokes in his wheels if he stepped outside his legal prerogatives.
