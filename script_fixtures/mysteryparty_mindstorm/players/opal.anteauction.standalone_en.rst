===================================

You are **{{character_properties.opal.official_name}}**.

Having dabbled in every shady scheme of a world being reborn, you were long unable to say whether what drove you was money, or the taste of the forbidden. In any case, since your birth in Baynon (in the land of Lordania), you have been round the world three or four times, and frequented the most sordid taverns as well as the most refined embassies.
Not to mention a few helping hands given to the - relatively accommodating - **Sabarith secret services**; this through the intercession of your friend "The Docker", who has been a mission supervisor there forever, and with whom you have kept good ties. {% fact "opal_has_friend_in_sabarith_spies_called_the_docker" as author %}

But a few years ago, something happened to you that changes a man. The novels of this era like to say that a sulphurous life brings nothing but money and death. You discovered a third thing when you saw Lydia again, a long-standing acquaintance, half archaeologist, half smuggler depending on the state of her finances; that thing weighed 13 kilos, was 2 years old and was called Khaal. {% fact 'lydia_presented_2_years_old_khaal_to_opal' %}

Between a fortune acquired without quite noticing, a dreaded fortieth birthday, and an unexpected fatherhood, you first took it for a cosmic joke - without managing to laugh at it. Yet for some time already, your world had been slowly losing its savour, its pleasures were withering. Money made you respectable, the crooks no longer laughed quite as heartily with you. Age, too, was driving them away: provocations were less frequent, people called you "sir", some even greeted you. So with a child in your arms, imagine... and yet, after a week of dithering, you let yourself be won over by the little fellow and his - still intoxicating - mother. That was ten years ago.

You put your affairs in order and started a new career, treating yourself along the way to a discreet but adorable wedding. Making the most of an upbringing worthy of polite society, and of honourable financial capital, you developed a network of taverns in Lordania. Rome not having been built in a day, you kept a few sidelines, and your most loyal customers never had cause to complain about the welcome they received in the back rooms of your establishments; but mind you, nothing that could land you in prison - Khaal deserved to grow up with both his parents. {% fact 'opal_develops_lordanian_taverns' as author %}

From then on, your life looked like a perfect fairy tale: a spicy wife and accomplice, a child growing stronger and smarter every day, a flourishing financial empire, a name that commanded respect throughout polite society, and a few schemes still flattering your roguish side.


Later on, however, the horizon darkened.

For 2 years now, your taverns have been in serious trouble, facing unfair competition from illegal gambling dens. You therefore contracted, in your own name, heavy debts to afford the services of the **{% symbol "LordaBild" for "opal_construction_contractor" %} construction company**; this in order to turn your taverns into upmarket restaurants and wine bars, establishments for which there is a real market. {% fact 'opal_transform_taverns_into_classy_restaurants' as author %}
It will therefore take a few years before you can live the high life again.

Furthermore, for about a year now, your muscular strength has been dangerously weakening. The best doctors in Lordania kept juggling diagnoses, before admitting they understood nothing of it, and that it was probably a genetic weakness revealed by "old age".

In search of better care, you therefore moved 6 months ago into a charming villa near the Alifir Academy; also a way of supporting Lydia in her new venture: becoming the first woman "Consul of Alifir", and thus joining the trio of consuls who effectively run the Sabarith nation. {% fact 'opal_lydia_moved_to_alifir_villa_6_months_ago' as author %} {% fact 'lydia_runs_for_alifir_consul_election' %}

All this has worn you out a great deal. But with fifty approaching, you have finally found the answer to your great question: more than money and the forbidden, you love adventure. Hence your growing passion for Pangean antiquity, its artefacts and its legends. Like a child waiting for proof that pixies exist, you have since been searching, with growing frenzy, for whatever will make you shiver once more.

You would therefore not, for anything in the world, have missed taking part in the auctions organised by the now famous Loyd Georges, on the outskirts of the city of Salima. Especially since you had already met, even befriended a little, the man; that was 12 years ago, during a small, unpretentious expedition. Too bad for him that, *business is business*, you had to discreetly plunder and fence the few pretty liturgical objects of that tiny Yodic sanctuary in far-off Slavinka. Hard to say what colour Loyd Georges' laughter would be, today, were he to find out. {% fact 'opal_stole_lg_keroskian_artefacts' as author %}

========================================


As soon as you registered for the auctions, you received no fewer than three threatening letters, apparently from three different senders. You did not worry over so little - you were not born yesterday. {% fact 'opal_receives_akarith_threat_letters' as author %}

During the first series of sales, you bought a few unpretentious lots of gems. You also conducted your own little investigation, both into the myth of the orbs underlying this whole Akarith civilisation, and into the disreputable occupations of the other auction participants. For this you used your secure web account, on which your pseudonym is: **"Opal"**.

Little by little you unearthed clues about these famous Divine Orbs, to the point of becoming certain of their existence. Only one, however, has been located: an orb abandoned for centuries in a forgotten temple... the very temple that Loyd Georges "visited", in defiance of the Akarith locals. Full of admiration for Loyd Georges' audacity, you investigated this man seriously, along with the orb he cannot have failed to unearth. And one day, your detective alerted you to two consecutive burglary attempts at the unfortunate gentleman's manor. These misdeeds were carried out by mercenaries without a banner, which at first sight conceals who ordered them.

Except that you have kept your contacts in the "underworld". You did in fact belong to the "Red Wolves" gang in your youth, {% fact 'opal_was_in_red_wolves_gang' as author %} taking part notably in the memorable theft of the golden candles from the very apartments of the late Duchess of Doria, mother of the current Duke; not to mention the theft of the "Queen's Necklace", which belonged to Her Honour the Lord Mayor of Ouram (Lordania). Later, you also called upon the "Golden Hand" gang {% fact 'opal_worked_with_golden_hand_gang' as author %} to blackmail the Heliossarian prime minister, and recover the imperial studs that were part of the national treasure.

Your contacts thus allowed you to quickly identify the culprits of the two burglary attempts against Loyd Georges: an agent "Peridot", from the Dorian secret services, was the quickest, but ran up against the manor's security systems. The second burglary was attempted by "{{character_properties.spinel.official_name}}", but he too came back empty-handed. {% fact 'peridot_robs_lg_manor_fail' %} {% fact 'spinel_robs_lg_manor_fail' %}

You then used the network account of agent "Emerald", a former Lordanian spy, to conduct a more thorough investigation. {% fact 'opal_accesses_emerald_agent_web_account' as author %} He would have nothing to say against it: you swapped your glass with his, during a recent dinner at the Akarith embassy in Doria, after seeing him fill that glass of yours a little too conscientiously, in a corner of the room; so you feel only slightly guilty about his death, since you merely saved your own skin.
