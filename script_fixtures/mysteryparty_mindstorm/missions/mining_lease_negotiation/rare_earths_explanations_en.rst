*In search of Rare Earths*

To face the dark times ahead, no precaution is too great. Thus, our cutting-edge military industries are going to need, in increasing quantities, new materials called rare earths, to forge the electronic components of military vehicles and weaponry; we therefore absolutely must diversify our supplies.

Now, a Sabarim public figure is offering for sale a large deposit of such tar sands, which happens to contain precisely these "impurities".
With modern techniques, we can extract within a few months all the resources of interest to us in the deposit, that is to say the equivalent of **12 megakashes** of rare earths (the remaining components are of no concern to us).
