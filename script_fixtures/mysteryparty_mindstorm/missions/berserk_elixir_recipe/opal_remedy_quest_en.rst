
MISSION - The Berserk Life-saving Elixir
========================================

You are now convinced of it: your only hope of survival now lies in the mythical Berserk Elixir, whose healing and warrior-boosting powers are mentioned in numerous Akarith legends.

Loyd Georges has hinted to you many times that one of his adventuring companions, a globetrotter called **{% symbol "Rodok" for "lg_adventurer_friend_name" %}**, was gradually making headway on promising leads, following the exploration of the Temple of Nazur.
But he would catch himself right afterwards, murmuring that he "did not want to give you false hopes".

The only thing the writings agree on is that it is a mixture mainly made up of **three elements** that are fairly common in Akaris. But which ones? In what proportions? With what method of preparation? And are there other products to be used as additives?

With Loyd Georges' interviews and the preparations for the special evening, the subject was naturally swept under the carpet.

But now that everything has gone wrong, you hope with all your heart that the butler Rydji, Loyd Georges' closest friend, will be able to help you get in touch with this globetrotter, and to continue the search for this damned recipe. Not only for yourself, but also to get Loyd Georges back on his feet.
