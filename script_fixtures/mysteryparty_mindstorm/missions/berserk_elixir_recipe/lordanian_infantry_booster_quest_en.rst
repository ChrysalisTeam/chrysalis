
MISSION - The Berserk Military Elixir
========================================

{% fact 'agent_mission_investigate_berserk_elixir' %}

Agents,

Our homeland currently enjoys a comfortable military lead over the other Pangean powers, mainly thanks to our monopoly on flying fortresses.
However, we remain weak in steep and underground terrain, where our infantry, and even our special forces, lack decisive advantages.

Our laboratories are focusing their efforts on new steam-powered exoskeleton armour, but the biological enhancement of our soldiers remains an interesting avenue, and the Yodic conflicts of the past could teach us a thing or two on this subject.

Indeed, legends recurrently mention a healing and performance-enhancing brew jealously guarded by the Yodic clans: the Berserk Elixir. We know very little about its basic composition, but we have recovered extremely rare clues about its more sophisticated variants. {% hint "berserk_elixir_advanced_ingredients_for_amethyst" is needed %}

We have learned that Loyd Georges had found writings on this subject, in the buried Akarith temple; and that he hoped to let his friend {{character_properties.opal.official_name}}, whose health is inexorably declining, benefit from them.

Historically, "magic potions" of this kind have always yielded disastrous results. We therefore mandate you to discreetly help these fellows manufacture and ingest some variant of this Elixir. That way, we will know for certain.

If this Elixir really works (and without irremediable side effects), there will be time enough to recover its recipe, one way or another ({{character_properties.opal.official_name}} owns numerous assets in Lordania, so it should even be possible to work with him on good terms and in exchange for fair compensation).
