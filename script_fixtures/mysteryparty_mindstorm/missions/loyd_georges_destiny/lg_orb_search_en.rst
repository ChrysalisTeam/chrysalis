MISSION - Loyd Georges' Orb
===========================================

Agent,

we must give our utmost to recover this damned "sacred orb" found by Loyd Georges. Given the number of countries involved in the affair, it is only a matter of days before someone else gets their hands on this artefact. And in the hands of someone who wishes us harm - or who simply does not share our intentions - this could have painful repercussions.

Our priority target is Loyd Georges' Anthropia account. We have tried to gain access to it through a recovery mechanism, on the portal's authentication page: the "secret answer". But we do not have the solution to the question set by Loyd Georges: "**{{ character_properties['loyd.georges'].secret_question }}**"

Take advantage of being on the premises to investigate this; by hacking and searching Loyd Georges' Anthropia account, we will learn a great deal more. If he has entrusted the orb to one of his acquaintances, locating that person will become the new priority.

Furthermore, it is likely that Loyd Georges hid part of his archaeological haul along with the orb, as it seems to be neither in his manor nor in his safe at the Bank of Salima. This opens up great prospects for us, because the brand-new **World Scanner** service allows us to locate fairly precisely, on Pangea, the items similar to the scanned object.

So take advantage of the auctions, and other opportunities, to get hold of promising artefacts, suitable for narrowing down - by correlation - the position of the orb. Of course, these acquired artefacts must be neither too common, otherwise the scanner will return far too many possible locations, nor too rare, otherwise the chances that another specimen was hidden with the orb will be too slim. Submit the relevant items to the World Scanner as soon as you can.
