We cannot afford this betrayal by our former Dorian allies. We have therefore sweated blood to find out more about this council. And it appears that the Akarith religious leaders are going to call various external experts, to ask their opinion on this possible merger between the two peoples.

**Prof. Schaladuk**, an expatriate Akarith sociologist living in Doria, is (unknowingly) one of these interlocutors. We have therefore connected a telephone interceptor at the entry point of his home. We will redirect the call to the first of our agents who can be reached, via low-frequency waves (with a very long range, and able to get through even telephone signal jammers).

Let every agent therefore stand ready to answer a call from this council, and to feed them whatever line is needed, depending on their questions, to scupper this attempt at a "merger of the Far-Midolian peoples".

Keep abreast of the latest developments in Yodic theology, because the council will not be content with asking for your opinion; it will want to know what the Dorian people think of this or that theological question, and on these subjects, the devil is in the details...
