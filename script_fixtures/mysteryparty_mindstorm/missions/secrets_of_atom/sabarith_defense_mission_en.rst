MISSION - The Atomic Traitor
====================================


{% include "missions/secrets_of_atom/generic_mission_briefing_en.rst" %}

The matter came up without warning: during routine wiretaps of the powerful (and not very cooperative) Alifir Academy, the Sabarith secret services were surprised to come across a venerable professor of atomic physics, in the middle of negotiations with a Dorian ambassador. And not on just any subject: "{% symbol "revolutionary discoveries in the military field" for "physicist_discovery_claims" %}", to be exchanged for money and glory.

Immediately, all hands on deck; you were sent to search the house of this "Professor Voluto", while your colleagues cast their nets around all his means of communication.

Bad luck, the professor came home unexpectedly, in the middle of the afternoon. His arrest went very badly; despite his great age, he proved incredibly violent, and his heart gave out when you pinned him to the ground...

The affair was hushed up as it had to be: the worthy scientist supposedly died "{% symbol "at home, of a cerebral haemorrhage following a fall" for "physicist_death_circumstances" %}", according to the Press.
But while national security is preserved for now, there is no trace to be found of the deceased's findings, neither at his home, nor in his digital documents.

Approached by the Sabarith State, the Alifir Academy sidestepped the issue, assuring that after exploring various unsuccessful theories, the professor was only working on improving the precision of radiating body scanners. In a move of historic significance, it agreed to open the professor's office to a ministerial delegation, and there again nothing in particular was found. Strangely, the Academy's CCTV recordings for the day of his death were reportedly "prematurely destroyed".  {% fact "voluto_death_day_academy_recordings_disappeared" %}

Hard-fought steps are under way to gain access to Professor Voluto's Anthropia digital account, but cunning as that jackal was, even if this were to succeed one day, you do not expect much from it. {% fact "voluto_anthropia_account_is_being_investigated_by_sabariths" %} {% hint "voluto_anthropia_account_investigation_result" is needed %}

You have identified several scientists of the Academy with whom he worked during the past year: **{% symbol "Doctor Marloff of the applied optics department, the young physicist Waden Shaw, and Professor Idn Gark of the New Energies division" for "voluto_suspicious_colleagues" %}**. At the risk of rekindling tensions with the Academy, you will have to grill these worthy researchers a little.

**The triumvirate of Sabarith consuls, which supervises the activity of the secret services, has in any case been very clear: Sabarim is indeed a neutral and peaceful country, but "If you want peace, prepare for war"; Professor Voluto's discoveries must therefore return as quickly as possible into the hands of the national defence army.**
