

Beneath the sumptuous vaults of the Pangean scientific academies, scientific (re)discoveries have been flourishing for a few decades. "New energy sources" are a particularly recurrent subject of study: sun, winds, tides, rock oils... every country is looking for alternatives to coal steam.

Thus, what worldwide joy when a new energy was discovered, 8 years ago. An energy making it possible to photograph through the human body, to date antediluvian objects, and to make beauty creams of unrivalled virtue. This marvel of Nature, the "Sun of Atoms", did not however remain the darling of the media for long. Expensive to obtain, insufficient to power vehicles, it turned out above all to be a slow and insidious poison.

This technology has therefore returned to obscurity in recent years; but it has not said its last word.
