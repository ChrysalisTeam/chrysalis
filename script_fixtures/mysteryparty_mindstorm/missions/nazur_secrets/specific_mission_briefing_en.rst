MISSION - The Secret of Nazur
===================================

While this ill-fated day passes rather quietly, you recall your last conversations with Loyd Georges.
And in particular, the dissatisfaction he confided to you at still not having managed to understand what had really happened in the buried Temple of Nazur.

Why that vandalism on the walls and ceiling, when the holy of holies was sealed, and the gems were still there?
Why that mummy right in the middle of the room?

To make matters worse, the biophysics laboratory of Belez had stated that they had sent the results of the analysis of said mummy by express mail, 4 days ago.
But Loyd Georges had still received nothing as of yesterday, and for confidentiality reasons, the laboratory never keeps archives of its analyses...

Your intuition screams at you that there is material here for a real archaeological scoop, just as much as the skirmishes and wanderings of the Yodic denominations between Keroskia and Midolia.
Whether Loyd Georges pulls through or not, you can sense that his dearest wish would be for you to unravel this mystery.
And for your part, a little burst of glory would fulfil you, even if the elusive Akarith assassins were to catch up with you one day.

It is in this frame of mind that you receive, on the stroke of noon, an ultra-secure message from The Docker of the Sabarith secret services:

  Hello my dear,

  with a few comrades, we have dug into this business of an aquatic incursion into the buried Temple of Nazur.

  Apparently the Judicators placed the temple under interdict before even having fully explored it, which guarantees us a fine haul if we get to the many gems that Loyd Georges' expedition must have left behind in its haste. But time is running out, because the temple is slowly filling with water following the blocking of groundwater tables, and the damage will soon be critical. We have therefore made a deal with our superiors to borrow equipment and share the proceeds; and we are now on our way, by private airship, to the Akarith border.

  I seem to recall you told me about a puzzle in this temple, with artefacts and a well. Know that we will have a 3D printer on site, so if you want to send us digitised models so that we also take care of digging into this matter, don't hesitate. It is probably the last opportunity.


You then steel your resolve for the strange evening that lies ahead.

- manage to convince the two other jokers to lend you the crucial artefacts, the Rusty Grate and the Stone Lantern, which they acquired at the auctions.
- then contact the Bank of Salima so that it sends 3D scans of these items to The Docker's address
- also try to track down the results of the mummy analysis, because given the usual high quality of the Sabarim Postal Service, there must be something fishy going on
- keep an ear out for any other clue relating to this Temple of Nazur
- bear in mind that a gang of Akarith assassins is on your trail, and why not beat them at their own game
