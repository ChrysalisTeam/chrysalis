
MISSION - The Panorbium Fortuna
================================

{% fact 'agent_mission_find_panorbium_fortuna' %}

Our intelligence agents have traced an intriguing event, around the excavations that Prof. Loakim, a friend of Loyd Georges, is currently conducting in Keroskia, in the land of Slavinka.
Indeed, a week ago, the Sabarith researchers came across jars filled with ancient paperwork, in a fairly good state of preservation.
But instead of quietly digitising them in the field laboratory, they took part of these finds away from the site after nightfall.

According to our various cross-checks, it is Prof. Loakim's direct deputy, **Ernest De Villeneuve**, who is said to have left with a briefcase, via an airship specially chartered for the operation, heading for Sabarim.

We had agents on surveillance around the Alifir Academy, but De Villeneuve never went there; and two days later, he was back on the Keroskian site, as if nothing had happened.

We have reason to believe that this briefcase ended up in Loyd Georges' manor, but by roundabout means, because our informant on site did not see this De Villeneuve go by either.

Why take such an interest in this paperwork, you may ask? According to an indiscretion from someone close to Prof. Loakim, one of the exhumed booklets may be none other than the **Panorbium Fortuna**, a slim runic book detailing the power of the three Divine Orbs and their ultimate destiny. Suffice to say it is planetary theological dynamite, if only because of the sacred aura surrounding it.

Until now we knew of only one copy of this grimoire, lost for centuries before being found by the Akarith Judicators. Since then, they have kept it jealously guarded in the secrecy of their citadel. A copy of it would allow us to learn more about the Akariths' plans, because for them this book is almost a strategic doctrine, a plan for world conquest.
You will therefore take advantage of being admitted into Loyd Georges' manor, during this evening, to investigate, and try to get your hands on this wretched book, or failing that, on its contents.

It is impossible to know, as things stand, what the various inhabitants of the manor know about this spiriting-away operation, or whether Inspector Shark is in on the secret.
But what is certain is that other factions have surely not missed all these goings-on, so you will have to play it close.
