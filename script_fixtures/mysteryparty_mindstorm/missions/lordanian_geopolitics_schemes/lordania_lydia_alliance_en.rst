MISSION - The alliance with Sabarim
=====================================

Agents,

the land of Sabarim has historically always been very neutral, but the latest consuls have shown themselves suspiciously distant towards our country, and accommodating towards the Akarith Judicators, despite the growing infiltration of Akarith henchmen into Sabarim.

All this could change with the consular elections now under way. {{character_properties.lydia.official_name}} embodies a new political generation, she is leading in the polls, and she is married to a Lordanian; all this could herald a pleasant rapprochement between our two countries: economic, scientific, and above all military.

Indeed, nothing would delight us more than obtaining **access to a Sabarith air base**, so that the regions of Midolia and Axolia are within striking range of our glorious flying fortresses.

We therefore mandate you, on the one hand, to support the candidate in the - unlikely - event that she needs help, and on the other hand, to advance with her (and her husband) the idea of a rapprochement with Lordania.

Beware, flagrant interference by our country in the Sabarith electoral process could painfully backfire on us; so you will need to show delicacy.
