


The events punctuating the evening
=========================================


*Here is a chronology of the various clues, facts and sub-quests that may come into play; the Game Master is free to adapt these examples to their own game.*

Long in advance:

- RADIO SPOTS: "Special auction evening"

Well before the game, between GMs and NPCs:

- brief the NPCs, give them their papers and additional props
- tell them to regularly report to the Game Master the progress in the quests, the decisions, the miscellaneous interactions made by the players; in particular, after an interview with a player (in person or by phone), quickly report what came out of it.
- give a demonstration of a duel of honour
- brief them on the key scenes: arrival of the inspector, of the Tabellion, of the Akarith Ambassador; reading of LG's will; negotiation of the mining deposit; auctions; assassination of the Akarith Ambassador; Opal's Berserk transformation; assault by the Akarith Purifiers
- brief Inspector Shark on his study, on how he can proceed with the interrogations (SHORT, 10-15 min), in particular putting pressure on the players (e.g. lights, noise generator), and typing on the typewriter; give him the document "The Origin of Precious Stones" to question the "jewellers"; give him the jacket with the antidote sewn into the lining

- check that the technical systems work properly (fuses, switches, steam engine...)
- set up the full recording of the murder party, including sound if possible
- make-up: to age a character, draw fine wrinkles with a black kohl-type pencil and blend, or else with grey eyeshadow; or better, mark the dark circles under the eyes with dark eyeshadow and grey the hair

- WARN ABOUT ALLERGENIC DISHES: "OBSIDIAN: Allergy to goat's cheese (and a few others, but I know how to avoid them, and they are not serious)."


- how the NPCs work:

    - the NPCs RYDJI, ALPHONSE and INSPECTOR SHARK stay as such all evening.
    - the NPC playing the TABELLION starts as a Servant, then arrives at the manor as the Tabellion and remains so until the end
    - the 2 multi-role NPCs alternate between different characters, according to the indications given below.
    - as far as possible, it is an "armed guard" who comes to bring Obsidian the search reports and to take orders


When the players are there, out-of-game:

- give a tour of the premises, say what is IC and OOC, the potential "purely OOC" characters to ignore (e.g. the photographer)
- recall the most important rules (immersion, safety...)
- FORBID drinks in the computer area
- take stock of FOOD ALLERGIES for the buffet!!
- tell EVERYONE "you know how to detect whether a SERIOUS POISON is in your glass"
- ASK whether anyone is allergic to SMOKE-machine fog!!
- Specify AUTOMATIC AUTHENTICATION: yes, but only for emails/letters/calls that concern YOU, directly or handed over in person
- advise them to COMMUNICATE and to PLAY AS A TEAM
- ANNOUNCE the overall schedule, and that 30 min before the end of the game, the players will be offered herbal tea/tea!!
- say that the fax-phone also has VIDEO (but rather blurry)

- give the players their last-minute additional clues, and their accessories such as coloured scarves, UV lamp, money in kashes...
- draw the mark of the "Red Wolves" on Opal's hand (a red fir tree)
- ask whether there are OOC questions, about the character sheet, about the rules...
- brief the civilians and Obsidian on what is happening outside the manor (they do not have the "last minute context" sheet like the others)

- IMPORTANT: take photos and videos!


Briefings in roleplay mode:

- a "slightly roleplay" PHOTOGRAPHER takes pictures of them individually and by nationality
- ask the players to confer in groups as they would have done just before the evening
- do the "initial briefing" of each group of players with their "mentor"
