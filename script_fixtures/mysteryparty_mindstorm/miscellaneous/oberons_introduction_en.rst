

Oberon Mediation
=============================


*The Oberons are an international brotherhood of high-end mercenaries, used for all kinds of more or less legal missions. They are known as much for their discretion as for their loyalty. The Oberon hub of the city of Salima has just sent this circular to all its contacts.*

Dear clients, many of you have requested our services in connection with Loyd Georges' famous Private Evening.

You are certainly familiar with our policy in the event of conflicts of interest between our clients: ABSTENTION.

However, in order to bring out constructive synergies, we have decided to place go-betweens at your disposal. Do not read anything untoward into this; these are simply some of our agents who, under cover of waiting on guests at the manor, will be at your disposal for various favours and mediations:

- delivering, free of charge, anonymously or not, a message (spoken or written) to another guest.

- putting you in touch, free of charge, with another of our clients who has the abilities you need to make progress in your undertakings. This service is free, but for the sake of confidentiality, the Oberon will not point you directly to the person who can help you; rather, he will go and tell them about your request.

- carrying out on your behalf a simple action on the Anthropia web portal: transferring money or an item, activating a special ability (analysis of an item, wiretapping...) etc.

- fetching from another room of the manor, or placing there, the item of your choice, provided that room is not specifically secured. This service is charged at 50 kashes per task. In case of failure, the client receives a credit for a new service.

- for any other service, feasibility and price are to be negotiated case by case with the Oberon.

Recognising an Oberon mediator is very simple: use our password, and tell him **"you have a mighty pretty nose"**; if he answers **"your eyebrows are just as pretty"**, then he is one of ours.

Of course, our agents will never take an action they know is likely to harm them, or to harm another of our clients. We count on your cooperation, so that this call for mutual collaboration goes as smoothly as possible. The only exception to this rule is the Akariths: these fanatics respect nothing and violently meddle even in our Oberon affairs in Sabarim, so we will be rather cooperative if you seek to harm one of them.

Furthermore, you are always free to hire the services of a group of Oberons, for actions outside Sir Loyd Georges' estate, if you had previously obtained the right to do so; in that case, use our worldwide recruitment page on the Anthropia web portal.

most delicately yours, *The Oberons*
