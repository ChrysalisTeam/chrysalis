
Overview of Special Abilities on Pangea
==============================================

.. container:: emphasis

    Here are the advanced abilities you have already heard of, or even already encountered, over the course of your career; some of them are discussed in more detail in the chapter on the "Anthropia Portal".

    **Unless otherwise stated, by default, you do not master these abilities.**

    But who knows, among the various protagonists of this evening, you may well be dealing with experts in one or another of these fields, or with initiates mastering hitherto unknown techniques...

    **Call upon Oberon mediation if you lack some of these abilities to progress towards your objectives.**

.. raw:: pdf

   Spacer 0 10

Hand-to-hand combat, martial arts, the art of vital points...

Geolocation of the senders/recipients of a call or a computer message...

Communication (with or without encryption) by telephone (landline/mobile), long-range radio, pneumatic tubes, carrier pigeons, couriers...

Computer hacking, interception or destruction of digital data...

Recognition of forged documents, graphology, stylistic analysis...

Manipulation of the Press, disinformation, concealment of events...

Translation of texts or runes from various civilisations, decoding of coded messages...

Expertise in explosives, poisons, weapons, sensors, high technology...

All-terrain engineering: making explosives from kitchen ingredients, weapons from hardware, communication systems from car wrecks...

Intrusion, camouflage, lockpicking...

Criminology: forensic medicine, fingerprints, ballistics...

Sciences: expertise in physics, chemistry, medicine, mathematics...

Hypnosis: truth interrogation, suggestion or erasure of memories, treatment of post-traumatic stress...

Airborne and underwater operations, operations in forest, desert or polar environments...
