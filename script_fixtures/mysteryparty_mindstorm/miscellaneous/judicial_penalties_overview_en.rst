
Sabarim Justice
===========================

The Sabarith judicial system is known for its **proverbial severity**.

Indeed, it is rare for the sentence handed down to be lower than the maximum penalty provided for by the Penal Code. And penalties can be combined without limit when several offences are committed.

Moreover, Sabarith Law has no notion of **"fairness of evidence"** as other countries do. Admittedly, investigators, witnesses, or victims who use illegal means to obtain evidence risk being prosecuted for it; but this does not invalidate the incriminating elements they may have against others, as long as these elements are authentic.

As an accused person, it is possible to remain free until the trial, if one pays **bail of 2,000 kashes**. {% fact 'misc_release_on_bail_possible' %}

**For information, an excerpt from the Sabarith scale of penalties, in years of imprisonment:**

- Dumping rubbish on the public highway: 1 year
- Identity theft: 10 years
- Interception of private communications: 12 years
- Hacking of a computer system: 12 years
- Cruelty to animals: 8 years
- Burglary: 8 years
- Robbery with violence: 12 years
- Attempted murder: 15 years
- Murder: 25 years
- Arson: 13 years
- Criminal conspiracy: 10 years
- Breach of national security: 15 years
- Counterfeiting of currency: 50 years
- Insult to the Sabarith anthem: 100 years


Strangely, certain unlawful acts are **not punished by law**, because case law has equated them with involuntary or addictive behaviours, giving rise at most to a simple treatment order:

- Spreading false news: equated with mythomania
- Simple theft (pickpocketing and the like): equated with kleptomania
- Failure to comply with instructions or prohibitions issued by a police officer: equated with memory disorders
