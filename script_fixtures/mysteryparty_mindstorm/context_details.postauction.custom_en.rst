Sir Loyd Georges' Household
===================================

Following all the attacks of which Sir Loyd Georges has been the target, most of his servants have chosen to resign.
The poor sir, for his part, has stepped up his protection: on the one hand thanks to the services of Inspector Shark, sent by the State, and on the other hand by fitting the manor with **sophisticated security systems: an electrified outer fence, surveillance cameras in the main rooms**... Unfortunately for him, neither has always proved very effective.

{# .. Meanwhile, you have been mixing with high society, which will also, you have no doubt, be present at this famous evening. #}

During the evening of private sales, you will have the opportunity to meet once again the close circle of the famous adventurer of Salima:

Rydji
    Loyal butler and friend of Loyd Georges, who took part in the archaeological expedition to Midolia, and has recently specialised in fortifying the manor. His integrity is beyond reproach, but he knows how to cooperate even with complex intrigues, when it serves the interests of his household.
    He is authorised to referee **Sabarith duels of honour**.

.. You have sometimes seen him cast a highly suspicious eye on you.

Alphonse
    Known as Alph', he is the only servant (along with Rydji) who has not deserted Loyd Georges' manor. Perhaps because he is the most senior of Loyd Georges' servants. Perhaps because his devotion and courage are boundless. Perhaps, more simply, because he has little grasp of what is going on around him, lost as he is in concerns of an altogether higher philosophical plane. When it comes to cooperating with a stranger, he seems particularly receptive to financial arguments. He too is authorised to referee **Sabarith duels of honour**.


Robb Barrow
    Senior servant of {% symbol "Count Blispin" for "lg_close_friend_lord_name" %} (a friend of Loyd Georges), Robb is a man of many skills (cook, barman, gardener...), but whose senses are in steep decline with age. He has been seconded, for the last 10 days, to the service of Loyd Georges, in particular to help with the special evening.
    As far as you know, he is "respectable in every way".


The dogs
    **Bedou** is an ageing angora sheepdog, who mostly wanders around inside the buildings.
    But Loyd Georges' new guard dog, **Oupaout**, a touchy mastiff, is of an altogether different mettle.
    Both usually sleep in the kennel adjoining the manor.
