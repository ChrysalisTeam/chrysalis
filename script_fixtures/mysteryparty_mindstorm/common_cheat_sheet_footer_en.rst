
Generic abilities and miscellaneous advice
=============================================

These initiatives might prove useful to you:

- use the escrow agent system to seal secure contracts, on paper or via Anthropia messaging
- grease the palms of the right people to obtain clues or a helping hand
- challenge opponents to a duel of honour, to extract valuables from them or to hamper their manoeuvres
- call upon the Oberon mediator to obtain specific abilities, or to carry out simple actions on the Anthropia web portal without cutting yourself off from the course of the evening
- contact supporters outside the manor (superiors, mentors, family, friends...) to obtain advice or help
- fight (really only as a last resort)

**Beware of Inspector Shark.** If he takes a dislike to you, he may well destroy everything that matters most to you.
