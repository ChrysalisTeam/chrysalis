Sheet of the Tabellion of Salima
================================

**{% symbol "Maestre Ramye Leydelass" for "notary_name" %}**

You are a Tabellion (a sworn notary) of Salima, and secretary general of the Order of Tabellions.

This prestigious post, peculiar to Sabarim, demands extreme probity of you (you are sworn in), and gives you numerous prerogatives: you may act as a notary, banker, accountant, bailiff, auctioneer...

You had to fight hard, and spend a great deal of money, to make such a fine career, coming as you did from a family of petty bourgeois who had never held any political role. You are therefore very attached to this professional success and to its rewards.

Loyd Georges had recently become a major client, and almost a friend. Not only had he called upon you to conduct his auctions, but he had also let you in on the secret of his finances, which were in the middle of being put back afloat.

After the first series of sales (fairly ordinary artefacts and gems to begin with), he invited you to his "very special evening", entrusting you with several missions.

- you must conduct a **series of archaeological sales, concerning objects as well as precious stones**. {% symbol "Gem lots are sold by descending auction, while artefacts are sold by normal ascending auction." for "auction_process_instructions" %}

- you must read in private, with him, the **draft of his will**, which he keeps in his safe. You are carrying various copies of his documents, including the said draft. Should anything happen to him, Loyd Georges has asked you to proceed without delay to the reading of his will, so as to preserve the interests of his household.

- you must lend assistance to the **sale of the mining concession** that Lydia will conduct, that is to say let her freely conduct this sale, but make sure beforehand that the potential buyers do have a valid mandate, and remain at her disposal should she need advice. The purchase price must be a multiple of 0.5 megakashes.

Currency conversion and deposit
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You can convert real banknotes into virtual money, and vice versa, using the "administrator" mode of the Anthropia portal. Beware, this is always money in "kashes", not Sabarith Pounds (which are a far more regulated currency).

In the same way, you know how to transfer "standard" gems (small and transparent, handed over in person) to their owner's Anthropia account (but the reverse operation is not possible).

*The deposited banknotes and gems must be put in the dedicated out-of-game cash box.*

Bills of sale
++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You are authorised to carry out auctions, to register property transfers, and more generally all administrative deeds within the land of Sabarim.

For the auctions, be sure to insist on the description of each lot (nature, number, origin, presence of runes in 3D visualisation...), and the mode of sale (ascending or descending auction). Note down the buyers and the sale price as you go along; they will have to be entered on the Anthropia web portal.


Bailiff's deeds
++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Your testimony is worth its weight in gold, and to this day the word of a Tabellion has never been ignored or contradicted in the context of a court case.
Make yourself available free of charge to players who would like you to formally witness something.


Political influence
++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Your son Vik Leydelass has just been elected (at the start of the evening) as consul of Salima.
His success honours you, but young as he is, he remains extremely fond of your good advice, in matters of foreign as well as domestic policy.

You therefore have it at heart to guide his first decisions, so that Sabarim keeps its status as a civilised haven of peace, and so that the sovereignty of its State is not undermined by economic or scientific entities. For there are tensions because of the monopolies on knowledge, the privileges of the notables, the exploitation of the social caste of the "Illiterate"...

You do however have **one principle that you proclaim loud and clear**: you do not lend much of an ear to what the other (future) consuls and their relatives (such as Lydia or her husband) may say, because you know only too well all the subtle political calculations that may lurk behind their most down-to-earth arguments. Only more "neutral" people can make you change your mind on the issues the country is facing.

And beware: as ready as you are for certain situations in the country to change, your own privileges and immunities as a Tabellion are, for you, **non-negotiable**; at a pinch, these exceptional statuses could be modified for the new generations of notables, but that you yourself and your current colleagues keep your entitlements until your death is the very least one can expect. You can cite as an example the "home distillers" who kept their personal right to produce brandy when the law changed on this matter, only the new generations being affected.

In the event of a popular revolt, you are initially rather of the "let the army crush these delinquents in blood" persuasion, but this position may evolve over the course of the debates.




Miscellaneous interactions
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Your briefcase contains documents of interest to the players; it will be deliberately left in a corner where they can handle it discreetly. Its code is your **abbreviated date of birth**: 24/11/68 ({% symbol '24 November' for 'notary_birth_date_day_month' %} {% symbol '1968' for 'notary_birth_date_year' %}).
So be evasive if you are questioned about your age, or your birthday, giving only a small part of the answer...

You protest against the fact that Inspector Shark is taking advantage of a legal loophole to use a signal jammer, thereby preventing mobile telecommunications.

Shark should remain aggressive towards you all evening, because of your interference and your immunities.



Interactions with the Akarith ambassador
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

In the name of Sabarim's "neutrality", Consul {% symbol "Mundlish Odalisc" for "lydia_adversary_name" %} has just decreed that the Akarith ambassador to Sabarim, Archdeacon Menelas, be allowed to take part in Sir Loyd Georges' auctions. Given the hate campaign launched against Loyd Georges by the Akarith politico-religious leaders (the Judicators), and the growing tension in Midolia, you found this decision absurd, but you had to comply.

Since Menelas was not present at the start of the evening, and is certainly aware of the attack suffered by Loyd Georges (perhaps he is even involved in it), you assume he will have had the good taste to refrain from coming tonight. But when he turns up, looking jovial and innocent, you nearly explode with fury; you express your disapproval, but without being able to do much about it.

When Menelas tries to gatecrash the private sale of {{character_properties.lydia.official_name}} (for the tar sands deposit), all you can do is warn the potential future consul against the schemes of this Akarith; ultimately it is up to her to decide whom she accepts at her table.

The ambassador is protected by diplomatic immunity, and therefore beyond the reach of Inspector Shark's judicial requisition; but as sharply as the inspector criticised your own immunity as a tabellion, he seems to readily accept that of the ambassador; you take public offence at this.
