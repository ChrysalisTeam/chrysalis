Sheet of the Akarith Ambassador to Sabarim
===============================================

You are **Archdeacon Menelas**. You are a high-ranking member of the politico-religious order of the Judicators, which controls the land of Akaris; you are also **the official ambassador of your homeland to the land of Sabarim** (Sir Loyd Georges' homeland).

You typically wear a richly ornamented alb, together with a skullcap or a mask on your head.
**You must always wear on your fingers a silver skull ring (originating from the {% symbol "Keroskia highlands" for "akarith_ambassador_skull_ring_origin" %}), and another one with a large rectangular sapphire, both of which you display ostentatiously.** {% hint "akarith_ambassador_rings" is needed %}
You are all smiles, full of bonhomie, of apparent gentleness, of soothing phrases.
You spontaneously go and chat with the guests of the evening, and ignore any reactions of rejection they may have.
You make free use of lies, sophistry, euphemisms, and cruel psychological games.

You are the cornerstone of the Akarith plots concerning the land of Sabarim.
It is thanks to your ambassadorial privileges that the "Purifiers" (anti-desecrator assassins) have infiltrated the country, and have set themselves up in the taverns of the slums; they are the ones who attack desecrators like the famous **{{character_properties.opal.official_name}}**.

You know that Loyd Georges' auctions have attracted secret agents from several countries, including some from Doria (a state which collaborates, willingly or not, with Akaris).

You have also learned that your fellow Judicators (including Archdeacon Baazel in Akaris) have managed to turn a "leading figure" of this affair to their advantage; but you asked to be kept out of the details of this operation, so as not to risk the slightest leak, because lately you know yourself to be ever more closely watched by your enemies.

You arrive at the evening well after Loyd Georges' guests, just before the start of the various sales. <{ sabarim_notable_has_no_body_search }/>

If you are led to speak on these subjects:

- you announce that you are here to buy back, in all legality, the artefacts that were nonetheless "stolen" by Loyd Georges from an Akarith temple; you gloss over the fact that it was a heretic temple, and on the contrary claim a "spiritual filiation" with the ancient Yodic denomination that used this buried temple.
- you claim to be learning only this very moment that Loyd Georges has been attacked, "otherwise you would of course not have come, but now that you are here..."
- it is absolutely normal, in your eyes, that you enjoy your ambassadorial prerogatives, such as being exempt from security searches, diplomatic immunity, etc.
- you assert that the unrest in Midolia is merely the expression of the latent desire of neighbouring peoples to join the glory of the Holy Akarith Empire; you are a fervent advocate of Transfiliation, i.e. the idea (controversial among Akariths) that the unbelieving peoples may join the chosen people of the Yods, the Akarith people, instead of having to be destroyed or enslaved.
- you say that the death threats uttered by Akariths against Loyd Georges have been exaggerated, or badly translated, and are merely the expression of legitimate indignation (and you repeat that foreign journalists constantly lie about Akaris)


Miscellaneous interactions:

- if you run into {{character_properties.opal.official_name}}, you appear moved by his pitiful physical state, and offer to bless him in the name of the Yods "even though he is an unbeliever"
- you offer culinary specialities you have brought along (in a magnificent silver bowl and a skull-headed bottle), laugh at the fear this inspires, and serenely eat/drink some yourself {% hint "akarith_ambassador_food_offering" is needed %}



MISSION 1: the tar sands deposits
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You try to gatecrash Lydia's private sale, despite the Tabellion's protests. If she refuses, you try to at least get an interview with her afterwards.

Your goal is to make her sign a (non-exclusive) negotiating contract, attesting that she is interested in selling her deposit to Akaris for a large sum, and will therefore not sell it to anyone else for a certain period of time. This serves a double purpose: to have a chance of getting hold of this very interesting piece of land, but above all to compromise Lydia in the eyes of Sabarith public opinion, so as to make her give up the post of consul in favour of {% symbol "Mundlish Odalisc" for "lydia_adversary_name" %} (whom the Akariths already manipulate easily through blackmail).

Thoroughly reassure Lydia that "business is business", that "this commits her to nothing", and that in any case it is in her interest to think carefully before making her decision.

If this negotiating contract is signed, pass a copy of it, via the mediator, to Cynthia (to see whether she makes a scoop of it), and in any case the Press soon learns of it and goes wild about the traitor Lydia, to the benefit of her rival in the consular elections.




MISSION 2: the auctions
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You have at your disposal a capital in Sabarith Pounds, intended to sow trouble in the auctions, to gain negotiating leverage over certain guests by snatching away the lots they covet, and why not get hold of artefacts that would allow you to get closer to Loyd Georges' Orb.

You therefore apply a little pressure to **force the guests to outbid one another in the ascending auctions**, as long as the nominal value of the lot has not been reached.

In the case of descending auctions, you watch the guests' reactions, and buy the lot if the amount becomes really attractive.
OUT-OF-GAME CONSTRAINT: your character unintentionally leaves the players a few seconds to react, by telegraphing his "I'll take it" gesture too early.

You show a certain irritation when a bidder thwarts your purchasing plans during these auctions.
You also become inquisitive when a bidder seems interested in a lot that is not naturally within his remit (especially if he is a Sabarith citizen).

**YOUR GOAL**: with only **600 Sabarith Pounds**, manage to buy ONE of the lots put up for auction,

Mission 3: discord and helping hands
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

After the auctions, withdraw with one of the **Masslavians** in a very conspicuous manner, in the style of "come, my dear fellow, we need to talk about our little business"; then feed him some pointless waffle (e.g. by starting to tell him a childhood memory); then end the conversation, making it quite clear that "this way, your fellows are going to be asking themselves a lot of questions about you".

Withdraw more discreetly with one of the **Dorians**, to wish him that all goes for the best in the rapprochement between your two peoples, and hand him **100 kashes** "for whatever purpose it may serve".



Mission 4: a beautiful death
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Several guests will receive orders to assassinate you, in a more or less "natural" way.

Some will challenge you to a duel to obtain one of your rings. You are initially reluctant, but faced with the urgings of the household staff, you finally accept, including if further duels are proposed to you.

Either you delegate to old Alphonse the task of representing you (he announces that he is a veteran of this kind of duel, but refuses to give his win rate), or you joust yourself if Alphonse has already lost a duel.

If a guest brings you food or drink, you are suspicious.
If it is a servant (especially Alphonse), you accept it without any problem.

If a guest warns you of an assassination attempt, pointing out the culprits or the means, you redouble your caution and do everything you can not to fall for it (check with the Game Master if there is any uncertainty about how to proceed).
