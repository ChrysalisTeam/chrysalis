

Sheet of the servant Alphonse
===============================

*This NPC is very important to the game, not only because of the information and items he constantly hands out, but also because of the strong dose of humour and absurdity he brings to the evening, to the players' great delight.*


Your background
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You are Alphonse, the ageing but larger-than-life servant of Sir Loyd Georges.

You have followed your master and friend throughout his globetrotting life (while keeping away from the dangerous operations), right up to his recent expedition to Akaris.
You did not take part in the tumultuous excavation of the buried temple, but you were able to see that the crates of artefacts brought up from it contained magnificent gems and artefacts. Not to mention a "strange orb" that Loyd Georges has kept under wraps.

Since then, the household has suffered numerous attacks, most of which you only noticed when it was too late to do anything about them. That is the advantage of being often distracted: you spare yourself moments of great anguish.

But with the attack on Loyd Georges, you find yourself in a situation of great uncertainty: what will become of the debt-ridden manor, and of your old carcass? Who will be able to ensure your safety, and give you a future if things go wrong? Even more than Loyd Georges' failing health, or the search for the criminal, this is the subject that will torment you during the evening, and guide most of your actions.



Your very peculiar "way of being"
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You are an unforgettable character.

Always out of step in your activities, always behind in understanding what is going on around you, often away with the fairies, you are a source of uninterrupted amusement for the guests, and of intermittent irritation for Loyd Georges' household.

You are in the habit of:

- talking for the sake of talking, or even delivering monologues on very specific subjects (astronomy, oyster farming...)
- divulging confidential information (unwittingly) as soon as someone coaxes you a little, but jealously guarding "secrets" such as the recipe for your favourite cocktail
- neglecting crucial issues such as the security of the manor, but making a scene over trifles (such as the proper arrangement of the cutlery at table...)
- delivering "punch lines" (shock phrases) while remaining deadpan, great philosophical quotations in a learned tone, unintentionally ambiguous turns of phrase, mangling words ("succulate these dishes for me", "I am emotioned", "solutionate a problematic"...)
- being grandiloquent, farcical, when it comes to praising "SIR Loyd Georges" or the History of Sabarim
- showing great respect for Sabarith traditions (such as the national anthem, duels of honour...), but snubbing the country's notables to the point of insolence
- wallowing in self-pity to get help
- overflowing with affection for your dogs or your dear little Cynthia Loakim
- acting the braggart at the mention of danger, but overflowing with cowardice as soon as it is actually present
- very serenely accepting tasks far too complex for your meagre IQ, then looking for help in all directions when you get stuck on them
- overestimating your talent in any art whatsoever; if the moment is right, your vocalising or your improvised poems will astound even the most phlegmatic of audiences
- having unexpected memory lapses, even when the context should immediately remind you, for example, "why you came into the guests' lounge with a tray full of petits fours"
- indulging your pronounced taste for alcohol and gambling; you often haunt the taverns of the Salima slums, systematically leaving most of your wages there, which has recently earned you a few problems with unpaid debts

More than that: you adore duels of honour. If the challenged party tries to avoid a duel, you insist, in the name of his "honour", that he accept it, even if he is within his rights (e.g. if he has already been challenged during the evening). If another referee can replace you, you even volunteer to defend the honour of anyone who is challenged, even if it were an Akarith; you claim to be a "veteran" at this activity, carefully avoiding any mention of your win rate; and you always lose, of course.


Miscellaneous interactions
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You bellow out with emphasis the "in-game" hours as they pass: "Ladies and gentlemen, it is 20:00 and all is well".
You also loudly announce the arrival of the various dishes and drinks at the buffet.

The players will have multiple ways of making use of your services, whether win-win (with your little private sales, or palm-greasing to get extras...), or by fleecing you: blackmail, manipulation of your naivety, exploitation of your taste for alcohol and gambling, pilfering of items...

When Cynthia and Waden arrive at the manor, you and Rydji are flabbergasted: Loyd Georges had always wished to keep them away from these auctions and their procession of shady characters. How come they were invited? {% fact "alphonse_and_rydji_are_surprised_by_cynthia_waden_presence" as author %}

Like most people, you are a little afraid of Inspector Shark, so you always go through his **deputy Obsidian** to pass on letters, items or information to him.

Towards the Tabellion of Salima, you are rather indifferent; you think that "this kind of upstart notable cannot hold a candle to true nobility like that of Loyd Georges".

When the Akarith Ambassador turns up at the manor, like the whole household, you do not hide your indignation. However you have an *irrepressible* attraction to the dishes he brings, which poses you a crisis of conscience; until you crack and stuff yourself with them.

You are the only one who knows how to operate an **antique landline fax-phone (with blurry video)** present in the manor. You take advantage of this to charge for its use: **50 kashes per call, or 150 kashes for unlimited use by one person**.

Private sales
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

To pay off your gambling debts, you sell a few of your finds to the highest bidder. **See the detailed list in the dedicated document.**

You conduct this sale with the patter of a fairground hawker, extolling each lot as if it were a legendary artefact, and inventing for yourself an aura of great modern-day adventurer, whose possessions are already relics.

This is not an auction: the first buyer to come forward gets the lot at the price fixed in advance, unless two buyers fight over it (in which case you take the highest bidder).

You do not discriminate between buyers, except for the Akarith Ambassador, to whom you refuse - as far as possible - to speak.

Cynthia (and she alone) can get a **50% discount**, on 1 single lot that you sell, if she begs you for it.

About Loyd Georges' first pet
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You do not quite remember, but it seems to you that as a small child, he had a **cat**.
You point out that, being the History and Mythology enthusiast that he is, Loyd Georges has always given his animals the **names of deities from ancient civilisations**.


About the Tabellion's date of birth
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

If you are asked the question, you no longer remember which day/month he was born, but you remember the year, the same as yours give or take 20 years: **Year {% symbol '1968' for 'notary_birth_date_year' %} of the new era**.
