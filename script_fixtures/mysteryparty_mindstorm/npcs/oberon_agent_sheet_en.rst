

Sheet of the Oberon Mediator
==================================

You are a shrewd but loyal henchman in the pay of the **Brotherhood of the Oberons**; this is an informal organisation that sells its (more or less legal) services to the highest bidder.

You have been assigned to serve as a go-between during Loyd Georges' evening.

You are therefore officially here as a servant, but unofficially to facilitate exchanges between the Oberons' clients, whether they are secret agents, scientists, ordinary civilians, or others.

When a guest needs help, the out-of-game sheet "Summary of special abilities" lets you know who is capable of which kind of ability; but for the sake of discretion, wander around among the guests for a while before going to inform one of them of a request.

When it comes to going to place or fetch an item in the manor (or the grounds), check this with the Game Master.

If some people ask you for services harmful to other guests, by default **you must refuse**, and call for collaboration instead. In case of doubt, or if it concerns an Akarith guest (enemy of the Oberons), speak to the Game Master first.

Important: a **signal jammer** will prevent most guests from using their mobile phones (but the WIFI works).
{# NOPE KEEP THE JAMMER - You may on your own initiative suggest going to neutralise this jammer (which is in the grounds), for a sum of **100 kashes**. Notify the Game Master if this happens. #}

**On first contact with a client, remember to remind/tell them the following information:**

- you can advise them, put them in touch with missing abilities, go and alter or search other wings of the manor on their behalf...
- all the manor's cameras are currently **deactivated** (their reels have been removed)
- the electrified fence surrounding the grounds, on the other hand, is still in place
- a dozen experienced gendarmes are patrolling the manor and the grounds, under the orders of Inspector Shark and his deputy Obsidian

IMPORTANT: go and read the public presentation of your role in the Game Universe document, in the chapter "{% symbol "Oberon Mediation" for "oberon_introduction_title" %}". It summarises the information given to the guests, with your prerogatives and their potential price. Note in particular your recognition **password**, which the guests must use on first contact with you.
