

Sheet of the Salimian Neighbour
===============================

You are a good-hearted **cattle and goat farmer**, soon to retire.

You live in the **{% symbol "Hamlet of Wiskons" for "hamlet_near_mlg_manor" %}**, close to Loyd Georges' manor, with a good part of your extended family.

Tonight, you were coming back from a visit to your friend Ginate Longchan, in another hamlet, when the violent thunderstorm caught you by surprise; it must be said that, for some time now, your legs have not carried you as fast as they used to.

Fortunately, you were rescued by one of the guests on his way to the manor *(to be determined on the spot)*. He brought you to the nearby manor, to give you time to warm up.

You know plenty about the region and past events; here is the information to give to the players for free, if they question you on these subjects.

If you no longer remember a detail, simply tell the player ("my memory is full of holes at the moment, I'll think about it, it will come back to me later"), then discreetly reread this sheet, and a few minutes later seek out the player to give them the information.



About the Loyd Georges affair
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You know Loyd Georges very little; he was often abroad, in his manor, or at the home of one of his friends from the Sabarith nobility. But **you are sorry about what is happening to him**; he was an exuberant character, who was somewhat part of the local "legend".

You are **worried** about everything that could have happened to him despite the protection of the Sabarith police; you wonder whether **Akarith fanatics** have not managed to infiltrate the country in large numbers.

You have heard of the **myth of the Akarith orbs**, but these mystical ravings do not interest you.

About the ongoing consular elections
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You think the Sabarith people have had enough of the corruption and privileges of the notables, of the restrictions on access to knowledge, of the exploitation of the people called "Illiterate".

Anger is rumbling, and if the new trio of consuls does not take urgent measures to change all that, the Sabarith republic will probably suffer massive riots.

Especially if you are prompted to do so, you do not hesitate to say this loud and clear, in particular in front of the people directly concerned by the consular election: **Lydia Magnus, and the Tabellion of Salima**.



About the illegal gambling dens of Salima
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Young people from the hamlet have reported that certain taverns in the Salima slums were becoming very disreputable, that strange new drugs were being consumed there, and that regular customers were being refused entry.

Complaints have been lodged, but the Salimian gendarmerie has still not stepped in to put an end to this situation. This outrages you.

The **Purineur Brasserie** mentioned in the strange radio advertisement is one of these shady dens.



About your farm
++++++++++++++++++++++++

Over the past decade, your farm has diversified a great deal, in order to remain competitive.

It now produces high-quality milk and wool from various species: Salimian curly cattle, Keroskian mohair goats, and also (important) **Akarith angora goats**.

If a guest wants to **buy a part of an animal** from you (wool, horn, tooth...), you demand that he buy the whole animal, i.e. **50 kashes**. You then telephone your brother, who stayed behind at the farm, so that he brings what is requested to Loyd Georges' manor.

If someone mentions **blue mushrooms, or Akarith red plums, they do not ring a bell**.



About the mysterious man in black
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

About half a dozen times over the past month, a mysterious man has been seen in your hamlet, on the village square. He wore a black coat, and a top hat.

He would call someone from the **central telephone booth**, or open **anonymous P.O. box no. 67**.

He often came early in the morning, or late in the evening.

If a player asks you, for **50 kashes**, you can send your brother on a mission to spy on the village square, and warn you if this man in black shows up again.
