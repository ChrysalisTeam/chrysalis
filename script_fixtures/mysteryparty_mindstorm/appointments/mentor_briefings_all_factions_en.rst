Guests' phone check-ins with their mentors
##############################################################


*Keep the summary sheets of the characters concerned at hand, in order to have a clear picture of their backgrounds, their missions and their means of action.*


Initial briefing (before the game starts)
==========================================

The secret agents get called by their respective superiors (in pairs, except Obsidian):

- ask them whether they have all their "missions" in mind, and whether these are clear (list them)
- ask them what their plans are for the beginning of the evening
- remind them that they must play it "united", and not fall into the hands of Sabarith justice

Lydia and Magnus get called by their estate manager, who asks whether he can help them in any way. He enquires about Opal's health, about Lydia's electoral prospects, and more generally about what they intend to do given the circumstances.

Waden and Cynthia get called by Emilos Loakim, who is on his tumultuous excavation site on the continent of Keroskia; he wonders why Loyd Georges has invited them to this evening, and urges them to be wary.

Obsidian is asked "what is this business about a confiscated service weapon"; his superiors recommend that he discreetly get hold of a weapon, because "you never know what can happen at this kind of evening"


Mid-game briefing
================================

*It takes place halfway through the game, before the conclusion of the Akarith council, but after its first leanings have been disclosed over the radio.*

Same set-up for the secret agents, whereas the 4 civilians (Cynthia, Waden, Opal and Lydia) all get called together by Emilos Loakim.

Take stock of their progress in the various missions, the friends and enemies identified, the sticking points encountered...

Also go over the notable events that have taken place in the manor (e.g. as a result of other protagonists' quests).

Beware, **NO possibility of sending money or gems**: all available resources are already committed, and administrative delays make it impossible in the short term anyway.

Importantly, remind the non-Sabarith agents that the assault on Loyd Georges is a **secondary incident**, and that their missions (including the search for the Orb) remain the priority.


Information for the Lordanians (Amethyst and Garnet)
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

If the players have not yet provided the information, ask them whether they have any information on the balance of military forces in Midolia.
Adjust the instructions (for or against the Midolian war) according to the strategic information they bring, and the possible decisions of the Akarith council.

In any case, the agents now have a mandate to negotiate, with the future consuls, a military cooperation agreement between Lordania and Sabarim: **give them the pact template on paper** (via the fax).


Information for the Masslavians (Malachite and Spinel)
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Obsidian is said to belong to the Sabarith secret services; he seems to be concerned above all with national security, so try to form an alliance with him.

The Lordanians' intentions are more or less clear, but if they too want to prevent the creation of an Akarith empire, an attempt must be made to pool the information about the Orb with them, so as to put it out of the Akariths' reach (and thus prevent their "prophecies" from galvanising them).


Information for the Dorians (Topaz and Peridot)
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Take stock of the first leanings that have leaked from the Akarith council, and remind them that everything is being decided now, it is now or never to tip it doctrinally one way or the other; sketch out the possible solutions for Doria (rallying or rebellion), depending on the case.


Information for Obsidian
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Sit-ins and mini-riots are taking place at this very moment in many cities of Sabarim; the Press is being pressured not to report on them, but the situation is serious.


Information for the 4 civilians
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

For Opal: what about the Berserk Elixir? In theory the old cook Robb Barrow is at the manor and will know how to prepare it according to the instructions he is given; find out where this fellow is.
