Faction - The Diakons of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}
==============================================

You are three Diakons of the god {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}, the great cosmic dragon, blue and invisible, just and generous.
One of you specialises in the bewitching (and dispelling) of objects; another in exorcisms; the last in invocation rituals.
But all of you are **{% symbol "initiates" for "ritualist_kind_name" %}**, which means you can officiate at a sacred ritual combining talismans, incantatory formulas, sacred gestures, lights, smoke, and other mystical signs.

{% symbol "500 years" for "library_initial_events_timedelta" %} ago, your order arrived in the region of {% symbol "Thelassar" for "events_region_name" %}, under the colonising leadership of **{% symbol "Cardinal Tridentes" for "old_guru_name" %}**. It turned out that this religious leader was less concerned with virtue and prayer than with power and money; he thus committed a number of highly questionable acts, such as the sacking of the manor of the Maupertuis, an ancestral lineage of healer-mages who were overshadowing him. But at least the cult of the god {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %} took firm root in the region, as shown by the influence, brighter than ever, of the **{% symbol "Rochesombre Monastery" for "common_monastery_name" %}** to which you belong (a monastery that formerly belonged to the Yodic cult). {% fact "bahamoot_monastery_belonged_to_yodic_order_in_the_past" %}

However, a few days ago, the monastery's oracle received mystical visions, warning her that a beast drunk with rage, and souls in torment, were held prisoner on the outskirts of the Maupertuis manor. Abnormal interferences with parallel worlds were also sensed in those surroundings. Your small team therefore went there without delay, in order to restore the order of things.

You were soon joined by a **renegade Djinn** (cut off from his community), who showed an interest in your quest. By combining invocations and prayers, you gave him an immaterial body for a few weeks, so that he could assist you with his knowledge. But your joint investigations yielded nothing.
