Your abilities
====================================

Ever since you were little, reading and abstract sciences have bored you, but you come alive in the great outdoors and in intense physical activity. That is why you naturally became a "Woodsman", a prestigious title that encompasses the activities of gamekeeper, lumberjack-gardener and monster tracker.


Herculean Strength
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You are strong. But really, really strong. This allows you to lift entire tree trunks on your own, and more generally to carry objects considered **ultra-heavy** by ordinary mortals.

*Out-of-game note: Such objects will be marked in the game by a label, their actual weight of course remaining low. Simulate your efforts and your breathlessness when moving "ultra-heavy" objects.*

Some of these objects are relatively small in size, but are weighed down by their magical link to other places.

*Out-of-game note: If you find a wooden and red-cloth "collection pouch", also ultra-heavy, bring it to the Game Master so that he can show you how to use it.*  {% hint 'thief_heavy_lost_moneybox' is provided %}


Mini Crossbow
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You never part with your dear handheld weapon, which has already saved your skin more than once when night fell in the woods.

Its darts are made of solid **{% symbol "Iridium" for "parcival_woodsman_arrows_metal" %}**, so they are effective even against animal shells or light armour. But they have received no enchantments allowing them to pierce magical defences, or to injure entities of the world of the dead. Luckily for you, the case has never arisen. Against machines, these precise darts are very ineffective, unless they hit their vital systems; explosives would be more appropriate.

Defensive Strategist
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Your parents never hid it from you: if there is one person in the family who will always pull through, it is you.
But your Achilles' heel is the people in your circle of family and friends. You have therefore made a habit of keeping an eye out for potential perils, and of spotting the strengths and weaknesses of the terrain you find yourself on.

Is there a dead-end to avoid so as not to end up cornered by enemies? Or, on the contrary, a nook in which to hide, or even to avoid being surrounded? Paths that seem booby-trapped? You know you cannot be everywhere, but at the very least you want to be able to stand between the dangers and your most vulnerable loved ones.


Knowledge of dangerous beasts
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You could write a novel about all the nasty things you have come across in the undergrowth, rivers or caves of your native land.

Vampires are, in your opinion, greatly exaggerated dangers; whether in human or animal form, a blow from a club quickly teaches them good manners; their fear of light makes them easy to repel, and their bite is actually far more painful than toxic.

You have, on the other hand, had to flee more than once from their distant cousins, the cave ghouls. With their long claws and their strong resistance to magic, they are creatures too dangerous to be fought by ordinary mortals. You do know, however, that some peasants set sophisticated traps for them, taking advantage of their innate addiction to *{% symbol "moon stones" for "phosphorescent_pebbles_name" %}*, a kind of phosphorescent pebble, as well as of their severe short-sightedness. {% fact "beast_is_addicted_to_moon_stones" %} {% fact "characters_know_how_the_beast_works_regarding_3m_sight" %}

You have only ever come across one werewolf, as he was emerging from one of his nocturnal transformations. The good care of the village shamans made it possible to quickly cure this person, which is fortunate because the strength of a fully enraged lycanthrope easily exceeds your own. Luckily, you know them to be very - even overly - sensitive to strong smells (rotten eggs, carrion...), which can disrupt their fighting ability.

Wendigos, emaciated humanoids fond of human flesh, are very rare on Pangea. You fought two of them in your hovel, in the dead of winter, and luckily the traditional recipes worked: a good {% symbol "Iridium" for "parcival_woodsman_arrows_metal" %} dart in the heart of one, and a good plunge into the hearth fire for the other.

A creature you have never seen, and wish never to see, is without a doubt the thestral. This equine monster, skeletal and winged, moves swiftly between the trees, and is said to attack lone walkers violently, out of pure sadistic pleasure. So you always take care to hide when you hear a shrill yelp in the distance. Especially since it is known to have no particular vulnerability, other than a sufficient number of axe blows to its scrawny body.

You have very little knowledge of necromancy, and no counter-measures in that field; but you do know, for instance, that unlike phantoms - immaterial and therefore persistent - zombies and other undead are only temporary, and draw from the magic of their invokers, or of their tomb, what they need to animate their flesh for a time; fleeing and then waiting is therefore often the best solution. {% fact "phantoms_are_persistent_but_zombies_are_transient" %}

As for strixes, dryads, spriggans and other magic-loving creatures, you know little about them, and have always taken care to seek the help of experts when the case arose; those beasts are fortunately only dangerous when they are starving or injured.

Finally, you have heard sickening stories of peaceful woodland beasts parasitised by fungi, climbing plants, or marsh molluscs. The resulting chimera then posed a danger that was hard to predict. It is even said that peaceful agricultural machinery has already "come to life" in this way. But on these subjects, legend probably exceeds reality.
