Recognising artefact bewitchments
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

By laying your hands on an object and closing your eyes, you can sense whether it has been the target of curses or blessings.

*Out-of-game notes*: An element that can be analysed by your sensitivity bears a **gold sticker star**.  {% fact "sticker_gold_star_means_magical_analysis" %}

This sticker dot may be accompanied by various geometric symbols, typically on a white label next to it.

If it contains a **triangle**, then the object is cursed and harmful; otherwise, it is bewitched too, but in a neutral or even beneficial way (in particular, this is the case if no geometric symbol is present). Beware, an object bewitched in a neutral/beneficial way may still turn out to be **dangerous** (e.g. if it is a blessed weapon), but it will not be "booby-trapped" for whoever touches it.

By default, a bewitchment that is present is of "simple" level.
If there is a small **circle** anywhere in the sticker dot, then the bewitchment is of "complex" level.
If this circle additionally contains a **single dot**, then the bewitchment rises to "legendary" level.
In the absence of geometric symbols, you cannot determine the power of the bewitchment involved.

A gold star may also be accompanied by a **3-letter code**; enter this code into the {% symbol "knowledge portal (available on site)" for "hint_website_name" %}, in the **"{% symbol "magic" for "bewitchment_portal_hint_category" %}" category**, to learn more about the object in question.  {% hint "hint_website_device" is needed %}

You must of course act out your laying on of hands theatrically, and not explain this out-of-game signage with the gold sticker dot to anyone.


Removing negative bewitchments
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

**Tell the Game Master about the dispellings you wish to carry out.**

If the recognised bewitchment is "negative", it is liable to injure the person who touches it, not on the spiritual level but on the biological one. {% fact "cursed_objects_give_magic_and_not_spiritual_injuries" %}

You may therefore wish to dispel this bewitchment through a ritual.

Neutral/positive enchantments, for their part, cannot be removed in this way.


Simple bewitchment
-----------------------------

A "simple" level curse requires you to call upon the help of any "initiate" (probably a fellow Diakon) to dispel it.

You must both lay your hands above the object, and you (alone) must loudly pronounce the incantation: **"By the sacred breath of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}, O inert matter, we sever the bonds that bind you to the bewitched strata".**

If successful, the Game Master removes/masks the gold sticker dot from the object.

Complex bewitchment
-----------------------------

If the bewitchment is of "complex" level, then at least **three {% symbol "initiates" for "ritualist_kind_name" %}** are needed to perform the dispelling.

You must then, in this order:

- Call upon the censing talents of your fellow Diakon (walk 3 times around the object in procession, with the censer)
- Then all stand in a semicircle (or a circle if possible) around the object
- Then you (alone) pronounce the incantation: **"Open wide your ears, open wide your eyes, for by the pacifying power of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}, behold, we break the unholy chains that imprison this matter!"**

If successful, the Game Master once again removes/masks the gold sticker dot from the object.

Legendary bewitchment
-----------------------------

If the bewitchment is of "legendary" level, then it is **impossible** for you to remove it, except by finding a counter-measure specific to this spell. But the game is not always worth the candle, as some bewitched artefacts are simply **traps** meant for intruders, which will only vanish once triggered by some poor victim.
