Your abilities
====================================

From your earliest youth, you realised that you could see and hear things that no one else seemed to perceive.
A blessing or a curse? In any case, your grand lineage bequeathed you this gift of clairvoyance, which has never left you.

Your oracles are often very enigmatic, but with the help of your relatives, you always manage to make sense of them. You thus sensed, well before everyone else, the coming of wars and famines in the surrounding countries, the best times to sow cereals, and even - a terrible moment - your little sister's illness.

As you enter the buried library of the Maupertuis, all your senses are on alert, so as to warn your relatives, through these mysterious revelations, of the windfalls and dangers it holds.


The occurrence of revelations
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Sometimes, prophecies fall upon you without warning. But far more often, they occur when you meet people who are also accustomed to prophetic influences. Your senses then enter into mutual resonance, yielding prophecies that are either more precise, or simply shared between those present.

You can also trigger revelations by touching objects with a heavily charged past.

*Out-of-game note*: These revelation triggers are materialised by **coloured feathers**; announce them to another clairvoyant person or to the GM when you find some. {% fact "oracle_revelations_are_triggered_by_colored_feathers" %}

You have always dreamed of coming across a **mother-of-pearl unicorn, or an incense fountain, or a five-leaf clover**. Such encounters mean the coming of particularly vivid prophecies, from the depths of the invisible world; provided, at least, that the seer knows how to activate such artefacts, which will lose their power as soon as their role is fulfilled. The Alchemist of your group can help on this matter. {% fact "incense_fountain_can_deliver_powerful_prophecy" %} {% hint "incense_fountain" is needed %} {% fact "parcival_alchemist_can_create_incense_cone" %}

Certain exceptional prophecies appear and address those present by themselves. You have heard that some old paintings, once covered with a dose of **Lotion of Revelation**, have delivered such wonders.

Revelations of the "visual" type
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

In this scenario, images appear before your eyes for several seconds. You must then grasp as many details as possible, before this vision disappears forever. Then you go into a trance, to describe it all to your relatives.


Revelations of the "auditory" type
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

In this case, you hear a dialogue or some other narrative unfolding in your head, and you must then proclaim it as it comes, without paying attention to it, so that the people for whom these messages are intended can memorise them.


Out-of-game notes: how revelations unfold
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

{% macro prophecy_workflow_instructions() %}

The aim is to make use of the scenery, lighting effects and soundtracks to create a beautiful mystical atmosphere around these revelations (you will find plants and sound and music projectors on the premises).

Thus showcased, you will be able to act out in a somewhat theatrical way the prophecies that come to you - in the manner of the Oracles of the ancient "Pythia of Delphi".

The various **available soundtracks** should have been communicated to you along with your character sheet, so that you can practise placing your exclamations between the bursts of percussion.

For **pictorial revelations**: an NPC actor will show you an image, which you will then have to describe in front of the other characters as if it were suddenly appearing to you. You may call on the witnesses of the scene to ask you for details about the points that strike them, and answer them as the vision unfolds.

For **auditory revelations**: an NPC actor will have you listen to the soundtrack once or twice, then you will be able to simulate receiving it in front of the other characters, possibly while keeping an earpiece replaying the soundtrack. In that case, stand in profile so that the audience does not see the earpiece. Do not listen to any potential questions from the audience, so as not to be distracted.

Intuitions
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

At times you have, not enigmatic revelations, but just strong attractions or apprehensions; or else a few sentences that imprint themselves on you.

You then communicate them to your relatives as faithfully as possible, without necessarily going into a trance to do so.

As you take the portal to the Maupertuis Archives, you in fact receive a powerful image in your mind: **several groups of lambs fleeing from wolves, and taking refuge, huddled against one another, in the centre of the forest. A clamour rises: "United we shall stand, divided we shall fall".** *(Out-of-game note: you will have the opportunity to declaim this intuition in front of all the players, at the end of the musical introductory speech delivered by the Game Master.)*
