The Fallen
#########################

*Dragon gods turned into mice, yet at the controls of mechanical androids.*
*Nothing less.*


Your story
=======================

You are dragons. Or rather, what humans call "dragons". The image of a gigantic winged reptile that this conjures up for them is but one of the thousand appearances you could have chosen to appear before them; but this one pleased you aesthetically. In reality, you come from a parallel universe, an otherworld, in which life and the senses behave very differently.

Like a few compatriots thirsting for novelty, you left your native universe thanks to a powerful draconic ritual, to explore other dimensions; you thus reached Pangea, several centuries before the New Era.
Like the Djinns, you wandered through an invisible in-between world, gradually learning how human societies work.
But unlike those pure spirits of knowledge, you decided to interfere with this reality, so as to pass on some of your wisdom.

That is how you became spiritual guides. Then, unbeknownst to you, deities, **{% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %} and Behemoth**.
You counselled bewildered souls, healed the suffering, sometimes brought back to life the sick who were dying; "prophesied", too, whatever your keenness of mind allowed you to foresee about human destiny.
Above all, you taught the ways of righteousness, of fair measure, of harmonious life within a living web where everything is interconnected.
But your work soon outgrew you; your initiates went on performing wonders with their own magical power, without realising that they no longer depended on you; and unscrupulous tyrants tarnished your name in their crusades against other faiths.

One event, however, hastened your withdrawal. You discovered that your native world had been corrupted. Chaotic spirits, "demonic" entities, to use human terms, had found fertile ground in the delicate minds of the dragons. And your wonderful universe, thus entirely manipulated by dark forces, became an unfathomable poison; for itself, but also for any other traveller of the otherworlds who came into contact with it. The mystics gave it a new name: the Abyssal Vortex. And a new coat of arms, instantly taboo: three flames arranged in a triangle.

Your duty became clear to you: to use your rear base in the world of Pangea to reconquer the Abyssal Vortex, and to free your deranged kin. A difficult and dangerous task, but the humans and beasts of this world are, luckily for you, fairly resistant to demonic intrusions; the gesticulations and vociferations of "possessed" beings very quickly get them noticed by exorcist priests.

For several centuries now you have been turning Pangea upside down in search of a solution. Djinns, prophets and spiritualists find themselves quite helpless in the face of your questions. But your most serious leads now point to the Secret Archives of the Maupertuis family. A place buried so deep underground, so mobile, so surrounded by protections, that it takes an inter-world spirit years of wandering each time to find it again.

However, things sped up when the oracle of {% symbol "Rochesombre Monastery" for "common_monastery_name" %}, devoted to your worship, sent you powerful prayers: archaeologists had found traces of the magic portals which, once a year, open the door to the Maupertuis sanctuary. She could feel it: momentous events were soon to take place, which would affect the world of humans as much as that of dragons. And in your current, highly spiritual form, you would be easy prey for the demonic forces.

You then made the most profound decision of your long existence: to **become incarnate**.
To give up your majesty, your power, and your eternity.
And thereby to bind your soul to the psychic robustness of this world, but also to its system of life and death, of flesh and of the divine.

Given the time constraints, the only pregnant creature that lent itself to such an operation was... a mouse.
That is how you became **Pistachio and Cashew**, two young grey mice. And the oracle of the monastery had you carried with all urgency to the Diakons who were about to enter the buried sanctuary of the Maupertuis.


Your strategy
===========================

Fortunately, even as baby mice, you have a plan. Or at least the outline of a plan.

- First, each take control of one of the automatons which - according to ancient Djinn accounts - have been bustling about this underground place for centuries.

- Make on site a mind-strengthening elixir powerful enough to let you withstand an immense demonic invasion (one drop of this brew would suffice, since you are mice).

- Then, find the Spiritual Orb, a legendary artefact capable of opening tunnels into otherworld dimensions; it is most probably located in the Forbidden Domain of the library.

- Connect, thanks to it, to the Abyssal Vortex, and draw all the demonic legions into your minds through the intercession of the Diakons.

- Finally, perform with the Diakons a powerful exorcism, to send these demons back through a million worlds, to the chaotic strata they should never have left.

**You have no grimoire, no alchemical recipe, no precise accounts of such mystical operations. This is an unprecedented mission.**

So you will have to intuit, improvise, try with your fingers crossed. And avoid creating pandemonium in this world while trying to repair your own.
Fortune, it is said, favours the bold, so perhaps it will take pity on your rather suicidal mission.


Your draconic clairvoyance
=======================================

You have retained part of your ability to probe souls.
As soon as you meet them, you thus divine the wishes, specialities and possessions of the other visitors to this sanctuary.

Your last scraps of power
========================================

Now that you are incarnated as mice, your life expectancy is very limited, and you have only a few fractions of your draconic knowledge and magic left. You will therefore have to make good use of them.

**Ritual Innovation**: You have always known it: what gives ceremonies their power is not the sequence of words and gestures, it is the immense faith that the initiates have in their performance, a faith ennobled by your blessing. You are therefore able to teach new rituals of your own invention, which will succeed as long as they comply with the inner workings of the universe, and as long as the initiates performing them have enough psychic strength (potentially aided by potions). *Out-of-game note: this ability can only be used once in total during the game; check with the Game Master if you have an idea for a new ritual, so that they can validate it and oversee its implementation.*

**Reward**: If a human being displays fine qualities, you may reward them by revealing a secret of the place. Here it is: in the Forbidden Domain of the Maupertuis secret archives, there is a small metal well called "useless". It is actually very useful, for if a human lowers **5 grams of diamonds** into it, with the attached bucket, they receive the blessing of the *leprechauns*. This person then gains **triple longevity**: this does not make them more resistant to epidemics or injuries, but they age three times more slowly than their fellows. {% hint "longevity_metal_well" is needed %} {% hint "tiny_metal_scale" is needed %}

**Salvation**: If a human being is dying, all the fallen dragons together can, by mobilising what remains of their draconic energy, restore them in soul and body. *Out-of-game note: this ability can only be used once in total during the game; improvise the ritual with gestures and chants.*

**Herbalism**: You have heard of a plant called "Holistix", which comes in the form of purple bells with sharp edges and pointed tips. It is said to have the property of immensely expanding a being's mental serenity, and could therefore greatly help you in your "psychic demon trap" plan. {% hint "purple_bell_plant_in_vial" is needed %}


Your entry into the game
========================================

*Out-of-game notes*

During the briefings, introduce yourselves to the other players as "automatons"; it is better if they think you are mere NPC actors.

At the start of the evening, you will really be playing automatons. Very plain, old-fashioned clothes can do the trick; a little make-up on the face will be welcome.

You may watch the game's introduction from a distance, then you will go about automaton business in the library (dusting, serving, greeting visitors arriving on the premises...), keeping a very mechanical gait and voice. Do not concern yourselves with the characters already present on site, nor with the phantoms.

When you meet the Diakons of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %} (who wear a purple stole), it is assumed that the mice escape from their satchel, and each go and take control of an automaton, thereby endowing it with an almost human personality.

From then on, you are part of the world of the living of Pangea, so you become vulnerable to everything a human would fear (in particular wandering phantoms), and you are subject to the rules of the {% symbol "Oath of Zarathustra" for "unbreakable_oath_name" %}. You keep a somewhat mechanical demeanour, since your body is made that way.

The rest of the game unfolds normally for you, as a player; it is up to you to make contact with the various players and NPC actors of the game, to see how to help them in their quests, and how to obtain their help for yours.

It is up to you to decide at what point you would like to reveal your draconic origin to the Diakons, or even to everyone present - or not.
