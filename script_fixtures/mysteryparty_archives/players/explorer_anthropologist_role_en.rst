Ritual initiations
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

With a view to getting to know certain tribal societies and certain cults better, from the inside, you have diligently followed their initiatory paths. This makes you the only person in the world to hold such diverse titles:

{% macro mystics_knowledge_article() %}

- **Shaman of the Kamilia Jungle**

  Greeting: *"By the three-headed bird, receive my greeting"*; reply: *"By the headless snake, receive mine"*

  This title attests to your endurance through the trials of fire, water, earth and sky. It allows you to perform ritual sacrifices in honour of the Great Sage, to prepare the Kahflu of ceremonies (the alcoholic tuber juice of banquets), and to read the word of the spirits in river pebbles.

- **Yodic Arkon**

  Greeting: *"I beseech the Yods to enlighten you"*; reply: *"May I be their mirror unto you"*

  This title corresponds to a form of "knighthood blessed by the gods" among the faithful of this religion; it binds you to moral righteousness, perseverance and discernment in your actions; it allows you to comment on the holy texts of the Yodic religion, to sit on the ordinary councils of the Arkons, and to receive the **{% symbol "mission confession" for "arkon_mission_confession" %}** of a fellow Arkon (to do so, he kneels before you, and you keep one hand held in the air above his head). {% fact "arkon_means_blessed_knight" %} You are quite proud of this title, which places you in the line of legendary yet real figures such as Zarathustra, or {% symbol "Valerias" for "arkon_name" %}.

- **Ritualist of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}**; you are one of the **{% symbol "initiates" for "ritualist_kind_name" %}** of this cult, and even though it was a long time ago, you are able to join a mystical ritual to multiply its effect.

Mystic Reminiscences
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

You are able to sense the human emotions and memories anchored in certain powerfully enchanted places; for instance places used for ancient family rituals, tombs, sanctuaries... or even on certain magic artefacts. They appear to your eyes in the form of **short messages**.

*Out-of-game notes*: These words come in the form of *polychromatic codes*, in other words clouds of letters of all colours, on a white sign. To read the truly relevant letters, you will simply need to wear **coloured glasses** (provided by the organisers), which, depending on which eye you close, will or will not mask the useless letters.

Knowledge of coats of arms
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

The {% symbol "Magus Mos Peratys" for "maupertuis_dynasty_founder" %}, founder of the Maupertuis dynasty, had adopted towards the end of his life a coat of arms showing a **golden dragon on a black field**.{% fact "coat_of_arms_of_mos_peraty_is_golden_dragon" %} His descendants quickly moved away from it in favour of a return to the **caduceus** (a staff topped with two wings and encircled by two intertwined snakes). {% fact "coat_of_arms_of_maupertuis_is_caduceus" %}
The lineages that branched off from the Maupertuis family have often retained part of this medical symbolism. {% fact "coat_of_arms_of_parcival_is_partial_caduceus" %}

By decision of the International Order of Heraldry, it was for a long time forbidden to create a coat of arms oneself, even for a work of fiction. However, following the cruel and shameful ways of certain royal families, their coats of arms, as well as the symbols that featured on them, were banished from the symbolism of the living long before the {% symbol "Planetary Cataclysm" for "catastrophic_period" %}. It was therefore argued that these "taboo" symbols could be used in works of fiction, thereby clearly separating real coats of arms from those meant for entertainment.

The symbols thus reserved for works of the imagination are: **the hedgehog, the crane bird, the goat, the pangolin, the sickle, the whip, the stool and the holly leaf**. {% fact "sickle_and_holly_emblems_are_banned_in_reality" %}

One last coat of arms, finally, has been banned since time immemorial, across the entire planet: the **"flame triad"**, that is, three fires on a light field. You had the greatest difficulty breaking the taboo surrounding this coat of arms - every work touching on the subject had been censored, with entire chapters torn out. One of your friends, a sorcerer from the silver mountains, nonetheless gave you a scrap of an answer: this coat of arms belonged to a sect of cultists who had sought to enter an infernal cosmic dimension. He too advised you not to dig into this subject. {% fact "anthropologist_knows_about_fire_triad_emblem" %}
