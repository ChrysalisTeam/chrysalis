Your abilities
====================================

You spent the evenings of your childhood deciphering the few esoteric books, inherited from your lineage, that had escaped the ravages of time. And later on, as a perfect autodidact, you never missed an opportunity to find some dusty grimoire or some herbalist willing to teach you more.

You are therefore starting to acquire a certain skill in making potions, not medicinal ones - despite the tradition of your forebears - but rather "surprising" ones. You still have a long way to go, however, so hard is it to gather the ingredients, the utensils, and - above all - the recipes of this mysterious art.

*Out-of-game note: Ingredients and utensils that are easy to find in our real world are assumed to be just as accessible in the world of Pangea.*

The recipes you know
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++


Pestilence Potion
--------------------------

By mixing essence of sulphur with red Fungal juice, you can obtain a liquid that spreads a pestilential smell over 10 paces in diameter. {% hint 'potion_red_fungal' is needed %}


Anti-Philosopher's Stone
--------------------------

Coat a fluorite gem with tarantula lymph. The stone thus created makes it possible to turn small gold objects into lead, by mere contact. {% hint "gem_fluorine" is needed %}


Potion of Authority
-----------------------

{% hint "recipe_authority_potion" is provided %}

The "Tongue-Loosening Potion" that many alchemists know is actually of little interest; wine achieves equally good chattering effects. {% hint "potion_tongue_untier" is needed %}

But if a Chrysocolla stone is dissolved in it, the resulting potion gives a boost of energy and charisma that makes it much easier to command other humans, animals, and even (if one is not in a hurry) plants. {% hint "gem_chrysocolle" is needed %}


Prophetic Incense
-----------------------

You would like to help the prophetic talents of your relative the Oracle, and certain sacred places of revelation require a very particular incense cone to be burned for the mysticism to activate.

You know that a "tiger's eye" and an "aventurine" (ingredients you are not familiar with) must be melted in a cauldron, with castor oil, to obtain this incense once the mixture has cooled.

Potion of Invulnerability
------------------------------------

This elixir has been sought after or jealously guarded for centuries by armies the world over.

It is said to grant, for a short period - less than 10 minutes - total invulnerability to the person thus enchanted, against any type of injury whatsoever.

You do not know the exact method of preparation, but you have managed to gather the more or less complete list of ingredients:

- a unicorn tail
- a dwarf wyvern scale
- a triceratops eye
- a cardanium crystal
- three edelweiss leaves
- an ash-grey cockroach kidney
