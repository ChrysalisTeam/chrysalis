How the different worlds work
=============================================

Fundamental principles
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

**In short, the worlds of the phantoms (including the Beast) and of the avatars are completely separate: they can neither see nor sense each other, nor hear one another, and it is important to simulate these gaps realistically in every interaction. On the other hand, both of these worlds can see and hear the living (the players), as well as the automatons (the servants). The automatons see everyone except the phantoms; and everyone can see and be seen by the Ankou.** {% fact "phantom_and_avatar_worlds_are_disjoint" %}

This behaviour of the different worlds comes as a surprise to all the NPC actors (except, at a stretch, the Arkon, who had some inkling of it); they must therefore be intrigued when the players "talk to thin air", then gradually come to understand how all this works, and realise (for the phantoms) why the automatons sometimes start moving on their own, why certain objects from the world of the living float about in mid-air, and so on.

Occasionally, the NPC actors may communicate from one world to the other through the players, to greet each other, pass on information, etc.

Advice for the NPC actors
=============================================

Reminder of the fundamental principle
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Stay focused on this point: the worlds of the phantoms and of the avatars are hermetically sealed from each other. The avatars cannot see/hear/sense the phantoms (nor the Beast), and vice versa. On the other hand, all of them can see/hear the living and the automatons; the latter, being mechanical, cannot see the phantoms. And all of them can see/hear the Ankou (who belongs to the "divine" world), as well as the renegade Djinn (the Game Master), who has been made visible (but immaterial) by the Diakons. {% fact "phantom_and_avatar_worlds_are_disjoint" %}

The phantoms can be recognised by their **purple tunic**, and the automatons by their **{% symbol "white ribbons" for "automaton_symbolic_cloth" %}**. The avatars have no particular distinguishing sign, just a costume matching their novel character.


Advice for the scripted speeches
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Some NPC actors have pre-written speeches, accompanied by music, for important events of the game.

It is important to learn and rehearse these speeches several times, on several consecutive days, so as to be thoroughly "steeped" in them when the moment comes, since they are to be declaimed without notes.

The Game Master should have sent you, in advance, the relevant music, and possibly a demonstration of how to deliver the speech.

In addition, during the game, the texts can be whispered into the NPC actor's ear through an **earpiece connected to an MP3 player**, 1-2 seconds ahead of the music, to prevent any lapse of memory. When launching the 2 soundtracks (music and voice) simultaneously, check that the low piano note is properly synchronised. But this method remains fragile, hence the importance of knowing your speech well in any case.


Steering the players towards the right people
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

- Octave, the Archivist and the Arkon can pass the buck to one another when asked for information they have forgotten about the Maupertuis household or the library curse.
- If a player is looking for information about the automatons, or has a tool to repair, steer them towards the Inventor.  {% fact "inventor_can_repair_items" %}
- If it is about gifts of sight, the Duchess is the best placed. {% fact "duchess_knowns_about_prophecies" %}
- If it is about herbalism (for potions) or gemmology, the Druid may have a few answers. {% fact "druid_known_about_plants_and_gems" %}
- If a player is injured, depending on the type of injury, call upon the avatar who could heal them

Being credible in your character
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

All the NPC actors have a story, and a history with the other NPC actors, which must be taken into account in every interaction.

- When the avatars gather for the banquet: pleasure, exchange of rather unsurprising "news" (since their novel universes change very little)
- When the avatars or phantoms meet the players: depending on their history, they show surprise, suspicion, hope of finding there a solution to the library curse. Both avatars and phantoms wonder **what year** it currently is, in the world of the living.
- The avatars and phantoms are relieved to "find each other again" through the players; they then question each other, again indirectly, about what happened 500 years ago during the tragedy.
- When Octave and the Archivist are reunited: outpourings of joy, mutual questioning about the "why" of the situation
- When Octave and the Archivist find the Arkon again: surprise, suspicion, accusatory questions
- When Octave and the Archivist see the Beast: surprise, attempts to tame it in the name of the Maupertuis
- The avatars are at a loss when trying to protect the players from "invisible" dangers such as the wandering Beast
- When the avatars or phantoms meet the Thief: surprise, indignation, condemnation (he remains mocking)
- When the avatars or phantoms meet the Ankou: respect, hope, questions about the deceased Maupertuis; the phantoms have already met him a few times
- Do not hesitate to call upon the players, to question them, if they are not very proactive towards the NPC actors.
- Avatars or phantoms, if someone wants to make you swear something "by Zarathustra": you can say whatever you like, and even mock the players, because this oath does not apply to you; you risk nothing by perjuring yourself with it.


Improvising anecdotes, debates, and bugs
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

The scenario leaves a lot of leeway regarding the events that took place before the curse - whether before or after the sacking of the Maupertuis manor, or in the avatars' alternative universes.

You are therefore free to bring back to life the **nostalgia of those bygone days**, by improvising tales, anecdotes, teasing, about everything your characters (avatars or phantoms) may have experienced on their own, or with one another. In particular, Octave can tell the Parcival descendants a few snippets of their ancestors' story. Seize the openings offered by the other NPC actors, just taking care to respect the fundamental character of your persona. However, avoid making this sound like clues for the players' missions, by staying well away from those specific subjects.

A few examples of **short controversies** that may arise unexpectedly between the avatars, in particular if the pace of the game happens to slow down:

- The Duchess considers that only rhymed poetry is worthy of interest, and that prose poems are signs of cowardice and laziness. The Inventor, on the contrary, claims that only prose gives enough freedom to choose the strongest and most evocative rhythms and words. The Druid, standing back, asserts that only the morning dew on the moss of the woods, or the sunset over the misty mountains, can claim to be poetry.
- The Duchess swears only by a cappella singing and classical music; the Inventor tries to get her to listen to some ElectroSwing music of his own composition (e.g. on a small speaker hidden in a "music box"). The Druid says that only the sound of the wind in the trees and the polyphonies of wolves are real music.
- And many other somewhat clichéd oppositions between "conservative" and "progressive"...

The rifts between the different worlds can lead to comical situations. For example, since the avatars and the phantoms cannot hear one another, they will at times speak simultaneously to the same players, creating a veritable **cacophony** (if possible ending on the same sentence, by "coincidence").



Interactions between embodied phantoms
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

The phantoms CANNOT harm one another, but they are not initially aware of this invulnerability.

So, when they are embodied and conscious of their actions, they react to the other phantoms as if they were alive:

- All the human phantoms are afraid to approach the wandering phantom Beast, even though the Arkon is braver than the others in this respect.
- If the Thief's phantom is released from his anchoring and becomes threatening, some of the other phantoms try to put themselves between him and the players, even if he is embodied and therefore harmless.



Embodiment, anchoring and spectral wandering of the phantoms
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Any phantom NPC actor who died violently is initially **anchored** to a small lit area, which was the place of their violent death. {% fact "beings_dead_violently_are_anchored" %}
They are then in the **embodied phase**: they have a human appearance, can communicate normally, their face is uncovered, and their light-up gloves are switched off.
They can handle a few props representing them; they nevertheless retain a certain slowness in their movements, and if possible a pallid face.

When the **bells** ring (or the **table bell** of spectral frenzy), the phantom switches to spectral, disembodied mode, and goes wandering through the library (including the Forbidden Domain if it has been opened).

This wandering phase also occurs for a phantom who has been released from their anchoring (see below), but far less often. {% fact "anchored_phantoms_turn_more_often_into_specters" %}

Wandering is done by walking not too fast, arms more or less outstretched in front, breathing in and out in a long, hoarse way (no overly clichéd "booo" noise). The phantom must have their face covered with the veil, and their light-up gloves switched on.

Movement is faster and more aggressive for a character who led a life heavy with sins (e.g. the Thief).
{% fact "phantoms_of_sinners_have_faster_specters" %}

To play fair, a spectre should plan its rounds a little in advance (in a circle, in a figure of eight, passing through certain dead-ends or not), and not deviate too much from this route, even if the players have meanwhile gathered somewhere else. But do not hesitate to put a little more pressure on the players if they are too relaxed.

Warning: if a spectre passes **within 3 m of a player**, lit or not, it "senses" them and quickens its pace, veering towards them. If the player runs away, the phantom gradually returns to its initial route. {% fact "phantoms_feel_humans_closer_than_3m" %}

A player touched by a spectre is injured and must call for help, while remaining on the ground - the GM will tell them their penalty. The phantom then **loses interest** in the injured player.

Reminder: following certain events, a phantom may be seized by **{% symbol "spectral frenzy" for "phantom_frenzy_name" %}**, which is a bit like wandering, but with more precise rules, and more liveliness and aggressiveness.

When a single **gong sound** rings out, the wandering phantoms switch back to the **embodied phase**, after having returned to their **anchor point** if they had one. {% fact "bell_and_gong_sounds_trigger_phantom_phases" %}

The Diakons can detach the dead from their spiritual anchoring; but the released phantoms then announce that the walls of the library are like a prison (as the Ankou had pointed out to them during a previous visit), and that they still cannot pass on to the afterlife; they can however go to the forbidden floor, in embodied form or not, if it has been opened.

Note that beings who died of **old age**, like the **Beast**, are not anchored; they roam freely whether embodied or wandering; the rest of the rules apply to them identically. {% fact "beings_dead_naturally_are_not_anchored" %}

As a phantom, including the Beast: Pay attention to the **Exorcist's incantations**, which can drive you away (by switching you back to spectral form) or draw you slowly towards him (in embodied form); even the Beast does nothing to avoid this Exorcist, because it is not supposed to know his powers. And if you are hit by a sacred bullet, you leave at random, moaning in spectral form, for a few minutes, then you go and see the GM discreetly. More details below. {% fact "beast_does_not_fear_rituals" %}
