DETAILED GAME SCHEDULE
==============================

*Theoretical schedule of events, to be re-validated as you go along with the Game Master. The prophecies have their own schedule, managed by the Duchess who supervises the Oracle player.*

H-45 min: General briefing of the **NPC actors** (see sheet). Tour of the library together, if possible.

H-30 min: Briefing of each **group of players** (see sheet), carried out respectively by each NPC actor in charge of it. The GM comes round to hand out the missing objects and answer questions. The players then move on to a **round of introductions** of each of them, mirroring the shared dream they had according to their character sheets.

**-15 min: PREPARATION OF THE PREMISES - Check that all audio/video systems are switched on at least on the authorised floor (for the forbidden floor, it depends on battery life). Be careful to switch on, each time, the speaker, the MP3 player on the right track, and the external battery if applicable. Set the sound volumes of the whispering boxes to maximum so that they are audible. For the other sound sources, set the volumes so that each ambience does not encroach on the rest of the room. The candles already present must also be lit at least on the authorised floor, in particular in the Magus's tomb. Leave the projector at the top of the stairs switched off, if present. The luminous pentacles and seals (braided LED garlands) must remain off. The Beast's soundtrack (one of the 2 audio tracks at the Thief's location) must be switched on, at maximum volume.**

H-10 min: The avatars must be ready to scatter throughout the library, and the phantoms to wander spectrally when the bell/frenzy rings; but all these NPC actors can listen to the introductory speech from afar; some automatons may already be scattered around the premises, activated or not.

H-10 min: Joint briefing of the **players** (see sheet).

H-5 min: [Music] Introductory speech of the Game Master, all together. At the end, make sure to hand over to the **Parcival Oracle** player for their **first intuition**.

H: [Music] The serving automatons take the groups of players respectively to the 4 corners of the authorised floor, then go about their business. **The sentinel automaton** does a patrol, and makes the players it meets swear, by Zarathustra, that they come here in peace.

H+2 min: [Frenzy] Be ready to trigger the **spectral frenzy** music if a player presses the silver table bell.

H+8 min: [Bell] Entry of the phantoms into the game, if not already done

H+15 min: [Gong] The phantoms each become embodied at their location, and accost the players they see passing by.

H+17 min: [Music] Prepare for the Arkon's Confession (kneeling speech).

H+20 min: The automatons invite the visitors to approach the buffet.

H+22 min: [Music] The Inventor avatar opens the buffet, with the automatons (dancing speech).

H+25 min: The GM "renegade Djinn" can entrust the Diakons with his quest to become bound to his community again.

H+30 min: The Druid discreetly steals the Spectral Bell, BEFORE the Ankou's arrival. {% fact "druid_must_steal_table_bell_when_usage_understood" %}

H+35 min: [Music] The Ankou's speech at the top of the stairs; SWITCH ON the projector during his presence, and switch it off afterwards.

**H+40 min: PREPARATION OF THE PREMISES - Go and check/switch on all the sound ambiences of the laboratories/den, spots/lights, and candles (lit because magical) of the forbidden floor. Leave the luminous seals, the incense fountain, and the plasma ball switched off. But switch on the wooden board with the giant LED crystals. Switch off the speaker in the Thief's nook; he will switch it on himself when he becomes embodied.**

H+40 min: [Bells] The phantoms switch back to spectres for 2-3 minutes, then [Gong].

H+45 min: [Music] Prepare for the arrival of the Beast, when the forbidden floor is opened.

H+50 min: [Music] Be ready to put on an instrumental soundtrack when the players enter the forbidden floor. The Thief's phantom then begins his wandering there, with the blue gloves and the LED mask.

H+55 min: [Gong] The Thief becomes embodied in turn, at his anchor point, and accosts the players.

H+1:20: [Music] If the players have still not opened the forbidden floor, the Ankou comes to give them clues on how to access the necessary symbol (ultraviolet symbol in the black administrative briefcase, whose code is whispered by the {% symbol "Venture Prins" for "small_wooden_fake_book_name" %}).

H+1:20: [Bells] The phantoms switch back to spectres for 2-3 minutes, then [Gong].

H+2:00: [Bells] The phantoms switch back to spectres for 2-3 minutes, then [Gong].

H+X: [Music] Prepare for Octave's initiation ceremony, to music.

H+X: [Music] Prepare for Cassandra's holographic prophecy (puzzle of the scary lenticular paintings).

H+X: To frame the Arkon, the Druid plants the falsely compromising document satchel, on the forbidden floor. {% fact "druid_makes_conspiracy_against_arkon" %}

H+X: [Music] Stage the attack of the sentinel automaton.

H+X: [Music] Prepare for the unmasking of the traitor (quarrel between the Duchess and the Druid)

H+X: [Music] Prepare for the players' intrusion into the Magus's tomb

H+X: The Arkon can go and question (or even make them swear) the various groups of players, as to what they will do with the portals leading to the library, if they come out of it alive. {% fact "arkon_queries_players_about_their_choice_for_final_destiny_of_archives" %}

H+X: Give the Druid the grimoire emitting occult sounds, and the Thanatologue. The Druid goes and sits near the pentacle, delighted, with his readings which he proclaims to be promising for escaping from the place. The Ankou goes to prepare for his final speech. {% hint "mystic_soundtrack_for_druid_magic_books" is needed %}

H+X: The Arkon briefs the Diakon who is preparing to confront the Druid by completing the expulsion ritual; the avatars decide to return to their universes so as not to be in danger, and say their farewells.

H+X: [Music] Be ready to trigger the 3 phases of the final battle music, depending on whether the Druid is still immersed in his grimoires (phase 1), then attacks the Diakon following the start of the ritual (phase 2), then collapses after 1 minute (phase 3, entrance of the Arkon). If the Diakon abandons the ritual, phase 2 continues until the situation has calmed down (e.g. a player killed "as an example" by the Druid), in which case the music stops; or until the Druid is killed, by any means whatsoever, which triggers phase 3 of the music.

H+X: [Music] A few minutes after the removal of the Absolute Enclosure (caused by the Druid or by the Diakons' ritual), the players must be gathered at the bottom of the stairs by the automatons. Then comes the musical epilogue, with the **return of the Ankou** who calls to him the phantoms fitted with their **wings** (then SWITCH ON the projector until the end of the epilogue), then Octave's farewell speech, then the departure of the phantoms. The automatons then escort the players back to the briefing room.

H+X: [Music] **Debriefing** with the players and NPC actors:

- Summarise TO MUSIC the outcome of the game.
- Summarise the scenario and which quests succeeded or failed.
- Thank everyone for their participation: PLAYERS, NPC ACTORS, ORGANISERS, LIBRARY.
- Present the CLIVRA and CHRYSALIS and hand out FLYERS.
- Remind everyone of the need for the GUEST BOOK (comments to be made online)
- Take **photographs** together.

**Before tidying up:**

- Collect the **players' props and garments**. IMPORTANT, check that the players do not keep any **keys or jewels** on them.
- Remind everyone that the BATTERIES, JEWELS, KEYS/PADLOCKS, POTIONS, GEMS, RICHES must be taken back separately.
