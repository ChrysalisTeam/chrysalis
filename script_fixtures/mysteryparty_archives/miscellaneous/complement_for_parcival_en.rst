Your battle plan
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

You have only one thing in mind: find the life-saving {% symbol "Rejuvenation Cocktail" for "parcival_disease_main_remedy_name" %}, and - as its name suggests - all the sub-recipes it might be made up of. The most dangerous ones will no doubt be stored in the Forbidden Domain of these Archives, which will not make your task any easier; but it is said that magical systems exist to easily track down a recipe.

It is likely that this preparation will require little-known ingredients; time being short, you will have to either collect them on the spot, or identify them precisely enough to be able to gather them within a few days of your return. The small laboratories present in the Maupertuis sanctuary will be good places to begin the research.
