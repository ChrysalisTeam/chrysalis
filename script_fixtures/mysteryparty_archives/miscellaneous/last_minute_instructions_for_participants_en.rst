

ADDITIONAL BRIEFINGS FOR PARTICIPANTS

GENERAL BRIEFING OF THE NPC ACTORS
----------------------------------------


INFORMATION FOR ALL:

- The Game Master, the **renegade Djinn Geeyem**, will himself be in-game, and able to talk to everyone.

- The NPC actors must wonder with curiosity about the presence of the players, but ALSO of the renegade Djinn in these premises.

- The phantoms (including the Beast), and the avatars, **cannot see each other**. But all can see the Ankou, who is divine.

- The avatars cannot handle novels, neither their own nor those of others; the other books and grimoires remain accessible to them, however. {% fact "avatars_cannot_carry_novels" %}

- Note the well-known and acknowledged presence of a **sentinel automaton**, armed with a Nerf gun.

- A fifth team is part of the game; you will discover it on the day.

- The NPC actors will each more specifically follow one group during the game, preferably with that group's quest progression sheet. The idea is to be able to steer them subtly, if they are struggling to make progress.

    - OCTAVE AND THE ARCHIVIST follow the Parcival Heirs (the Duchess follows the Oracle more specifically)
    - THE INVENTOR follows the Mirandian Spies
    - THE DRUID follows the Heliossarian Explorers
    - THE ARKON follows the Diakons of Bahamoot

- The players cannot easily tell in-game from out-of-game, so avoid discouraging them from doing certain things (going to the forbidden floor or into the Magus's tomb, desecrating the graveyard, using the Spiritual Orb...) even if your *character* would naturally react that way. It is always better to encourage them to show initiative, while reminding them of the rules of caution: pay attention to the markings on artefacts, to the signs on the floor, etc.


FOR THE PHANTOMS:

- You switch on your light-up gloves, and cover your faces, only during your WANDERINGS and spectral frenzies, not when you are in the "embodied phase".
- Triggers: you switch to wandering when the BELLS ring (or the spectral frenzy table bell, which triggers an intense soundtrack); you switch back to embodied (potentially ANCHORED) when the GONG rings. {% fact "bell_and_gong_sounds_trigger_phantom_phases" %}
- **IMPORTANT CHANGE**: at the very start of the game, you will not be there straight away, and will appear in the wandering phase!
- We will need to test the phantoms' WINGS, which will be used for the epilogue, for example: feathers for the Arkon, LEDs for the Archivist, steampunk for Octave
- THIEF: When the players arrive on the forbidden floor, you are already wandering, dangerous. When you embody yourself, it must be at your anchor point. Then switch on the "pirate" soundtrack that goes with your role. Unlike the other phantoms, your switches between wandering and embodiment do not depend on the gong and bell sounds, because you are wandering far more often than they are - so this must be arranged with the Game Master.


FOR THE AVATARS:

- You can interact with all the contents of the library, but not carry novels!
- You all have talents for healing one type of injury or another; reread the section on player injuries, in the "NPC Actors' Supplements" document (in particular the Druid heals with his blood); do ask how an injury happened, to know which of you must act:

    - The Duchess can invoke her own gods, with the help of the initiates present, to repair a sacred injury (affecting the soul).
    - The Druid can use his sea-blue blood, with the help of the spirits of Nature, to heal all kinds of biological ailments.
    - **ALTERNATIVE PLAN**: Druid and Duchess can heal ALL types of injuries (except a torn-off limb)
    - The Inventor can cobble together a mechanical prosthesis to replace a lost limb.


FOR THE AUTOMATONS:

- They cannot move ULTRA-HEAVY objects!!!
- Give their {% symbol 'white ribbons' for 'automaton_symbolic_cloth' %} to the automatons before the game
- Right after the introductory speech at the start of the game, you will have to **escort the groups of players** to their starting points (agree during the briefing on who does what)
- When the game really starts, some of you may be INACTIVE, seated or on the floor, until the Inventor switches you on
- If you are sent into a poisoned area, no problem; but if it is a mechanical or magical trap (thread, jingle bells...), you are broken and fall to the floor; the Inventor must repair you; the GM will say whether the trap is still in place or not.
- Towards the end of the game, after the mystery is solved, you help gather the players at the bottom of the stairs while waiting for the epilogue.
- After the epilogue and the final departure of the phantoms, escort the players back to the round room of the initial briefing, saying that the teleportation portals to the surface will soon close.
- Take photographs during the game, if possible, PLEASE!
- Sentinel automaton: you are vulnerable to DARTS, after all

- Anthropologist Explorer: the letter-cloud posters in BLUE are undecipherable; pretend they are runes that are too ancient.
- Dispeller Diakon: The small skull signs are weak clues; only the symbolic signage is reliable
- Invoker Diakon: Warn the Game Master when you want to activate a seal or a pentacle
- Diakons: You have the possibility of teaching one of your rites to another initiate, but it takes time and has less chance of succeeding
- Diakons: You have received a few grey mice as gifts...
- Parcival Oracle: Do not try to interpret the prophecies (images or sounds) you receive; pass them on as faithfully as possible to the widest possible audience
- Parcival Alchemist: You can take/store all the potions, but communicate, because the Diakons may need them!

- Minesweeper Spy: Change of minesweeping mode => Electric hand now; the LUMINIS are used to mark interesting objects or traps
- Fallen: Remove the white ribbons from the fallen automatons when they are operational

**Miscellaneous advice:**

- You can keep your character sheet with you, in particular for the alchemical formulas or the sacred incantations; it is OUT-OF-GAME.
- You will meet other players: take the time to get to know each other a little, and to see everyone's abilities, as they could be useful to you. Know for example whom to call if you see sticker stars or coloured feathers. It is a rather **collaborative** game, thanks to the Oath of Zarathustra.
- You will also meet various NPC characters; here too, take the time to discover them, to talk with them; perhaps you can help them, perhaps they can also help you in your research!
- Finally, you will come across mechanisms, spells (LIGHT FLASH, cicada sound, jingle bells), and beings that are dangerous for your character... Be careful, they can injure you or much worse; and what do you do if you are injured, again? Yes, you stop moving and call for help.
