Specific information and instructions
========================================

You remember very well the illness of Mérédice de Maupertuis, and how the Maupertuis parents had sent you in person to fetch various highly specialised ingredients to create a potion called the **{% symbol "Rejuvenation Cocktail" for "parcival_disease_main_remedy_name" %}**. During the inventory, you had found and put back in its place the main recipe of said potion, and you point it out to the players who ask for it (see Game Map). {% fact "archivist_knows_about_meredice_rejuvenation_cocktail_recipe_location" %}

If you are asked about the recipes of the **intermediate potions** needed for this cocktail, you indicate that you had marked the presence of some of them, on the authorised floor, with **white arrows drawn on the floor** (represented in-game by white tape). {% hint "white_arrows_on_floor_towards_cure_recipes" is needed %} {% fact "archivist_has_marked_locations_of_rejuvenation_cocktail_subrecipes" %}

**Important**: you know that the last sub-recipe for the {% symbol "Rejuvenation Cocktail" for "parcival_disease_main_remedy_name" %} is on the forbidden floor, but that there exists a **{% symbol "Tracker Grimoire" for "frequency_scanner_book_name" %}** allowing one to follow the magical vibrations of these recipes, hidden because they are dangerous; a grimoire that must be in one of the chests of the authorised floor. When the players bring it back to you, you give them the magic phrase to pronounce (for example **"Tracker Grimoire, tell me where the Pyrolitis Tincture is"**), and the GM will activate the touch tablet for the players. {% hint "radio_frequency_scanner_app_in_chest" is needed %}

If you are asked where to find the following specific ingredients, you assume that these are old-fashioned names for herbs or minerals, and you send the players **to the Druid** to find out exactly what they are: **"eternal tree", "foxglove", "clairvoyant mineral", "aventurine", and "tiger's eye"**.

During one of your brief phantom embodiments, a very long time ago, you saw a **shady individual** pass through the library. He was heading towards the Forbidden Domain. You do not know what became of him. {% fact "archivist_saw_thief_exploring_the_library" %}
