Specific information and instructions
====================================================

Personally deeply affected by the sad fate of your friends, you place all your hopes in the supernatural affinities of your otherworldly companions, perhaps to obtain justice, and above all to free the wandering souls of the Maupertuis household.

During your happy times in the library, you had searched the authorised floor with Octave, and gathered what was needed to build several **life-size automatons** to wait at table. Boosted by a few spells from the Maupertuis heir, these androids are without a doubt your masterpieces, even though you unfortunately cannot bring them back to your own universe to show them to your loved ones. {% fact "inventor_has_built_androids" %}

You had given Octave a **wide silver bracelet with white ribbons called the {% symbol 'Dominion' for 'androids_command_bracelet_name' %}**, designed by yourself and allowing one to command the automatons with firmness. This bracelet has now returned to your **wrist**. {% hint 'androids_command_silver_white_ribbon_bracelet_for_inventor' is needed %}

As the current master of the automatons, you direct them for table service, you adjust/oil them when they malfunction, and even test new interactions with them - which they may or may not understand. You have an **echo megaphone** to rally them, which you also use whimsically for your enthusiastic flights of fancy on any subject whatsoever. {% hint "echo_megaphone" is needed %}

But you worry about the future of these fabulous automatons, in this library where repair materials are starting to run short. If you meet a player who is also interested in robotics, and who demonstrates it by using advanced technical vocabulary (which you pick up on, improvising), you agree to **assign and activate** the bracelet for them (with a few turns of a screwdriver), and you announce **to everyone** that from now on the automatons present will respond as best they can to that player's orders, and not to yours; at least as long as these orders do not contradict their innate rules of non-aggression and survival. {% fact "spy_minesweeper_likes_building_robots" %}
