Phantom - The Arkon {% symbol "Valerias" for "arkon_name" %}
==================================================================

Your story
=======================

Your profile
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++


<{ phantom_arkon_character_summary }/>

A heroic "Arkon" (a knight blessed by the gods) of the Yodic religion (and more specifically a disciple of the so-called "Mithraite" denomination), a great scholar, an adept of many forms of magic, a staunch defender of humans against the incursions of demonic entities, you have travelled the planet leaving countless legends in your wake. {% fact "arkon_means_blessed_knight" %}

Your epic journey came to a halt {% symbol "500 years" for "library_initial_events_timedelta" %} ago, during your stay at the **{% symbol "Rochesombre Monastery" for "common_monastery_name" %}**, not far from the ruins of the Maupertuis domain. On the occasion of the {% symbol "winter solstice" for "astral_opening_date" %}, you suddenly sensed the presence of otherworldly entities, neither human nor demonic, beyond a {% symbol "planetary corridor" for "teleportation_system_name" %} leading to a strange library.

Your order had warned you about these exceedingly rare phenomena in which different universes intertwined: their physical, magical and spiritual properties, partially incompatible, could give rise to great tragedies, even without ill intent. So you set off without delay to investigate the problem, before the magic passage to the library closed. And you convinced the inhabitants of the place to let you proceed.


The library curse
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

As you were carrying out, in a magical tumult, the successive rituals for closing the interdimensional portals, a malefic power shrouded in darkness intervened. You fought it relentlessly, for interminable minutes, with blows of your {% symbol "gladius" for "arkon_weapon_name" %}, with spells and with exorcism. It painfully took your attacks (except the sacred ones, which evidently did nothing to it)... but it regenerated in the blink of an eye.

When you received a projectile right in the forehead, you understood that the game was lost; but with your dying breath, you cast an immemorial spell, magnified tenfold by the power of your sacrifice: the **{% symbol "Absolute Enclosure" for "library_cursed_enclosure_name" %}**, a curse locking the entire library inside a sacred vortex, a spiritual "black hole", which would only cease when your attacker was wiped from this world. {% fact "traitor_death_ends_cursed_enclosure" %}

Since then, as you had foreseen in your ultimate sacrifice, your phantom soul has remained a prisoner of the library; less because of the spell of your death - you know that this can temporarily "bind" a soul to a place - than because of your own curse.

You have seen wandering spectres pass by, no doubt those of the child and the Archivist. What exactly did they die of? Of the monster that attacked you? You do not even know what it was. The Beast that guarded the floor above - probably a **cave ghoul**, judging by its anti-magic aura - a creature that nevertheless seemed quite harmless to a mage such as you? Another monstrosity lurking in the premises, awakened by your presence? One of the otherworldly characters gone mad, and surreptitiously returned to this human dimension? You have fleetingly seen a few of these "avatars" (as the mystics call them) pass by, whom you had not been able to seal back in their own universes. They seemed to be searching the library in vain, looking for answers. {% fact "only_arkon_knows_things_about_beast_species" %}

So you are waiting for someone from the surface, sensitive to these disturbances in the strata of existence, to come to your rescue... Preferably an Arkon of the same cult as you, whom you could trust enough to make your "{% symbol "mission confession" for "arkon_mission_confession" %}" to him and let him solve the problem properly. Indeed, even the {% symbol "Oath of Zarathustra" for "unbreakable_oath_name" %} is not a sufficient guarantee, for more than one fanatic has preferred to break it, and be mortally punished shortly afterwards, rather than do what was truly just and sworn.

Your saviour will need, not to break your {% symbol "Absolute Enclosure" for "library_cursed_enclosure_name" %} of the library - which would annihilate your efforts - but to hunt down and kill the monstrosity that is surely still lurking there, and to rupture the otherworld corridors for good. How you long to see this mission come to a happy end, and to be able to join your peers at the celestial banquets.

{#

Or, if you cannot find one, at least a person who will have sworn enough things by the "{% symbol "Oath of Zarathustra" for "unbreakable_oath_name" %}" for their good will to be attested.

You parleyed with Octave and the Archivist who had taken refuge in these places full of knowledge and magic, and you convinced them to close the forbidden portals it mentioned, in return for your help in getting them out of here and to safety. The otherworldly characters were more or less been globally very angry with you, but they had to accepted the decision. So you it set up advantage of the ritual, and begin to close the doors one by one.

Specific information and instructions
========================================

At the very beginning of your initiatory journey as an Arkon, you found the legendary **{% symbol "Etheric Necklace of Blessing" for "etheric_benediction_necklace_name" %}**. You decided to melt it into your {% symbol 'Mithril armour' for 'arkon_armor_name' %}, which made it ULTRA-HEAVY but protected you entirely, when you wore it, against the attacks of entities from the world of the dead. Beware, this enchanted armour also blocks **physical attacks and offensive spells**, but **only at the torso** (so the rest of the body remains liable to be injured). You do not know what became of your armour after your death. {% fact "etheric_benediction_necklace_is_in_arkon_sacred_armor" %}

Nor do you know what became of your {% symbol "gladius" for "arkon_weapon_name" %}, a rustic weapon but very ULTRA-HEAVY and very sturdy, with which you had managed to land a few brave blows on your fatal assailant.
{% hint "arkon_heavy_gladius_sword_stolen_by_thief" is needed %}

Shortly before performing the banishing ritual, you had set down your **document satchel** containing a few valuables at a distance, so that it would not interfere; if it were found, its contents could help the living who are short of money. You tell them so if the opportunity arises. {% fact "arkon_bag_disappeared_just_after_his_death" %} {% hint "arkon_bag_containing_money" is needed %}
{% hint "arkon_belt_bag_with_compromising_sickle_and_holly_emblem_letter" is needed %}

You would be extremely surprised if belongings of yours were found in the Forbidden Domain, given that you never set foot there. {% fact "only_octave_had_been_in_forbidden_domain" %}

Long before your death, you had made your **skeleton indestructible**, as much to strengthen your body as to prevent your possible disappearance in the clutches of some enemy from remaining a mystery forever. That too is the spirit of foresight.

You know various legends dating back to the era of the {% symbol 'Magus Mos Peratys' for 'maupertuis_dynasty_founder' %}. One of them speaks of various artefacts (jewels, sceptres, weapons) created by a small sect, and called the **"Amplificans"**, whose particularity was to extend the spatial range of their bearer's spells. With this multiplying effect, a ritual that usually created a small wall of flame over a few paces could end up setting an entire plain ablaze. The symbol common to these artefacts was a **{% symbol "sun with a black whirl at its centre" for "amplificans_artefacts_symbol" %}**. In your opinion, clearly, the Magus used such sources of power. {% fact "arkon_has_hints_about_ring_amplificans" %}

You may be rather frustrated to discover that your former monastery now hosts a rival cult, that of the Diakons of {% symbol "Bahamoot" for "god_of_diakons" %}. But then, it is the eternal struggle between orthodoxy and heresies. {% fact "bahamoot_monastery_belonged_to_yodic_order_in_the_past" %}

If asked: you know of no miracle solution to a **legion of zombies** - other than fighting them one by one with weapons, preferably blessed ones. But you have heard of powerful ground enchantments, capable of preventing their initial "reanimation" from corpses. {% fact "arkon_has_clues_about_preventing_zombie_invocation_on_land" %}

In case of questions: you do not know what an **"{% symbol "Abyssal Vortex" for "link_to_demonic_space" %}"** is, nor a **"{% symbol "Fount of Knowledge" for "link_to_djinn_world" %}"**. {% fact "arkon_does_not_know_about_spiritual_orb_magic" %}

If a living person dies, you urge the others to find a way to **resurrect** them temporarily, so that they can still help; you know that this kind of subterfuge has often, in the past, unblocked situations deemed hopeless.

If the Exorcist attempts to **rupture the otherworld corridors** while avatars are still present in the library, you openly wonder whether this will not put them in grave danger. {% fact "diakon_exorcist_might_kill_remaining_avatars_with_expulsion_ritual" %}

If you meet a phantom in spectral wandering, and therefore dangerous, you can shield the various living people present with your body. If, once back in the embodied (and therefore harmless) phase, this phantom is not aware that they are dead, you try to explain this to them, and to convince them that any desire for earthly things no longer makes any sense. {% fact "phantom_touch_harms_the_living_only_when_disincarnated" %} {% fact "arkon_and_ankou_try_to_persuade_thief_that_he_is_dead" %}

If the denouement is approaching, you can go and question (or even make them swear) the various groups of players, as to what they will do with the portals leading to the library if they come out of it alive. You yourself do not necessarily have a firm opinion.

Finishing the final battle
=============================

If the villain is unmasked, and the players manage to complete a ritual to expel him, then this villain becomes as if injured.

You then step in to finish the battle, to music, with the help of the Diakons; the choreography of this final scene must be well rehearsed beforehand, with the Game Master.
