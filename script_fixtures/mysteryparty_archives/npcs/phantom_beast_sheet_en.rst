Your story
=======================

Once the protector of the Forbidden Domain, you found yourself permanently locked inside it when the curse was cast upon it, centuries ago.

Even once dead of old age, it was impossible either to reach the land of the dead, or (still) to pass into the normal area of the library. So you wandered, with growing resentment, until you became perpetually "disembodied", in spectral wandering (not having died violently, you were in any case not "anchored" to a specific place, even during your embodied phases).

When the passage to the stairs finally opens, naturally, you rush into it in a rage, in spectral form. {% fact "beast_cannot_access_normal_zone_because_of_ultimate_seal" %}


Instructions for the attack
==============================

- Talk with the Game Master, before the encounter, to recap the countermeasures the players already have.
- You cannot, of course, see the avatars, nor they you.
- You behave like a slightly frightened dog towards the other phantoms, yapping when you meet them, but without approaching them. {% fact "beast_ignores_or_fears_other_phantoms" %}
- If the opportunity arises, you can decide to go down by a passage where the players are not expecting you, to take them from behind.
- You can only walk, but with long strides if necessary.
- You attack the nearest players (you see very poorly beyond 3 m). {% fact "characters_know_how_the_beast_works_regarding_3m_sight" %}
- If a player is invulnerable (because wearing the Arkon's {% symbol "Mithril armour" for "arkon_armor_name" %}, for example), you attack them a few times in vain, then choose another target.
- If a player falls into your hands, you "claw" them for 5-6 seconds, then warn them that they are dying and move on to another.
- The players' goal is to lure you onto a **pentacle** and then "freeze" you there with a quick ritual (which you neither detect nor fear); play along if their plan is coherent, in particular be strongly attracted, step by step, by the **"{% symbol "moon stones" for "phosphorescent_pebbles_name" %}"** (phosphorescent pebbles) if you come across any (you cannot pick them up since you are a phantom, but you try anyway). {% hint "beast_snack_as_moon_stones" is needed %} {% fact "beast_is_addicted_to_moon_stones" %} {% fact "phantoms_always_keep_most_of_their_instinct" %} {% fact "beast_does_not_fear_rituals" %}
