Specific information and instructions
========================================

When the players arrive on the forbidden floor, you are already wandering, dangerous. When you become embodied, it must be at your anchor point. Then switch on the "pirate" soundtrack that goes with your role. Unlike the other phantoms, your switches between wandering and embodiment do not depend on the gong and bell sounds, because you are wandering far more often than they are - so this must be arranged with the Game Master.

To keep safely feeding your immense Treasure of the {% symbol "Pass'muraille Keep" for "thief_castle_name" %}, you demand that payments be made directly into your **{% symbol 'bottomless collection pouch' for 'thief_moneybox_name' %}**, a kind of magic moneybox that teleports goods to your family castle; the players must first find this moneybox for you. {% hint "thief_heavy_lost_moneybox" is needed %}

Important: you must **empty** this pouch into an out-of-game bag (or into your pockets) every time you are about to switch back to a spectre, so that the money spent is properly taken out of the game.

**Information to memorise well and to sell on**

- IMPORTANT: You know that there is an artefact in the Forbidden Domain, **the {% symbol "Spiritual Orb" for "spiritual_orb_name" %}**, allowing one to open spiritual links to other strata of this world. And you are *almost* certain that you are the only one who knows the formula leading more specifically to the **{% symbol "Fount of Knowledge" for "link_to_djinn_world" %}**, a powerful source of knowledge; because you had destroyed the exceedingly rare antique "cryptex" (locked cylinder) that gave indications about it. The formula is: **"O prodigies of wisdom and curiosity, come and answer my thirst for learning"**.  {% fact "phantom_thief_knows_formula_of_link_to_djinn_world" %} {% hint "cryptex_providing_formula_of_link_to_djinn_world" is needed %}
  You had however kept, as a souvenir, the paper giving the code of this cryptex in micro-lettering. {% hint "thief_paper_with_cryptex_code_as_microtext" is needed %}

- You had laid your hands on a precious jewellery box that had belonged to the young {% symbol "Isadora" for "maupertuis_mother" %}, Octave's future mother; nobody had managed to open it, because in case of failure it locked itself for a month; but your keen ear allowed you to discover the number expected by the gears: **{% symbol "159" for "maupertuis_mother_thanatologue_chest_code" %}**. With a bit of luck, she kept using this code to protect other secrets.  {% fact "thief_knows_about_isadora_thanatologue_chest_code" %} {% hint 'thief_wallet_with_isadora_code_in_beast_nest' is needed %}

- You know that the brass brooch of Mérédice de Maupertuis, mentioned in the memories of some of her descendants, was an important piece for gaining access to the family treasure; she left this brooch in her small occult-studies study, which stood **in a corner of the Forbidden Domain, beyond the alchemy laboratory (see the map with the Game Master)**; an area that has since collapsed. {% hint "maupertuis_daughter_jewel_under_rubbles_beyond_alchemist_laboratory" is needed %}
  {% fact "thief_knows_about_location_of_maupertuis_daughter_jewel" %}

- You are very crestfallen at the thought of having had your oh-so-precious {% symbol 'teleportation astrolabe' for 'thief_teleportation_device_name' %} stolen from you, but at the same time that is the law among thieves... The magic formula to activate it is **"Astrolabe, carry me over there"**. {% fact "thief_knows_teleportation_device_formula" %} It can teleport up to {% symbol '3 people' for 'thief_teleportation_device_simultaneous_limit' %} at a time, as long as each of them holds one of its feet. It can be used as many times as one wants, but you warn that one must wait **{% symbol '30 seconds' for 'thief_teleportation_device_cooldown' %}** between teleportations.

Of course, if the players solve puzzles without going through your paid clues, it irritates you, you grumble, you sulk...
