Welcome by the Police Inspector
---------------------------------------

- "The office of our President **Émile Loubet** has sent word of your coming."
- "I am honoured to welcome your agency of **paranormal detectives**, whose merits need no further proof."
- "This case is a murky one; for now we have heard of the **disappearance of a student, Andrea, and of a museum piece, an ancient gladius**, but we know no more."
- "We will therefore let you **interrogate** the witnesses in this case in turn, and inspect the museum's treasure."
- "Note that we already have one strange phenomenon: the **lighting has been malfunctioning** since this morning around the treasure."
- "I remain at your disposal, between each interrogation, should you need to have **facts checked** by my police forces."
- "We will also provide you with **police information**, as we go along, on our own investigations."
- "Here is a **newspaper article** about that strange institution, the Cromlech of Lyon."

**ROTATION ORDER: Guide, Reporter, Curator, and finally the Museum Treasure**


Initial Investigation Report
-----------------------------

- Identity sheets of the witnesses in the case.
- First findings made on the premises: available access routes, bloodstain, disappearance of the ancient gladius.


First Simultaneous Interview
----------------------------------

Guide: relatively relaxed and jovial, as he imagines it is merely the theft of a replica gladius; talks about his passions and his recent discoveries regarding Gallo-Roman antiquity (but without details). He momentarily loses his train of thought if the Druid "casts a spell" on him.

Reporter: recounts his meeting with Andrea, and the troubling clues of the morning (his friend's absence at the appointment, ancient gladius missing, reddish mark on the floor...).

Curator: claims to have spent the morning at a public ceremony and to understand nothing of all this fuss over a simple act of vandalism. Is visibly irritated, embarrassed, if the Druid's ghost distracts him by speaking to him.

Treasure: discovery of the museum's artefacts, and of the {% symbol "regeneration potion" for "potion_de_guerison" %} on the floor (used by Andrea).

Druid's intervention:

- He walks behind the Guide, listens to what he says, performs an incantation while laying hands above his head, then leaves without paying attention to the players' reactions.
- He comes to whisper in the Curator's ear, in an unintelligible tongue; he takes fright and leaves if the players point out that they can see him.
- He may pass briefly through the other rooms, without stopping, cursing in his unintelligible tongue. Being "seen" by the players stresses him out.


First Investigation Update
----------------------------------

- Information about a dubious key duplication carried out in town, a key identical to the Curator's and the Guide's.
- Information about the illicit analysis of an ancient gladius by a person resembling the Reporter (without proof).
- Information about the chaotic course of the public ceremony where the Curator claims to have spent the morning.
- Clue to the code of the chest in the Treasure Room.
- Analysis of the blood (blood type, age...) from the Treasure Room, showing that it belongs to Andrea.


Second Simultaneous Interview
---------------------------------------

Guide: shocked by the news of the spilled blood, somewhat distraught; candidly admits, without shame, that he duplicated the key to help Andrea succeed in his studies.

Reporter: put on the spot over the illicit analysis of the gladius, but not thrown off balance; admits he helped Andrea carry out this operation, but insists that this is not the priority right now.

Curator: put on the spot by the revelations about his timetable, thrown off balance; if cornered he can fall back on the theory that "Andrea committed suicide", which he supposedly witnessed by chance.

Treasure: probable opening of the chest's padlock, and examination of its contents (curse tablet and cryptex).

Police Inspector's intervention halfway through the interview:

- Points out the wound on the Curator's left hand. {% xitem "bandage sanglant au poignet pour le conservateur" is needed %}
- Accuses the Guide of having forged diplomas.

Druid's intervention: He goes to see the player groups currently with the Guide and the Curator; each time, he beckons the group's Psyker over to him; he introduces himself, then tells him (in French) a tall tale, to the effect that these artefacts are sacred relics of the Gaulish people, and that Andrea must have stolen the gladius out of greed.


Second Investigation Update
----------------------------------

- Analysis report obtained by Andrea and the Reporter, certifying that the gladius was a genuine antiquity.
- Discovery of the Curator's liturgical paraphernalia, and of a clue to the cryptex code.
- Discovery of the (fake) farewell letter mistakenly put in the Lost and Found by the Guide.


INTERLUDE: Professor Gustave Bloch's History Lesson
----------------------------------------------------------

**TAKE PHOTOS**

The players are gathered in the largest room.

The professor arrives, eccentric and agitated, and recaps the history of the conquest of Gaul and the battle of Uxellodunum, then sceptically touches on a few legends surrounding it.


Third Simultaneous Interview
----------------------------------

Guide: talks about the strange measures imposed on him by the Curator regarding the handling of the artefacts.

Reporter: does not believe for a moment that Andrea's farewell letter is genuine.

Curator: if cornered, he confesses everything, but protests that the stakes are far greater than "him versus Andrea", and that certain truths must remain hidden.

Treasure: probable opening of the cryptex, and study of its contents.

Police Inspector's intervention halfway through the interview:

- Comes to challenge the Curator on the fact that the letter was written with a trembling hand, hence perhaps a "wounded" one.
- Points out the mysterious signet ring worn by the Guide (a gift from Andrea).


Third and Final Investigation Update
------------------------------------------------

- Discovery of a revolver (with one bullet missing), bearing fingerprints, in the Treasure Room.
- Vibrations set off on the museum's fake grimoire.
- Feedback from the Cromlech regulars about their nightmares.
- Andrea's call to his allies, via a message scrawled on the envelope found on the floor.

**LOGISTICS: put the extra parchment (Andrea's message) in the Grimoire, and start the vortex video in the door of the miniature cromlech**


Fourth Simultaneous Interview
--------------------------------------

All the witnesses start to **ramble** halfway through the interview!

Guide: tries to contribute his historical and mythological knowledge to help the players.

Reporter: raises the alarm: "Andrea is in danger in another world, we must help him".

Curator: raises the alarm: "the artefacts must be left alone, and Andrea must be stopped from hijacking them for his own benefit!".

Treasure: study of the artefacts and messages discovered; nothing new is expected.

Druid's intervention: He goes to see the player groups currently with the Reporter and the Treasure; each time, he beckons the group's Psyker over to him, and now says (in French) that there is indeed an entity gestating in the artefacts, but that it is a benevolent deity, to be protected so that the druids' legacy is not lost forever.

Final Huddles
-------------------------------------

Everyone gathers **OUTSIDE the Treasure Room**.

- All the "clerks" are gathered by the Police Inspector to share the results of their investigations.
- All the "psykers" are gathered and briefed by the Druid: they are now like zombies, subjected to his druidic will, and will be his accomplices in the final scene (with **quick make-up** simulating demonic possession). From now on they must walk slowly, occasionally stammer unintelligible things, and follow the Druid's orders. {% xfact "le druide envoûte les psykers à la fin du jeu" %}
- The rest of the players stay and talk among themselves.

**LOGISTICS: Remove the smartphone from the door of the miniature cromlech (safety) and fit the needle of the Tesla coil.**


Final Scene
---------------------------------

- The players are called back to the **Treasure**.
- Meanwhile the Druid nudges the **zombie psykers** to return, rather discreetly, to their groups.
- The final soundtrack is started, the Cromlech begins to **crackle** from time to time.
- 30 s after the start, the Cromlech's Tesla coil also starts playing the "Dies Irae"; the **Reporter**, followed by the **Guide**, then calls on the players to join him on the **left** of the Cromlech, and to make the "Centurions' Sign" with him to help Andrea (arms crossed over the chest). **Slogan to repeat: "By the Glory of Jupiter".**
- 12 s later, when the Tesla coil takes up the "Dies Irae" again, it is the turn of the **Druid** (now in broken French), followed by the **Curator**, to urge the players to join him on the **right** of the Cromlech, and to make the "Druids' Sign" with him (forming a menhir shape with the arms and hands) to save the druids' last wish. The zombie psykers follow his orders. **Slogan to repeat: "By the Might of Toutatis".**
- The Cromlech calms down but the music goes on; both camps must keep up their slogans.
- The Inspector urges the undecided players to choose their side, because **"remaining impassive in such situations of danger is a crime"**.
- At the end of the music, Andrea appears; he explains the **ending (his victory or his defeat)** and what it means for the World.
- Announce the END OF GAME!
