

Safety Rules
============

**Physical and emotional safety**: no physical contact between participants, unless agreed beforehand (e.g. to act out a scuffle scene).

**Care of props**: you may inspect and handle the in-game props, but gently - no "forcing".
The artefacts displayed in the museum (weaponry, grimoire, model...) must not be moved.
As a special case, the fragile ancient model must not be touched at all.
