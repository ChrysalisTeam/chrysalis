PARANORMAL DETECTIVES
#############################

Situation Summary
=============================

The action takes place around the year 1900, in Lyon, France.

You belong to a large detective agency specialised in the supernatural.

Unlike "normal" humans, you therefore all possess the ability to see, hear and sense phenomena such as ghosts, curses, etc.

Recently, you were preparing to leave for the city of Rome, as Italian scientists had been recording, for about **{% symbol "1 year" for "délai_début_secousses_sismiques_italiennes" %}**, numerous events (seismic tremors, lava upwellings...) that their physical models could no longer explain. They feared the awakening of an old curse.

But you had to cancel your departure, because the French President Émile Loubet himself, following the plea of an Italian diplomat, summoned you to this unusual building called the "Cromlech", in order to investigate several disappearances.


Special Roles
==================

Three characters in your group may have a small additional power (and therefore an additional responsibility).

The other detectives, on the other hand, have priority in conducting the interviews and putting questions to the witnesses in the case.

[BR]

CLERK'S OFFICE
    The clerk is responsible for taking notes during the investigation. This person will also share their findings with the other groups of detectives, as well as with the judicial authorities.

PSYCHISM
    The psyker possesses advanced spiritualist gifts, which allow them to communicate telepathically with ghosts. It is the ghosts who contact the psykers when the time comes.

INTIMIDATION
    The intimidator is able, once per interview with a witness, to strike a threatening pose, putting on a dark look and banging a fist on the table. They then ask ONE SINGLE question, which the interviewee will be psychologically compelled to answer truthfully; but that person can only answer YES, NO, or I DON'T KNOW; so a "closed" question is required.
