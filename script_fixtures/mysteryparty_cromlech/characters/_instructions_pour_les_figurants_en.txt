Instructions for all NPC actors
===============================

Only the Curator and the Druid can see/hear the manifestations of the supernatural. These manifestations are the druid's spectre itself; the phosphorescence, lightning flashes and noises in the Treasure Room; the clue labels on certain objects... The police forces and the other witnesses in the case notice none of this, and show their bewilderment when the players question them about it.

If the players ask you about matters that are not covered in your character sheets, feel free to improvise. Either by saying you do not know, or by making up facts about your own story; but take care to memorise (or even write down) your answers, so as not to contradict yourself in a later interrogation.
