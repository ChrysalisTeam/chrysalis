DRUID'S SPECTRE
######################

**Read the other NPC actors' sheets first, so as to have a good picture of what has recently happened around the library-museum called the "Cromlech".**

Your Story
=====================

You are {% symbol "Olloudios" for "nom_du_spectre" %}. You were one of the druids who, 2 millennia ago, set in motion the gestation of a **divine warrior** capable of saving the Gaulish peoples from the Roman invasion.

Then you voluntarily became a ghost, in order to watch over this gestation. From generation to generation, you advise the dynasty charged with protecting the ancient artefacts that hold the divine entity.

But this gestation has been chaotic and irregular over the centuries; and today the artefacts are being endangered by the curiosity of that accursed **Andrea di Revel**.

Your actions during the investigation
=====================================

You helped the Curator act as best he could after his blunder with Andrea *(see his character sheet)*.

You went away to recharge in your spiritual "limbo", but on your return Andrea's body had vanished... as had the gladius. {% xfact "le glaive antique a disparu le jour j" %}

Since then, you have been searching for them everywhere, fearing they may have passed into the **in-between realm** where the entity is developing; but that realm is vast, and Andrea knows how to throw you off the scent.

In the real world, private detectives have arrived on the premises; but to your great astonishment, you will discover that they too can see you, and hear you (but in your native tongue; for examples of how it sounds, see the video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wc22W3bos64).

You can only make yourself understood (in French) by the **psykers**, those detectives who have additional mental powers; to do so you must step a little aside with them, to establish the mental link and be able to speak to them.

Your goal is to turn the players against Andrea and his allies, by lying skilfully:

- Initially, you can make them believe that these artefacts are very precious, that they are the last remnants of your civilisation, and that you want them to stay with the worthy Guesclin family. You claim that Andrea is after these artefacts for villainous motives.
- Later, you can admit that there is indeed an entity gestating, but that it is a benevolent deity; the soul of the Celtic people, which will allow Brittany to regain its former glory. In that case, you say you fear that Andrea wants to appropriate the powers of this deity.

Your role in the ending
================================

After the 4th and final interrogation rotation, you gather the psykers in a corner, and announce to them out-of-game that they are now under your control; they must therefore behave like zombies, and follow your orders during the final scene. You can draw lightning-bolt tattoos on their faces and arms to make this curse visible. {% xfact "le druide envoûte les psykers à la fin du jeu" as author %}

During the final scene, you harangue all the players (in the Celtic tongue, with sweeping gestures) to make the **"Druids' Sign"** with you, facing the artefacts: forming a menhir shape in front of themselves, with their arms and hands.

This will be a way for them to give their energy to the divine entity, so that it can slay the Hero who has come to fight it with the ancient gladius.
