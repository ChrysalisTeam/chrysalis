
The Loyd Georges Affair
==============================

The action takes place on the planet Pangea, where the level of development is similar to that of our 21st-century Earth, but with a far smaller population.

In fact, the entire history of this planet, from prehistory up to the year 1908, is identical to ours. References to those eras of our world, to their cultural and artistic output, are therefore valid on Pangea, even if few people know them. Indeed, at the beginning of the 20th century, the two worlds diverged. While ours went on its way towards the world wars, Pangea suffered a major tectonic and climatic upheaval (called **Tunguska**), which torpedoed all human development for millennia. The planet is at last, and with great difficulty, emerging from this cataclysm, and humanity is taking flight once more.

The **land of Sabarim**, where the action is set, is a young republic in full scientific ferment, in which the elites enjoy a modernity close to that of our Earth year 2000. Sabarim shines in particular through its academies, such as the one in Alifir attended by the newly famous Loyd Georges. *For the atmosphere, think "renaissance / steampunk / Victorian England", where well-off people have interior decoration all in velvet, paintings, crystal and brass.*

Titles of nobility still exist, but have only cosmetic value. The national currency is the kash (1 kash buys 10 baguettes of bread). The local justice system is very harsh; this does not stop a small underworld from surviving, but it sells its services very dearly, and rarely goes beyond the stage of petty crime.

Following the archaeological exploits of a fabulously wealthy sexagenarian from this land of Sabarim, **Sir Loyd Georges (from the city of Salima)**, the countries of the continent of **Midolia** (to the north-west, see the world map below) are on high alert.

This amateur adventurer has indeed discovered a buried temple in the Midolian country called **Akaris**, a temple whose contents have revived an ancestral legend, that of the **"Three Divine Orbs"**. A myth prophesying the final domination of Akaris – by far the most fanatical and imperialist of the Pangean states – over the rest of the populations, by the will of the Akarith deities called the **Yods**. A myth obviously liable to reawaken violent tensions between the countries of the region!

When Loyd Georges organised great auctions in his manor in Salima, to sell off various jewels and artefacts found on site, it was only natural that many countries (Masslavia, the Dorian Dukedom... and even the distant island-continent of Lordania) got involved in the affair.

Each State obviously came with its own approach to the problem; **Masslavia** seeks above all to avoid being invaded, once again, by the belligerent Akariths; the **Dorian Dukedom**, resigned, is considering a discreet alliance with the Akarith political and religious leaders (the powerful **Akarith Judicators**) so as not to end up among their future slaves; as for **Lordania**, it has come to make sure that no serious economic and military competitor can emerge from this conflict, and above all not an Akarith empire.

**Everyone suspects that these countries are not content with diplomatic communiqués, and that they must have sent their best secret agents on site, to learn more about the discoveries of the gentleman of Salima. Regional accents are very pronounced on Pangea, so it is almost impossible to hide one's origin; but actually proving that an honest foreign bidder is in fact a spy... is no easy matter.**

A few months have passed since the auctions were announced. A small sale of gems and artefacts, all fairly unremarkable, has already taken place; but the following auctions, which were meant to build up in quality, have been delayed by various incidents: threatening letters, hacking, attempted kidnappings or burglaries... {% fact 'loyd_georges_has_been_attacked_multiple_times' %}

Loyd Georges, determined to give the event fresh impetus, invites all participants to an **evening of private auctions**, in his manor.

The guests are well aware that they may run into the formidable **Inspector Shark** there, who is investigating the recent depredations their host has suffered. But the evening is certainly not going to go as planned.
