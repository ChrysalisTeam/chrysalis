

Generic abilities
=============================


World escrow agent
  A paper signed by you and by another protagonist, defining a mutual agreement (as well as penalties for non-compliance), is enough to establish a "perfect contract" protected by the "Pangean escrow agent" system. Whether the contract is lawful or not, this powerful international system will do everything possible to ensure that each party fulfils its obligations; some say it has even gone so far as to hire hitmen to punish dishonest contracting parties. Copying the escrow agent on messages exchanged on the Anthropia web portal also establishes protection of the contract. *Inform the Game Master of your pacts made on paper, of course, and draw a circle around your signatures so that they are considered "irrefutable" in the game (see Rules). The GM, on the other hand, automatically sees the messages sent on Anthropia.*

Palm-greasing
  Some people present at this evening will be incorruptible, others much less so.
  Knowing how to slip a few banknotes into the right hand could be a good idea, in order to obtain the little favour one desires. Bear in mind, moreover, that a "good tip", in the world of the Sabarith nobility, is more in the region of **100 kashes**. *Your money will be provided to you at the start of the game, but perhaps it is possible to get hold of more on the premises...*

Duel of honour
  Challenging random people to a duel of honour, even for a trivial reason, can allow you to extract valuables from them, or to prevent them from acting as they would like. But it is a double-edged sword. *See the dedicated chapter of the "Game Universe" document.*

Combat
  Violence is the last resort of the weak; but it is a resort all the same. *In the (unlikely) event that a violent altercation takes place, and unless otherwise directed, victory inevitably goes to the better-armed party: a bare-handed fighter loses to a fighter equipped with a melee weapon, who in turn loses to a ranged weapon. With similar weaponry, victory is decided by a "rock-paper-scissors" (this does not prevent you from simulating the confrontation, but only by mutual agreement and in compliance with the safety rules).*

Oberon Mediation
  *See the introduction to this brotherhood in the Game Universe document, in the chapter "{% symbol "Oberon Mediation" for "oberon_introduction_title" %}". It summarises the many services and pieces of advice these mediators can offer you during the evening.*

Flash mob of Akarith fanatics
  ONLY if you have control of a small troop (10-12 people) of Akarith zealots, undercover in the big cities of Sabarim.
  You know all their contact details, if you have a means at your disposal to contact them.
  Thanks to the wonderful Sabarith transport system, they can be gathered **in under 60 min** on the target of your choice to ransack/loot it, or carry out any other large-scale action (blocking a road, setting fire to a forest, dancing naked in a public square...). But do not expect to see them again after that.
