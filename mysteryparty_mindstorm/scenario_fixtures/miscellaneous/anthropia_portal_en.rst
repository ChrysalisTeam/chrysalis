The Anthropia Portal and the Telecoms of Pangea
==================================================

Introducing Anthropia
++++++++++++++++++++++++

This web portal, born **very recently** from a collaboration between several academies of Pangea, is a technological revolution. It is currently used mainly by scientists, state officials, and their relatives. It can be accessed both from fixed stations and from mobile terminals.

Many extensions exist for this web portal: chat room, encyclopedia, web radio...

The most widely used remains asset management; indeed, it lets you view and use:

- kashes (the common national currency, also accepted abroad)
- Sabarith Pounds (a highly regulated currency, used for auctions, at parity with kashes)
- items (stored remotely in a vault of the Bank of Salima)
- gems (usable individually, sometimes serving as currency)

Note that a **"Digital Friendship"** system, in place on the portal, allows you to share your data, in read-only mode, with your relatives. Remember to check: perhaps some digital friendships are already in place on your own account.


Means of Communication
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

Anthropia offers fast and powerful **text messaging**, which is on the way to supplanting other means of communication.

However, to contact the very many people who do not yet have an account on this portal, it remains essential to call the good old **Telephone Exchange**, and give it the name of the person you want to talk to, wherever they may be on Pangea.
