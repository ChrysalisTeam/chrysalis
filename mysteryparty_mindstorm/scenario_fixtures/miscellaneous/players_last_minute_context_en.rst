Approaching the manor...
################################

.. raw:: pdf

   Spacer 0 5


On the stroke of 18:00, on this day of Loyd Georges' grand evening, the cab comes to pick you up outside your home. Not a motorised cab; a proper old-fashioned cab, with horses and decorations, just the thing to charm Loyd Georges, with his nostalgia for a bygone era.

The manor you know well emerges from the mist, under pouring rain. An imposing, majestic edifice, certainly, but one that bears the scars of recent events: bars on the windows, high-voltage fences around the courtyard, and a veritable armed checkpoint at the entrance.

Guests are queuing there, half hidden by their umbrellas. When your turn comes, the security guards check your magnetised invitation card, then usher you through a metal airlock. A high-tech detector, like at the entrance of ministries. Enough to make sure the guests are carrying neither explosives, nor blades, nor firearms.

Crossing the grounds, you catch sight, in the middle of the lawn, of a black silhouette looking you up and down. The famous Inspector Shark, dispatched by the State to watch over Loyd Georges' interests. He clearly intends to treat you to his discreet but unsettling presence.

No matter, then; he can only be the witness of manoeuvres whose subtlety is beyond him.
Or at least, that is what you hope. For you have been told over and over again: the geopolitical situation is extremely tense, so it is out of the question for a foreign spy to be unmasked in this country; that would not be forgiven.

But the most worrying thing, in all this business, is the large circular antenna that a police officer is installing at the entrance of the manor. It looks very much like an electromagnetic jammer, capable of preventing any mobile phone from communicating with the outside world. Strange idea.

The manor door opens heavily just as you are about to knock. Let the party begin.
