
The hairdresser-masseur of Salima
====================================

For decades you have been Loyd Georges' hairdresser-masseur, which has allowed you to build up a certain intimacy with him.

You have been summoned to the manor by one of the guests, who acquired a "gift voucher for a scalp massage" issued by your shop.

You then perform the said scalp massage, while chatting about everything and nothing, and answering your client's questions.

Miscellaneous interactions:

- You lament what has happened to poor Sir Loyd Georges; oh dear, in this day and age nobody is safe from anything any more!
- Loyd Georges had confided to you that he had unearthed extraordinary artefacts - far better than those put up for auction - and that he had entrusted them to "a trusted person", "for study".
- The bars of Sabarim, and especially of the city of Salima, are becoming more and more disreputable, with shady types with a Midolian accent going there in gangs on certain evenings.

- if one of the protagonists entrusts you with **an item to take out of the manor, charge 50 kashes for it**.
