Sheet of Robb Barrow
===============================

Your story
++++++++++++++++

Senior servant of {% symbol "Count Blispin" for "lg_close_friend_lord_name" %} (a friend of Loyd Georges), you are a man of many skills (cook, barman, gardener...), with an ever-sharp mind, but whose senses are in steep decline with age: short-sightedness, deafness...

You have been seconded, for the past 7 days, to the service of Loyd Georges, in particular to help with the special evening. It seems indeed that, with the recent threats from fanatics coming from the land of Akaris, most of Sir Loyd Georges' servants have resigned.

On the day before Loyd Georges' reception, you see various guests come and go, although you are mostly busy in the kitchen.

Loyd Georges sends for you just before noon, to chill the bottle of aperitif brought by a guest. You serve this aperitif before Loyd Georges' meal, at around {% symbol "13:15" for "noon_meal_time_lg_opal" %}. For this lunch, you prepared a "cod gratin with chanterelles".

You feed the old dog Bedou and eat the leftovers, with Alphonse, at around {% symbol "14:00" for "noon_meal_time_servants" %}; then you go out to do a bit of gardening.

At around {% symbol "16:00" for "kitchen_cook_inspector_meeting_time" %} you get back to the stove, to cook a "veal blanquette with bay leaves"; indeed, a few days ago, you found on your worktop a note from Loyd Georges, requesting this dish for tonight. The inspector drops by to chat with you a little while you are busy; he talks about the recent attempts to burgle the manor, but you have no information to give him on that subject.

The inspector leaves before the evening meal. You give Bedou some blanquette, as a preview, shortly before {% symbol "19:00" for "evening_dinner_time_dog_bedou" %}; then you go off to eat in town with some relatives, before Loyd Georges dines.

The next day, the day of the special evening, you learn that Loyd Georges has been hospitalised, but the inspector does not tell you much, except to prepare the evening "as if nothing had happened".

You do not know the Tabellion of Salima, nor the various guests (bidders or not) present.

If you are asked for the recipe of one of the 2 dishes of the previous day, you go and fetch it (from the Game Master). If you are asked to go and fetch all the manor's recipe books, same thing.

**If someone proves to you that you mistakenly put oleander in a dish, instead of bay leaf, you are appalled, desperate, and ask to be imprisoned.**

If you are asked to make a (fake) cocktail, with various ingredients brought by a player, go and see the Game Master so that they can give you the result of this mixture.

All these game actions, you do for free (no need to ask for "kash" notes, the game's fictional money).
