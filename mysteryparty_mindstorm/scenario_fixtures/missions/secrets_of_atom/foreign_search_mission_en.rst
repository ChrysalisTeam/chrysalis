MISSION - What did Voluto want?
====================================

{% include "missions/secrets_of_atom/generic_mission_briefing_en.rst" %}

Indeed, your country was recently contacted, under the seal of secrecy, by a certain **Professor Voluto, of the atomic physics department of the Alifir Academy**.

He had "{% symbol "revolutionary discoveries in the military field" for "physicist_discovery_claims" %}" to offer, in exchange for money and glory.

You were to meet him on the sidelines of your stay in Sabarim, but as if by the greatest of coincidences, Professor Voluto died shortly before that, "{% symbol "at home, of a cerebral haemorrhage following a fall" for "physicist_death_circumstances" %}" according to the newspapers.

Hard to know what it was all about, or whether it had any connection with the Loyd Georges affair.

**But the orders are clear: investigate this affair as quickly as possible, and seize the possible findings of the late professor, before the competition does.**
