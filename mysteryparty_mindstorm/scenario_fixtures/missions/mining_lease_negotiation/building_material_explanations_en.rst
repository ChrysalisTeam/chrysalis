*In search of Building Materials*


To face the dark times ahead, no precaution is too great.

It seems that Akarith industrialists have found a clever chemical process to "coagulate" certain mixtures of sand and bitumen, which occur naturally. They thereby make excellent building materials out of them, light, sturdy and insulating, making it possible to deploy buildings and bridges in record time.

Our Duke has been discreetly but firmly *invited* by the Judicators to supply them with tar sand resources; for non-military purposes, they say; but no matter, it is not as if we had a choice.

A Sabarim public figure is now offering for sale a large deposit of such tar sands, which contains in sufficient quantity the "rare earths" needed for coagulation (a reaction that binds together the grains of sand and the drops of bitumen).
With modern techniques, we can extract within a few months all the accessible resources in the deposit, that is to say the equivalent of **20 megakashes** of whole tar sands.
