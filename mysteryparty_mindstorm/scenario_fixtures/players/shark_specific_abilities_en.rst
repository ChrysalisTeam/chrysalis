Lightning Investigation
  In exchange for an amnesty, an old mafia godfather from the Salimian underworld would be ready to move heaven and earth to bring you fresh (and potentially compromising) information on **ONE person** of your choice, whom you would point out to him. Hard to predict what might come out of it, but among his dozens of lieutenants, it would be surprising if none had ever had dealings, at one time or another, with Loyd Georges' guests.

Fake Evidence
  You know a former forger who, to make amends for certain recent lapses, is ready to make you **ONE forgery** (object, official paper, photograph...) of your choice, according to the instructions you give him; this could be very useful for creating a "missing exhibit", if you are convinced of a guest's guilt but cannot prove it. Beware, however, as such fake evidence could backfire on you, should your intuitions prove wrong.
