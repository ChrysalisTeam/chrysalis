The offences you have identified
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++


Numerous incidents have punctuated these last few weeks, and even though you strongly suspect they are no coincidence, you have mostly been unable to trace their perpetrators:

- There was thus the burglary of a private warehouse in Alifir, rented by Loyd Georges; the artefacts it contained, however, had already been transferred to the Alifir Academy. {% fact 'malachite&spinel_rob_alifir_warehouse' as author %}
- Then two successive attempts to burgle Loyd Georges' manor, both foiled by the security systems. {% fact 'spinel_robs_lg_manor_fail' %} {% fact 'peridot_robs_lg_manor_fail' %}
- The fact that several of the gentleman's close acquaintances, for instance the scholars of Alifir, grew worried about abnormal phenomena on their telephone line, or their personal online account.
- Prof. Loakim of the Alifir Academy, a friend of Loyd Georges, was nearly kidnapped in broad daylight.

Your intuition tells you that, during this coming evening, the culprits of these various crimes will not be far away.

Discreetly inspecting a digital terminal in the manor, you saw that it had recorded, in its web browsing memory, two new logins: "opal" and "emerald".  {% fact 'opal_accesses_emerald_agent_web_account' %}
Logins that appeared shortly after {{character_properties.opal.official_name}} and his family came to settle temporarily in Loyd Georges' manor, 2 weeks ago.  {% fact "opal_lydia_temporarily_moved_to_lg_manor_2_weeks_ago" as author %}

By analysing the manor's telephone network, you furthermore saw abnormal frames go by, seeking to redirect information flows; coincidentally, they came from the hotel room occupied by the gentleman "{{character_properties.topaz.official_name}}". That is already quite compromising, for this poor spy [write these frames, freestyle]. {% fact 'topaz_wiretaps_lg_and_friends' %}

Miscellaneous information
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

On the morning of the day of the interviews, before your arrival at the manor, a man came to bring a thin parcel to Loyd Georges (you heard Alphonse mention it to Rydji). {% fact "alphonse_blunder_revealed_panorbium_fortuna_arrival" %}
But contrary to procedures, the gentleman did not wait for you to inspect it for safety, and hid it after temporarily disabling the security cameras; you argued with him about it during the day. The next day, you learned, through Akarith informants, that there had been unusual literary discoveries - immediately hushed up - at one of the archaeological digs of Prof. Loakim, Loyd Georges' friend, in Keroskia. You think you have missed something important, but your efforts to track down this parcel have come to nothing. Perhaps Loyd Georges' servants will talk more, now that their master is out of the picture.
{% fact 'misc_panorbium_fortuna_brought_to_manor_in_bag' %}

During the midday meal with LG and you, on the day of the interviews, {{character_properties.opal.official_name}} revealed that an anti-desecrator Akarith sect was probably behind the poisoning of his antihistamine medication, which nearly killed him through muscular intoxication. He revealed that many former adventuring companions, of Loyd Georges and of himself, had died in mysterious circumstances. You took notes on what he was saying, and wrote down the names of the "survivors". This could be of interest to the **"Purifiers"**; this brotherhood, under the control of your Akarith religious leaders, hunts down the impious, and may well be behind all this. {% fact 'opal_and_others_were_targeted_by_sect' %}

You did notice the **safe** in Loyd Georges' bedroom, and have always dreamed of being able to rummage through its contents. But all your attempts to access it have been in vain. Now that Loyd Georges is out of the picture, you are pressuring the servants to find the means to open it, "for the needs of the investigation"; but for now they have come up empty-handed. {% fact 'lg_has_vault_in_manor' %}

Judicator Praeves of Akaris has sent you, in your P.O. box, an **antidote** to the poisons contained in lily of the valley; in accordance with his instructions, you have sewn it into the lining of your jacket. And you have also prepared a **lily of the valley extract**, which you left in the said P.O. box. This could come in handy, for instance if ever the poisoning of Loyd Georges did not work. But beware, as some guests are probably well versed in detecting poisons... {% fact 'shark_has_lily_of_the_valley_poison_and_antidote' as author %}

This Judicator also warned you that a closed-door council was to be held between the Akarith religious leaders, and that the doctrine of "transfiliation", which is still controversial, would be discussed there. It is not only a question of your own status as a "transfiliate in the making" (even though, having *an Akarith great-grandmother*, you are not 100% a "bathar" as they call them); what is at stake is the future of the entire Dorian people, who could peacefully attach themselves to Akaris and its religion, if theology validated this possibility of transfiliating them. Archdeacon Baazel explained that he would take advantage of the council's confidentiality to cite you there as an example of "the grace of the Yods reaching as far as Sabarim"; and that, moreover, several experts would be called upon by the council as part of these crucial debates.  {% fact 'misc_akarith_council_begins' %}
