===================================


You are Cynthia Loakim, daughter of the renowned archaeologist **Emilos Loakim**, and a rising figure in international journalism.

You spent your childhood in **Salima**. The exuberant Sir Loyd Georges, who is currently making the headlines, is like a close relative to you. You often went to see him with your family, as a child, and your taste for travel is largely due to the colourful accounts he gave of his not very fruitful expeditions.

When respiratory problems took your mother away, as you were about to turn 15, you moved to be closer to the **Alifir Academy**, where your father became head of the archaeology department.

After some law studies, it was international journalism that attracted you. And for the past 5 years, you have been travelling the world to discover other cultures, and glean information on the political luminaries of Pangea, for the renowned {% symbol "Sabarim Gazette" for "cynthia_employer_name" %}.

Your travels have led you to meet an incredible number of characters, sometimes dreadfully cynical, sometimes delightfully brave. But as fate would have it, it was a discreet and sedentary laboratory assistant from the academy, **Waden Shaw**, who won your heart. In the year since you met him, you have therefore been arranging to come home more regularly.



The Sacred Fire of Journalism
=======================================


The sacred fire of investigative journalism: you had it, in your early days. Unmasking the corrupt, the predators, the conspirators was not only an exciting activity, it was also an immense service rendered to the world. And your first investigations put more than one notable in a tight spot, which did not earn you only friends.
But several cases have shaken what you thought was your strength of character.

Sometimes for legitimate reasons. While doing a report on the **Dorian Duke**, 1 year ago, you came across videographs showing him **{% symbol "urinating on a crossentar, the sacred Yodic symbol" for "dorian_duke_profanation_act" %}**, during an evening of drunken revelry; enough to qualify the filial piety he has displayed, since his accession to power, towards the dominant religion in Midolia. Such a scene would be capable of triggering bloody riots in Doria, or even an attack from the Akarith neighbours. So you preferred to hide this aspect of the Duke's personality, in your final report.

Your father Emilos Loakim, in his frantic search for Akarith antiquities stolen by looters and traffickers, also asked for your help in reconstructing the history of a legendary **three-eyed Keroskian statuette**, which passed from hand to hand before he recovered it on the Sabarith black market. From DNA analyses to various interrogations, you put your finger on the original thief: **{{character_properties.opal.full_official_title}}**; a man who, as if by coincidence, had accompanied Loyd Georges during one of his most disastrous archaeological expeditions in Keroskia; but a man who has been settled for more than a decade already, in Lordania, with wife and child.
You set aside this last link in the chain, so as not to hurt Loyd Georges (who has always spoken of him as an old companion in adventure), and so as not to break up a family, given that this offence will be time-barred in 8 months.

Finally, recently, you stumbled upon a huge Zombifying Drug trafficking scandal, involving Lordanian as well as Sabarith notables, with the connivance of the **three consuls currently in office in Sabarim**. For the first time in your life, you buried the story not out of ethical concern, but out of fear; fear for yourself and for those close to you. High-ranking figures of the State clearly threatened you with the worst if this information got out, and that is somewhat the price of love: when one sees a life as a couple on the horizon, one becomes less of a daredevil.

All this gives you pause; are you going to end up like your colleagues, only going after the weakest, for fear of angering the powerful?
In any case, you keep the evidence of these sensitive cases at your home, **{% symbol "studio flat number 827 in the Wetfeet Peninsulas (a locality near Alifir)" for "cynthia_sabarim_home_address" %}**, in a **{% symbol "lead casket under a floorboard" for "cynthia_proofs_hiding_place" %}**.
It is not very safe, but far more discreet than a safe-deposit box at the Bank of Salima.

Your career as an international reporter is also hitting a glass ceiling: travel visas. They are taking longer and longer to obtain, and are increasingly hit-and-miss, and several red-hot news stories have slipped through your fingers because of this. One solution exists: **planetary visas**. Each country may grant a few of them per year, and it is most often diplomats and arms dealers who benefit from them. But your employer, the {% symbol "Sabarim Gazette" for "cynthia_employer_name" %}, has exceptionally obtained a planetary visa for the staff member of its choice. It remains for you to prove yourself more worthy of it than your colleagues, by bringing back what is needed to your dear editor-in-chief: "scoops, big ones, juicy ones, I expect nothing less from you my dear little Cynthia; with this special auction evening, you will be plunged into the heart of the Affair of the moment, may it be fruitful!". But you fully intend to keep a certain caution.
