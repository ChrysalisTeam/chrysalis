===================================

Officially, you were born in the land of Sabarim, and your name is **{{character_properties.lydia.official_name}}**. But you have had many nicknames, during your adventurous life. From one continent to another, you have been in turn a medical student, a diamond prospector, a beaver-pelt trader, an apprentice archaeologist, and even a tomb raider when your finances required it.

You have always had this taste for adventure, and strangely, when you found yourself pregnant, for you it was just another twist of fate, a new quest, opening its arms to you. On your little Khaal's second birthday, you decided, trembling, to introduce him to his father. {% fact 'lydia_presented_2_years_old_khaal_to_opal' as author %} Against all expectations, the lawless, faithless globetrotter that **{{character_properties.opal.official_name}}** was did not send you packing. After a week of dithering, he even decided to join this adventure. From long-standing accomplices, you thus became a family, treating yourselves along the way to a discreet but unforgettable wedding. That was ten years ago.

In search of respectability, you settled in **Lordania** with your husband, and put an end to all illicit activity. {{character_properties.opal.official_name}} quickly developed a network of taverns in Lordania, thanks to his respectable financial capital. {% fact 'opal_develops_lordanian_taverns' %}

From then on, your life looked like a perfect fairy tale: a spirited husband who was also your partner in crime, a child growing in strength and intelligence every day, a flourishing financial empire, and a name commanding the respect of all polite society.


However, afterwards, the horizon darkened.

For **1 year** now, your spouse has been growing dangerously weaker, and the doctors are proving powerless in the face of his growing muscular pains. They even dare to mention old age, when he is not yet fifty! {% fact 'opal_has_become_ill_for_1_year' %}

Moreover, for 2 years, your husband's taverns have been in great difficulty, faced with unfair competition from illegal gambling dens. He has taken on heavy debts in his own name, to turn them into more prestigious establishments. But a lawyer friend recently informed you that the company *LordaBild*, commissioned for this work, was going bankrupt, against a backdrop of massive tax fraud.

Your husband is therefore going to end up with half of his establishments in the middle of renovation works, the other half operating at a loss, monstrous debts towards the banks... you have not yet dared to break the news to him, for fear of further worsening his state of health. But by poking your nose into his accounts, you were able to calculate the scale of the problem: you need to find **{% symbol "18 megakashes" for "opal_lydia_financial_needs" %}** by next month, otherwise the family is headed for certain ruin. {% fact 'opal_lydia_family_faces_bankrupt' %}


Taking flight in politics
==========================

All these worries had pushed you to find new breathing space, new quests for your life. 6 months ago, you therefore went into politics, with a view to becoming the first female consul of the **Consulate of Alifir**; you indeed hold dual Lordanian/Sabarith nationality, and the stagnation of political life in your native country, with its trio of ageing and corrupt consuls, inspired these new ambitions in you. Your family therefore immediately settled into a charming villa near the Alifir Academy; also with a view to benefiting from better care for your husband.{% fact 'opal_lydia_moved_to_alifir_villa_6_months_ago' as author %} {% fact 'lydia_runs_for_alifir_consul_election' as author %}
