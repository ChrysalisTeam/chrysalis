    State of play
    ===============

    It is not always easy to be a defector, regarded as a traitor to one's homeland, and a new member of a powerful but still very mysterious brotherhood. However, having spirited away the three mythical orbs, right under the Akariths' noses, is deliciously gratifying. Having a foot in the door with the famous Loyd Georges is also a pleasant thing. And having a fine day of rest ahead of oneself, in this temperate climate, is the dream of every honest man. After all, the Paladium seems to have the matter well in hand; the Judicators and their delirious speeches had better watch out.


    Additional information - Spinel
    ==========================================
    During a conference with the Paladium, you were able to learn a few things about their practices. For instance, they mark with a "snake in a shield" symbol people "on borrowed time", who have violated ethical rules and would deserve to be dragged before the courts if circumstances allowed. They also know how to mark entire cities with symbols (e.g. a swan for "civilian zone, bombing and fighting strictly forbidden"), to serve as a warning to anyone of ill will.
